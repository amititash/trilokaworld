### "The Hidden Town of Ghaziabad"

## Loni Tourism

Loni, a surprisingly less known town in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, is a place of religious significance. The historical site is connected to many Hindu mythological stories. There are also many significant temples around the city.

The Loni Fort being another attraction in the city is believed to have been the place where Lord Ram's brother, Shathruguna killed the evil demon Lavansura. The fort which is ruins today is believed to have been because of the invasion of Mohammed Shah in the 18th century. There are also three other important places of significance - Kharanji Bagh, Uldipur and Ranap which are believed to be 500 years old.

Apart from the spiritual sites, there is also the Lal Bagh Ashram and IdiraPuri Gurudwara. Modernising the town, there are classic malls and theatres. Marking the popularity of the town is the world's largest LPG pipeline which is 1.270 kilometers long and is a definite place of visit. Also, on nature's side, there is the April Park which is filled with greenery. Tronica City is one of the city's fast developing places which is a must-visit.

## More on Loni Travel

### Places to Visit

The remains of the three famous orchards Uldipur, Ranap and Kharanji Bagh are some prominent places to visit. Katu Shyam Baba Mandir, Loni Hanuman Mandir and Shiv Mandir are some places of holy importance in the city. Also, April Park in Tronica City is another exciting place to visit to catch a beautiful sight of nature.

**Shiv Temple** \- At a distance of 13 kilometres from central Loni is the Shiv Temple. A massive flight of stairs reaches the sanctum. The view from the peak is extraordinary and gives a brief picture of the neighbourhood and town. The white tomb of the temple provides the shrine with a beautiful appearance.

**Movie Magic Cinema** \- The theatre is located in the heart of the city and is quite popular among the locals. With sufficient parking space and excellent facilities, the theatre is a rare find in a place like Loni. The tickets and eateries are decently priced and are a must-visit for some fun within the city.

### Loni Local Culture

**Ethnicity**

The fast-developing city has people from multicultural backgrounds. The people in the town are very respectful and helpful.

**Local Traditions**

A city known for its religious significance, holy activities and culture are part of a daily curriculum in every household. Children are taught art, music, dances and other cultural activities from a young age.

**Clothing**

The general attire worn by men is dhoti or kurta-pyjama and the ones worn by women are sarees or salwar-kameez. Men often sport the Topi as a headgear.

**Language of Loni**

The language spoken by the locals is Hindi. However, schools and colleges follow Engish as their medium. Festivals are celebrated with a lot of enthusiasm and by the tradition.

**Loni Celebrations**

Nag Panchami, Janmashtami, Mahavir Jayanthi, Christmas Buddha Jayanthi and Moharam are some famous festivities.

### Food and Restaurants in Loni

Loni is very much famous for North Indian foods which are widely available in different restaurants within the city. Rajma Chawal, Butter Chicken, Dal Makhani, Cholle Bhature and Dahi Bhalla are some super hits in the city. Bhola Sweets and Dhanuram Sweets are some popular sweet shops in the town which serve a variety of dishes like Gulab Jamun, Jalebi and the famous Doda Burfi. There are also other cuisines around the city which serve pizzas, burgers, Chinese and Burmese foods. The most lip-smacking food is the North-Indian chaat which are available in local fast-food shops around the town. Punjabi Zaika, Taj Chicken Point, Aggarwal Fast Food are some famous restaurants in the city.

### History of Loni

Most popularly known for its significance in mythology and history, it is a place where great rulers and essential figures from Hindu epics have spent their time. The name of the city derived from the historic legend King Lonnkaran who ruled this area and named his territory after his name. During the 12th century, Prithviraj Chauhan ruled the town. Even to this date, certain places of his fortification are significant places of visit in Loni. During the Mughal era, Zinat Mahal, the wife of Bahadur Shah Zafar built the three famous orchards. They are believed to be over 500 years old and are a major historical attraction in the city.

### Traveller Tips

- There are many banks located around the city, and the ATMs are open 24 hours.
- However, it is recommended that a small amount of cash is kept for safety because local buses and autos don't accept cards and it may get tedious during the commute.

## How to Reach Loni

### How to Reach Overview

Loni is well connected to most places surrounding the city. Noida and Delhi are most connected to the city to which many people commute for work.

### How to reach Loni by flight

Indira Gandhi International Airpot at Palam which is located at a distance of 35 kilometres from Loni is the closest airport. After the inauguration of Terminal 3 in the airport, it serves around 46 million travellers a year and has become South Asia's biggest aviation hub.

### How to reach Loni by road

The road network in Loni connects to significant parts of Delhi. One of the most commonly used means of travel is shared autos, which shuttles between Pawi, Loni and IndiraPuri.

### How to reach Loni by train

Railways and metro are the most commonly used means of commute by passengers. Noil Railway Station, Gothra Halt, Behtha Hazipur Halt and Nursrathabad KharKhar are some major depots and connects the city to major stations in Delhi. It serves about 12,000 commuters per day. A container depot at the station is the new addition. Pink Line of Delhi Metro now connects with Loni. Since the metro covers most parts of Loni, it has been an excellent replacement for shared autos which were not a very comfortable mode of transport.

### How to reach Loni by bus

There are also DTC and UPSRTC buses which people use for commuting within the city. Bus terminals are set up and significant points in the city. There are also buses which travel up to UP border and to Kashmir Gate.

### Local transport in Loni

Various modes of transportations are available to commute in Loni. Railways connect all significant points in the town, but it can get crowded during peak hours. Metro is an alternate option in case of travelling during such timings. There are also other options like buses, taxis and autos, all of which are available at ease and affordable fares. Vehicles can also be hired, most preferably self-drive zoom cars. Fares depend upon the time and distance travelled, and the price are comparatively lower than other car rental services within the city.

# Things To Do in Loni

Loni is a small town located in the state of Uttar Pradesh in northern India. Although it is not as well-known as some of the other popular tourist destinations in India, Loni has a lot to offer to visitors. Here are some of the Things To Do in Loni, Uttar Pradesh:

Visit the Siddheshwar Mahadev Temple: This ancient temple is dedicated to Lord Shiva and is believed to have been built around 400 years ago. The temple is known for its beautiful architecture and intricate carvings.

Explore the Balaji Temple: The Balaji Temple is another popular temple in Loni that is dedicated to Lord Hanuman. The temple is famous for its beautiful architecture and serene atmosphere.

Visit the Jama Masjid: The Jama Masjid is one of the most famous mosques in Loni and is known for its beautiful architecture and intricate carvings.

Take a stroll in the Loni Bird Sanctuary: The Loni Bird Sanctuary is a popular destination for birdwatchers and nature lovers. The sanctuary is home to a variety of bird species, including migratory birds that come here during the winter months.

Explore the ruins of the Loni Fort: The Loni Fort is an ancient fort that dates back to the 15th century. Although most of the fort is in ruins, visitors can still see the remains of the walls and gateways.

Visit the Rajaji National Park: The Rajaji National Park is a popular destination for wildlife enthusiasts. The park is home to a variety of animal species, including tigers, leopards, elephants, and deer.

Take a dip in the Ganges River: The Ganges River is considered to be one of the most sacred rivers in India. Visitors can take a dip in the river and offer prayers at the many ghats (riverbanks) along its banks.

Visit the Kali Temple: The Kali Temple is another popular temple in Loni that is dedicated to Goddess Kali. The temple is known for its beautiful architecture and serene atmosphere.

Explore the nearby town of Meerut: Meerut is a nearby town that is known for its rich history and cultural heritage. Visitors can explore the many historical monuments and temples in the town.

Taste the local cuisine: Loni is known for its delicious street food, including chaat, samosas, and jalebis. Visitors can also try the local delicacies like kebabs, biryani, and kachori.

More Things To Do in Loni, Uttar Pradesh

In addition to the attractions mentioned earlier, there are several more Things To Do in Loni, Uttar Pradesh. Here are some more activities that you can consider:

Take a walk in the Loni Forest Reserve: The Loni Forest Reserve is a beautiful natural area located near the town. Visitors can take a walk in the forest and enjoy the scenic beauty of the area.

Explore the nearby town of Ghaziabad: Ghaziabad is located about 25 km from Loni and is a bustling city with many shopping malls, restaurants, and entertainment options. Visitors can explore the many markets and shopping centers in the city and enjoy a day out.

Best Time To Visit Loni, Uttar Pradesh

The best time to visit Loni, Uttar Pradesh is during the winter season, which lasts from November to February. During this time, the weather is pleasant and comfortable, with temperatures ranging between 5°C and 20°C. The days are sunny and the nights are cool, making it an ideal time to explore the town and its surroundings.

The winter season is also the peak tourist season in Loni, and visitors can expect to see many cultural festivals and events taking place during this time. The Loni Mahotsav is a popular festival that is celebrated in December, and it offers visitors a chance to experience the local culture and cuisine.

Another reason why the winter season is the best time to visit Loni is because of the clear skies and good visibility. This makes it an ideal time to explore the historical sites and monuments in the nearby towns of Agra and Delhi, which are located within a few hours' drive from Loni.

During the summer season, which lasts from March to June, the weather in Loni can be hot and humid, with temperatures ranging between 25°C and 45°C. The monsoon season, which lasts from July to September, brings heavy rainfall and can make it difficult to explore the town and its surroundings.

The post-monsoon season, which lasts from October to November, can also be a good time to visit Loni. The weather during this time is pleasant, and the town is less crowded than during the winter season. Visitors can enjoy the scenic beauty of the surrounding areas and explore the many attractions without the crowds.
