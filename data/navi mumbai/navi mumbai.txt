# Places to Visit in Navi Mumbai

Navi Mumbai is a planned city that was constructed as a replica of Mumbai. It is situated in the eastern part of Thane and was built to alleviate the pressure that Mumbai's ever-increasing population places on the city's infrastructure. Many people go back and forth between Navi Mumbai and Mumbai using the local trains, buses, and ferry services. Visit these Navi Mumbai attractions to get a feel for the incredible beauty and thoughtful planning that went into this area. Not to mention, you can also explore various temples, malls and restaurants here.

[**Nerul Balaji Temple**] 

##### Sacred Hindu temple

The Venkateswara Temple and Balaji Temple at Tirupati served as models for the construction of the Nerul Balaji Temple. In fact, the Shilpa Shastra was followed in its construction. This shrine is perched atop a tiny hill on the western side of the Nerul Railway Station.

[**ISKCON**] 

##### Revered temple dedicated to Lord Krishna

The internationally renowned ISKCON (International Society for Krishna Consciousness) Temple is settled in the Navi Mumbai neighbourhood of Khargar. Not only is this building sacred, but it also serves other purposes. It aspires to provide a comprehensive cultural experience that will blow the visitors’ minds. The white ISKCON Kharghar complex stretches over eight acres of lush vegetation.

[**Pandavkada Falls**] 

##### Gurgling waterfall amid nature

Pandavkada Falls is located about 9 km away from Navi Mumbai and drops from a height of 107 metres. This waterfall, located in Mumbai's Western Ghats, is a plunge waterfall, meaning that it rushes through the mountains and then rapidly crashes down over a rocky area below. It takes roughly three hours to go from Mumbai to the waterfall, but just about an hour and a half from Navi Mumbai when traffic is light.

[**Belapur Fort**] 

##### Ancient fort with intricate architecture

The historic Belapur Fort is located in the Belapur Township in Navi Mumbai. The Siddhis of Janjira built this fort in the 16th century. The fort was transferred to the Portuguese and the British who were already stationed there.

[**Karnala Bird Sanctuary**] 

##### Home to a variety of birds and animals

Karnala Bird Sanctuary found not far from the Mumbai–Pune Expressway, is a 500-square-kilometre area packed with exotic plant and animal life. There are over 150 distinct bird species that call the Karnala Bird Sanctuary home.

[**Central Park**] 

##### Lush green park for picnic

Central Park, which opened in Navi Mumbai in 2010, has been greatly appreciated by residents of all ages for its proximity to nature and its ability to provide a variety of recreational opportunities. The park's many gathering spots make it ideal for both quiet evening strolls and large group outings. One such facility that continues to attract visitors to Central Park is the amphitheatre, which has hosted countless concerts and other social events throughout the years.

[**Utsav Chowk**] 

##### Bustling spot with Roman and Greek architecture

Utsav Chowk, a popular tourist destination in Kharghar, Navi Mumbai, is a great example of a fusion of Roman and Greek architectural styles. The chowk gets lit up at night, drawing the attention of visitors. In 72 AD, Flavian Emperor, Vespasian, conceived the massive building that exists in Utsav Chowk. This amazing structure, finished in the year 80 AD, retains all of its original beauty.

##### Stunning tourist attraction

The hills of Kharghar are always a pleasant sight to see, and they provide the ideal backdrop for a daily hike in the great outdoors. The Kharghar Hills are one of the city's top attractions, and they're only three kilometres from the Mumbai-Pune motorway.

##### Sprawling park for relaxation

Navi Mumbai is home to a truly one-of-a-kind park called Wonders Park which you may not have seen anywhere else in this world. Beautifully constructed, this park has exact reproductions of the world's Seven Wonders. The maintenance staff takes great care to ensure that these reproductions look and feel perfect at all times.

[**1\. Bawkhaleshwar Temple**] 

6 km from city center

Temple

The Bawkhaleshwar Temple that is located in the Navi Mumbai area of Maharashtra. It was managed by the Bawkhaleshwar Temple Trust since the year of 2010. Lord Ganesha, Lord Shiva, and Devi were the primary deities of this temple. The Bawkhaleshwar Temple was most well known for its majestic marble architecture, and the weekly aarti held for Lord Ganesha every Tuesday. The high court of Maharashtra (Read More) and later the Supreme Court had deemed this establishment illegal after a long legal battle spanning almost eight years, following which the temple was demolished in 2018 by the MIDC (Maharashtra Industrial Development Cooperation).

[**2\. Nerul Balaji Temple**] 

2 km from city center

Temple

The Nerul Balaji Temple is an almost-exact replica of the Tirupati Balaji Temple, and also replicates certain aspects of the Venkateswara temple at Tirupati. It has been constructed according to the Shilpa Shastra. This temple is located on a small hill close to the western side of the Nerul railway station. The primary deity worshipped in this temple is Shri Balaji, which is a name ascribed to a (Read More)form of Lord Hanuman is different parts of our country. The temple premise houses many other temples as well.

[**3\. Pandavkada Falls**] 

Waterfall

Pandavkada Falls is a 107 metres tall waterfall near Mumbai. It is a plunge type waterfall that flows through the mountains of the Western Ghats of Mumbai and cascades down with immense speed over rock formations underneath. It takes about 3 hours to reach the waterfall from Mumbai and barely an hour and a half from Navi Mumbai during low traffic situations. So, this attraction is quite preferred (Read More)for a short trek or a day-long picnic, especially during the monsoon season when the mountains are covered with dense natural vegetation and provide for a beautiful escape from the routine. Reaching the waterfall during the rainy season is a thrill that calls amateur adventurers too.

[**4\. ISKCON Kharghar**] 

Temple

The world famous temple of ISKCON (International Society for Krishna Consciousness) has a branch in the Khargar area of Navi Mumbai district. This structure is more than just a temple. It aims to offer a complete cultural experience that is bound to go beyond your imagination. The Sri Sri Radha Madan Mohanji Temple - Glory of Maharashtra Project shines brightly in its devotion to Lord Krishna. The (Read More) sprawling white ISKCON Kharghar structure is stretched over 8 acres of evergreen land. It is surrounded by the Sahyadri Hills that add to the magnificent view. Lakhs of pilgrims visit the Khargar Temple throughout the year to attend essential festivals and ceremonies. This ISKCON temple in Kharghar has an exclusive cultural and Vedic educational centre on its premises. It is known to conduct scriptural classes based on Srimad Bhagavad Gita and other Vedic scriptures each morning. The classes have benefitted many by teaching them about the religion and the belief system that they follow so ardently.

Bird watching area

Park

Nestled near the Mumbai-Pune Expressway, Karnala Bird Sanctuary spans 500 square kilometers and is home to a diverse range of indigenous flora and fauna. This sanctuary is a haven for over 150 bird species, including both common and rare varieties unique to this region. Situated in the picturesque Western Ghats, it boasts rich biodiversity and historical significance with the presence of Karnala Fortress. Nature enthusiasts can enjoy hiking in the well-equipped trails while admiring the abundant wildlife and nearby river.

Karnala Bird Sanctuary and Karnala Fort offer a perfect mix of nature, adventure, and history. Located near Panvel, it’s a great getaway for trekking and birdwatching. The sanctuary is lush and peaceful, home to many bird species, especially during winter. The trek to Karnala Fort is moderately challenging, with scenic trails and dense greenery. The fort ruins at the top offer breathtaking views of the Sahyadris and the surrounding forests.
The best time to visit is early morning to enjoy the cool weather and spot birds. Monsoon makes the trek more beautiful but also slippery, so proper trekking shoes are a must. Carry enough water and snacks, as there are limited options inside. The entry fee is reasonable, and parking is available. Overall, a must-visit for nature lovers, trekkers, and history enthusiasts looking for a short escape from Mumbai or Pune.

Amenities: There are no hotels within SGNP. Some visitors have noted a lack of directional signage in certain areas, which can make navigation challenging.
