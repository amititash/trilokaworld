[Know about oil exploration] 





This small museum is located inside the ONGC headquarters. Since it is not widely known, there is no rush at any time of day. The museum is housed in the summer palace of the erstwhile Maharaja of Patiala and has an entry fee of Rs. 10.The museum spans two floors and features three main sections. The first section displays the history of oil, including samples of crude oil. The second section focuses on the process of oil exploration, and the third section depicts various methods of oil refinement and transportation. Additionally, there is an audio-visual room showcasing the oil industry in India and the journey of ONGC.Outside the main museum building, real-life working models of drill bits and other drilling equipment are exhibited. A small souvenir shop is also located at the entrance of the museum.

## FAQs on Dehradun

### Can you suggest an itinerary for Dehradun?

**Day 1 -** Once in Dehradun, take some rest and begin your trip with a stroll through the local market, especially the Paltan Bazaar, a fascinating market, with its share of amazing streets and alleys, just near to another famous site in Dehradun city, The Clock Tower on the Rajpur Road. You can buy curios, handicrafts, souvenirs among many other things here.

**Day 2 -** The next day, you should set off officially for the trip, starting with blessings from Lord Shiva at the holy Tapkeshwar Mahadev Mandir. # Dehradun

HighlightsGallery

Situated at an elevation of 435 meters above sea level, Dehradun is currently enjoying its stature as the capital of Uttarakhand state. It is one of the oldest cities in India, bejeweled like a glittering jewel in the foothills of the Himalayas. Nestled amidst the Doon Valley in the state of [Uttarakhand], it proudly boasts of a scenic backdrop of the Garhwal Himalayas.

As you would expect from a city located in the foothills of the Himalayas, Dehradun is famous for caves, waterfalls, and natural springs. The city is the gateway to the famous [hill station of Mussoorie] and the pilgrimage spots of [Haridwar] and [Rishikesh].

Famous for its weather, Dehradun's climate remains salubrious throughout the year. One of the attractions of Dehradun is Robber's Cave, which is a natural cave surrounded by hills. Dip your feet in the ice-cold water or walk through it - it is a soul-pleasing experience, making Robber's Cave one of the most sought-after picnics and excursion spots in the city.
