
 About Saharanpur 
 Saharanpur is a town and a district located in Uttar Pradesh and growing at a pace which not a lot of people expected it to do. There aren’t a lot of tourist destinations here that one can visit and if you intend on staying here for maybe a short while, you can find things to entertain yourself with. You have a lot of religious places to visit here; inclusive both of temples and dargahs, not only bringing in spirituality, but also togetherness amongst the Hindus as well as the Muslims, as these places of worship have been known for its collective harmony. It is an upcoming place, with a lot of constructions taking place. You have the Botanical Gardens also here, if you just want a stroll through a park and are nature lovers. There are markets that specifically are known for the products they provide. For example, if you’re too keen on jewelry shopping, you have an option to choose from Naya Bazar, Nehru market Saheed Ganj and so on. Saharanpuris worth a short trip.

# Tourism
Saharanpur is located in the state of Uttar Pradesh and its district headquarter is located around 180 KM from Delhi and around 75 KM from Haridwar. Saharanpur can be reached by rail, road and air.The nearest International Airport is situated in New Delhi, about 180 km away. The closest major airport to Saharanpur is Jolly Grant Airport in Dehradun which is nearly 90 km. It is connected to a numerous cities like Delhi, Mumbai, Amritsar and Lucknow. Trains to these cities pass through the city.
The City is a prominent tourist destination and is famous for its wood carving. Don’t miss the beautiful wood carvings market during the visit.You would find a number of temples in Saharanpur, Malls and a few cinema halls as well. It is cocooned between the rivers Ganges and Yamuna. The city is blessed with abundant lush flora and a sample of this can be seen at the Saharanpur Company Garden, which is one of the oldest existing gardens in India. Gandhi Park -it is the place where political rallies are held and also where many circus companies park their tents in. The city has a mix of both religious, recreational spots and tourist Places in and around Saharanpur. Many worth-seeing site are located in the city
GNG Mall at Dehli road has a good multiplex theatre and good shops This Mall is just before Paramount Tulip Residential Colony on Delhi road.
Shri Baba Lal Das-Tourism spot This place is frequently visited by many religious sects and followers, this has a sacred meaning to the people.
Ghugha Veer is also an attraction for visitors.
Nau Gaza Peer:A Religious spot Located at nearly 9 KM from the main city this place is free form the hustle and bustle of the city. Nau gaza peer at the outskirt of Saharanpur is a beautiful combination of a dargah and temple which are located next to each other. People of all community comes here. It is located on the Saharanpur -Dehradun/Haridwar Highway.The Naugaja Peer is much favoured by the devotees who come here to get their wishes fulfilled.
People flock to Saharanpur to see the temples including Shakumbhari Devi Temple and Bala Sundari Temple of Deoband (Shakti Peethas).
Apart from the temples, Saharanpur also has a botanical garden known as Company garden, established by the British East India Company in the city is also quite famous, which provides the much relief from the chaos of the city.
