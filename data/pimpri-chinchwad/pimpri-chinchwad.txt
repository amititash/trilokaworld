Pimpri-Chinchwad, a great industrial hub, also known as ‘twin city’ of Pune, is a small fastest growing city near Pune which is governed by Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation (PCMC). The Municipal Corporation is working hard to make Pimpri-Chinchwad a well-known tourist destination. Recently, PCMC has launched ‘Sarathi’ (a System of Assisting Residents And Tourists through Helpline Information) to boost tourism in this city. Pimpri-Chinchwad city is basically well connected to major cities of the country by road, rail and airport, nearest airport is in Pune with a distance of 12km from the city. It is easy to get regular buses and trains in the city. The major Spectacular tourist places in Pimpri-Chinchwad are: Ganesha Temple in Pimpri Chinchwad It is a 14th century temple situated near Pawana River in Chinchwad. It is said that a Ganesha devotee named ‘Morya Gosavi’ worshiped Ganesha here and waited for the god to appear in front of him. This saint built a Ganesha Temple here and is very famous in Chinchwad. The temple has a peaceful ambiance and attracts many devotees who seek blessings of Lord Ganesh. Vaishno Devi Temple in Pimpri Chinchwad Vaishno Devi temple is one of the famous temple which is located Near Pimpri-Chinchwad Link Road. It is a replica of the Vaishno Devi temple situated in Jammu &amp; Kashmir. This temple is in the form of hexagon and there is a small cave at the entry of the temple with ankle-depth water, it is kept to wash devotee’s foot before visiting the god. It is one of the must visit place in Pimpri. Durgadevi Hill Park in Pimpri Chinchwad A Durga devi Temple is situated in a hill named ‘Tekdi’ is located in Nigdi near Appugad and here there is a park with 1,60000 trees planted on an area of 75 Hectors with Lawn on 3 acres of land. This park also has floating fountain and Waterfall which adds more beauty to the park. This Park is well maintained and the Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation has declared the area of this hill park as plastic free zone. It is a wonderful place to visit. This park is open to public between 11am to 6pm on all days with an entrance fee of Rs.10 for adult and Rs.5 for children. Bhakti Shakti in Pimpri Chinchwad Bhakti (Spirutialism) Shakti (Power) is a wonderful place with collection of statues installed in it. It is situated in Old Mumbai Pune Highway in Pradhikaran near Nigdi in Pimpri Chinchwad. It is said that, Bhakti Shakti is installed in the memory of the meeting of Tukaram Maharaj and Chatrapathi Shivaji Maharaj. There are two main statues of Tukaram Maharaj and Chatrapathi Shivaji Maharaj each with 20 foot tall and the other statues are with 10 foot tall. These fascinating sculptures on the hill rock were in a beautiful landscaping and it has become the most remarkable landmark for Pimpri Chinchwad Municipal Corporation. It is open for visitors from 6.00 AM to 8.30 PM with no entry fee. Nisargakavi Bahinabai Chaudhary Zoo in Pimpri Chinchwad Nisargakavi Bahinabai Chaudhary zoo is a small zoo located in Sambhaji Nagar in Chinchwad, Near Vrindavan. It is previously famous for its snack park and birds sanctuary. This zoo was established by Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation and is open from the year 1990. This zoo is always a best attraction among children and has an increased number of visitors every year. It is open from 11am to 6pm on all days except Tuesday with an entrance fee off Rs.2 only. Pimpri Chinchwad Science Centre It is a joint Project of Ministry of Culture, Government of India and Pimpri Chinchwad Municipal Corporation. The main objective of this Science Centre is to collect, restore and preserve historical objects which represent the development of science and technology industry. This Center gives interactive science exhibitions and non-formal science education programs to the people.  There are many sections in this center which includes Energy, Fun Science, Climate Change, 3D Science Show facility, Science Park, Dino Park and Taramandal. Among these sections 3D  Science Show and Taramandal are very Popular at the Science Park. It is one of the must visit place for Science Students. It is open from 11 am to 5 pm every day except Monday. Satellite cities, Pimpri-Chinchwad, though are considered to be a part of Pune, they also have their own separate identities. Located just a few kilometres from Pune, Pimpri-Chinchwad is the industrial hub, and home to some of the biggest companies in the country. The area is also home to some of the best colleges and institutes in the city. They play a huge role in Pune being known as a world-class education hub. Owing to the presence of many companies, Pimpri-Chinchwad is also a residential hub. One of the biggest reasons why Pimpri-Chinchwad is such a great place is because of its infrastructure. Wide concrete roads, underpasses, excellent service roads, and now even the Metro rail, Pimpri-Chinchwad is one of the most developed places in Pune. With so many things going its way, Pimpri-Chinchwad also has many places worth visiting. From places of historical importance to man-made marvels, theres a lot you can see and do in Pimpri-Chinchwad. In the following blog, theres a list of top 10 places to visit in Pimpri-Chinchwad. So, the next time you are going to be in Pune, do visit Pimpri-Chinchwad and check these places out.   Top 10 Places To Visit In Pimpri-Chinchwad Theres much to see and do in Pimpri-Chinchwad. If you are planning on just visiting, or even thinking of living for work or education, you are guaranteed to have a great time here thanks to these places. 1. Appu Ghar (Indira Gandhi Udyan) Image Source: Google Even before all the big amusement parks in India were even thought of, Appu Ghar existed in the Pimpri-Chinchwad area. It was developed as an eco-friendly place where children and parents can have a great time. Appu Ghar is at the foothill of Shri Durga Devi Temple, amidst lush greenery. The work for the park began in 1989, and it was completed in 1992. Later on, a water park section was also added to the park. Appu Ghar is an excellent place for family outings, school picnics, and even corporate retreats. It is one of the best places to see in Pimpri-Chinchwad.   2. Durga Tekdi Image Source: Google Right next Appu Ghar is the beautiful Durga Tekdi. It is one of the greenest places in the region with more 200,000 trees being planted and maintained by the Pimpri-Chinchwad Corporation. Though slightly crowded on weekends, Durga Tekdi is an ideal place for a picnic, or simply a hangout where you can relax and breathe in the fresh air. Nearby, there are many wonderful food joints as well, thanks to the presence of Appu Ghar. Don't forget to check out the lake as well. It is home to beautiful birdlife.   3. Bhaja Caves Image Source: Google Just a few kilometres ahead of Pimpri-Chinchwad is the village of Malauli. It is right before Lonavala. Close to the village is another village, Bhaja, and atop the hill near the village, 400 feet to be exact, are the legendary Bhaja Caves. So, whats special about this place? For starters, it was built in the 2nd Century B.C. The Bhaja Caves have been carved into the rock formation. They are Buddhist Caves, and have multiple Stupas in them. This is a beautiful place that is on the list of Monument of National Importance. Its a must-visit for all those who love history and ancient culture.   4. Karla Caves Image Source: Google Just like the Bhaja Caves, the Karla Caves are also Buddhist caves carved into stone. They were made during the 2nd century BC to the 5th century BC. This area used to be an important trade route. The caves are so well-made that even today, they are in excellent condition. Karla Caves are just a short drive or train ride away from Pimpri-Chinchwad. Many people from all over the country come to look at these phenomenal caves that showcase how talented our ancestors truly were. The caves are at a height, so be ready for doing some mild trekking. You can easily spend a few hours here, and click some amazing pictures.   5. Balaji Mandir Image Source: Google The people of Pimpri-Chinchwad and Pune dont have to travel to the actual Balaji temple down south, as there is an exact replica of the Balaji temple right here in the nearby village of Narayanpur. Famed as Mini Tirupati, this place sees a lot of devotees who come here for darshan and even enjoy the lush green surroundings. People actually make a whole day of it, as this place is huge. Families pack picnic baskets and head out to spend the whole day in the venerated surroundings. There are also many places around where you can relax, eat, and be at peace. All the tourists who come to Pimpri-Chinchwad make sure to visit this place.   6. Pimpri-Chinchwad Science Park Image Source: Google Delve deep into the world of science at the Pimpri-Chinchwad Science Park. This is the biggest science park in the twin cities, spread on close to 4 acres of land. Most of the land is an open-air science park. The rest of it is an auditorium, science &amp; tech galleries, activity areas, and even an inflatable Taramandal. Every attraction in the park is made to be interactive, so you are not just getting entertained, but also learning a lot. The park also shows 3D shows, live demonstrations, and taramandal shows. It is quite a lively place, and one of the best tourist destinations in Pimpri-Chinchwad.   7. Bird Valley Udyan Image Source: Google This is a wonderful place to come and watch rare bird species in their natural habitat. The udyan has a gorgeous body of water that is full of ducks. Theres also a walkway around the body where you can take a stroll and watch the beautiful birds in the park. Bird Valley Udyan is also a wonderful picnic destination, especially for families. On the small lake, you can go boating along with your loved ones. The beautiful surroundings of the area will put you in a good mood.   8. Diamond Parks Image Source: Google Splish, splash, and a lot of fun, Diamond Parks are one of the top tourist destinations in Pimpri-Chinchwad. It has an adventure park full of adventurous rides - 29 to be exact. You can zipline, zorb, do rock climbing, bungee jumping, and many other such wonderful activities. The most popular section of Diamond Park is the waterpark. This place also has many rides and slides. All 28 rides are of international quality that will ensure hours of unlimited fun. The wave pool too is amazing with 8 different types of waves. Diamond Parks attract crowds from all over the state, and you must visit this place too.   9. Old Mumbai-Pune Highway Image Source: Google Whats so special about a highway? Well, you have to travel to the Old Mumbai-Pune Highway and know for yourself. The highway has been developed in recent years to be just 20 mins slower than the Expressway. But the main advantage of this place is that unlike the Expressway, you can stop at the wonderful locations on the highway. Old Mumbai-Pune Highway has many wonderful stops where you can click pictures amidst the greenery. There are also amazing dhabas where you can have delicious food, and watch the world go by. The Old highway is even more beautiful during the monsoons as small waterfalls form along the roads. The whole highway encompasses in foggy beauty. You can afford to miss out travelling on this stunning highway that goes through a section of the naturally blessed Western Ghats.   10. Thergaon Boat Club Image Source: Google The Pimpri-Chinchwad area is full of many water bodies, and the big advantage of this is that it allows many lush green parks on them. One such place is the Thergaon Boat Club. Located in Thergaon, this is one of the best places for you to spend time in the Pimpri-Chinchwad area. The park has a garden where you can relax and enjoy the peaceful atmosphere. If you have kids with you, theres also a playground full of play equipment where they can play and have fun. But the biggest feature of the park is the boats. You can hire a nice boat, and enjoy a leisurely time on the lake.   Pimpri-Chinchwad is one of the most wonderful areas near Pune. It is the prime example of infrastructural development without harming the natural beauty of an area. The above-mentioned are the top 10 places to visit in Pimpri-Chinchwad, but there are many other places as well that are worth a visit. So, the next time you are in Pune or Pimpri-Chinchwad, plan an itinerary packed with the above-mentioned places and have a wonderful time.
