# Places to Visit in Kalyan

Kalyan  is mostly known for providing a significant workforce to Mumbai, one of the most important cities in India. The city of Kalyan is situated on the Ulhas River banks located in Maharashtra's Thane district. More specifically, it is located in the Koncan division. The whole city is maintained by the municipal corporation of Kalyan-Dombivli. Here it should be mentioned that Kalyan is a subsection under the Thane district. Kalyan is one of the founding cities of the Metropolitan region of Mumbai. The Kalyan city and its associated township of Dombivli region together form the Kalyan-Dombivli Municipal Corporation. In short, the municipal corporation is called KDMC. It is the 9th largest city among all the cities in Maharashtra and it is the 29th largest city among all the cities in India.

[**Durgadi Fort**] 

##### Witness the fort’s beauty and exquisite designs.

The Durgadi Fort is a place of historical significance and is located on the west of Kalyan. The Durgadi fort is located close to the Mumbai Agra road near several creeks on the small hill. The main entrance of the fort is known as Ganesh Darwaja. After entering you can easily visit the temple of Goddess Durga, located inside the fort. In the area, you may also find two Bastions and some remnants of the fort on the side of the creek.

## FAQs - Frequently Asked Questions about Kalyan

### How is the weather of Kalyan in peak season?

The weather of Kalyan in peak season is the winter period is just perfect to visit Kalyan as during this period the city provides travelers  with pleasant slightly warm weather to the travelers. During this period the temperature varies between 33.7°C – 30.5°C. On average it snows or rains insignificantly during this time and so are busy with tourists. Prices for hotels and restaurants are comparatively high as it is the peak season. It means you can easily plan a trip to Kalyan in the months of ​​​​​​​​December, January and February to enjoy your trip to the fullest.

### What are some of the best places to stay in Kalyan?

Kalyan has a large variety of resorts and stays that you can select from ranging from budget to luxurious stays. Some of the most popular places to stay in Kalyan are Hotel Stay Inn Kalyan, Hotel Praveen International and Anand Sagar Water park and resort.

### Which are the most popular shopping spots in Kalyan?

The best shopping places in Kalyan are Japani Bazar, Sarvoday Mall and Metro Junction Mall. You will have an amazing time exploring and finding unique things to buy at these shopping hotspots in Kalyan and will definitely come back home with a bag full of lovely artifacts and experiences.

 All about Dombivli
 
 
 
Dombivli, a bustling suburb in Maharashtra, India, offers a unique blend of cultural heritage and modern amenities, making it a must-visit destination for travelers. One of the best ways to explore the rich history and vibrant culture of this region is by taking the Charming Walking Tour, which will guide you through the most fascinating legends and charming places in Kalyan-Dombivli. Whether you’re a history buff or simply looking to immerse yourself in the local culture, this tour is sure to leave you captivated. Beyond the historical sites, Dombivli boasts a variety of local markets, delicious street food, and serene parks, providing a perfect balance of activities for both locals and tourists alike. Come and discover the hidden gems of Dombivli, where every corner has a story to tell. 
 
 
 
 
 FAQs 
 
 
 
 
 What is the best time to visit Dombivli? 
 
 
 
 The best time to visit Dombivli is during the winter months from November to February when the weather is pleasant and suitable for sightseeing. 
 
 
 
 
 How can I reach Dombivli? 
 
 
 
 Dombivli is well-connected by rail and road. The nearest airport is Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport in Mumbai, about 40 km away. From there, you can take a taxi or a train to Dombivli. 
 
 
 
 
 What are the must-visit places in Dombivli? 
 
 
 
 Some must-visit places in and around Dombivli include the Ganesh Temple, Khidkaleshwar Temple, and the nearby Matheran Hill Station for a day trip. 
 
 
 
 
 What kind of accommodation options are available in Dombivli? 
 
 
 
 Dombivli offers a range of accommodation options from budget hotels to mid-range hotels. For a more comfortable stay, you can also find serviced apartments. 
 
 
 
 
 Is Dombivli safe for solo female travelers? 
 
 
 
 Dombivli is generally safe for solo female travelers, but it's always advisable to take standard precautions such as avoiding isolated areas at night and keeping your belongings secure. 
 
 
 
 
 What local dishes should I try in Dombivli? 
 
 
 
 When in Dombivli, you should try local Maharashtrian dishes like Vada Pav, Misal Pav, and Puran Poli. There are also several good eateries offering a variety of cuisines. 
 
 
 
 
 Are there any cultural events or festivals in Dombivli? 
 
 
 
 Dombivli celebrates several cultural events and festivals, the most notable being Ganesh Chaturthi. The city comes alive with decorations, processions, and cultural performances during this time. 
 
 
 
 
 What is the local language spoken in Dombivli? 
 
 
 
 The local language spoken in Dombivli is Marathi. However, Hindi and English are also widely understood and spoken. 
 
 
 
 
 What transportation options are available within Dombivli? 
 
 
 
 Within Dombivli, you can use auto-rickshaws, taxis, and local buses for transportation. The local train network is also a convenient option for traveling to nearby areas. 
 
 
 
 
 Do I need a visa to visit Dombivli, Maharashtra? 
 
 
 
 Visa requirements depend on your nationality. It's best to check the latest visa regulations from the Indian embassy or consulate in your country before planning your trip.
