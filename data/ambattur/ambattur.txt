_Varahi Amman is one of the prominent goddesses of South India. Worshipped primarily in Tamil Nadu, Odisha, and Karnataka, she is one of the seven Matrikas (mother goddesses). The matrikas are all various incarnations of Shakti. Female version of Varaha, Varahi too is a fearsome goddess with the head of a boar and the body of a woman. There are many temples dedicated to the goddess in Chennai, but the **Varahi Amman Temple Ambattur** is a special one. Read on to know more._

![The deity of the Varahi Amman Temple in Ambattur]

One of the best [Varahi Amman temples in Chennai] to worship the goddess is in Ambattur. Located next to the Sai Baba temple, this is a small and humble abode of goddess Varahi but one can feel a powerful and spiritual ambience here. The temple is run by a single priest. One can feel the devotion towards Varahi Amman, especially on Fridays when followers bring the goddess pomegranate flowers.

Varahi Amman is one of the revered Hindu goddesses in Tamil Nadu. Her devotees are intensely ardent and visit the goddess in her temples throughout the city. This temple also features quintessential South Indian architecture and has significant historical and religious significance. The temple is dedicated to Goddess Varahi, an incarnation of Goddess Shakti. The temple’s exquisite frescoes and paintings depict Goddess Varahi’s story and her triumph over evil forces. Varahi Amman is a fierce goddess with the face of a sow and the body of a woman.

## Significance Of Varahi Amman Temple Ambattur

![Learn about the legend of the Varahi Amman Temple Ambattur] 

[Image Source: Pexels] 

The goddess Varahi is commonly honoured in Shaktism, a sect of Hinduism in which each deity is considered to be a woman. With the head of a boar and a woman’s body, the ferocious Varahi is the female version of Varaha, the third incarnation of Lord Vishnu. For this reason, the goddess Varahi Amman is also highly revered among Vaishnavites (devotees of Vishnu) and Shaivities (devotees of Shiva).

In her vengeful form, the goddess is popular for vanquishing the demons Raktabija and Visukaran. But she is not entirely without mercy and generosity. The goddess also blesses women with fertility fruit and heals diseases and illnesses. Also known as Dhruma Varahi (or Dark Varahi), the goddess is often worshipped at night. Worshipping Varahi Amman is also said to bring wealth and prosperity to her subjects. Many temples are dedicated to her in Chennai, and visiting Varahi Amman temple Ambattur is essential for a fruitful pilgrimage experience.

The Varahi Amman Temple in Ambattur is an excellent example of South Indian temple architecture. The primary sanctum, where the deity is placed, exudes a powerful and divine aura, making it a revered space for worship. The temple features beautiful gopurams and a magnificent shikara adorned with detailed carvings that showcase stories from Hindu mythology. Each pillar and wall features artistic depictions that resonate with the history of temples in the state.

## Festivals Of The Varahi Amman Temple

![Celebration of Chithirai Festival] 

[Image Source: Facebook] 

One of the most famous [festivals of Tamil Nadu] is the Chithirai festival, which is celebrated in the Tamil month of Chithirai. During this festival, the priests perform a special pooja. The Chithirai festival is a 15-day event usually held at the beginning of April. It is believed to bring harmony to everyone, irrespective of caste and religion.

Another divine festival celebrated in the temple is the Aadi Month festival. During this festival, the elderly ladies who visit the temple perform a ritual for the younger girls’ well-being and give them bangles and prasad. In Tamil Nadu, Ashadha Navratri is known as the Aadi Month festival, during which the devotees offer a variety of rice to the goddess Varahi.

# Ambattur

## Welcome to Ambattur

# Ambattur: A Hidden Gem in Chennai, Tamil Nadu

Ambattur is a bustling town located in the northern part of Chennai, Tamil Nadu. Known for its rapid urbanization, it is fast becoming one of the most sought-after destinations for both business and leisure travelers. Whether you're exploring the local culture, looking for places to stay, or seeking a peaceful getaway, Ambattur offers something for everyone.

In this guide, we will delve into everything you need to know about Ambattur, from tourist attractions and local food to transport options and the best time to visit.

## Overview of Ambattur

Ambattur is situated approximately 25 kilometers away from Chennai city center, making it an ideal spot for tourists who want to enjoy the charm of Chennai while staying away from the busy urban hustle. Ambattur is not just an industrial hub, but a growing locality with various residential complexes, parks, and markets. Its location along the Chennai-Bangalore National Highway makes it easily accessible by road and rail.

Ambattur, though primarily an industrial area, is rapidly becoming a favorite place to stay for people working in and around Chennai. It offers a quieter atmosphere compared to the main city, yet it is well-connected to the heart of Chennai, making it an excellent choice for both short-term and long-term stays.

## Location and Accessibility

Ambattur is strategically located in the northern part of Chennai, making it well-connected by road and rail. It lies close to key areas such as Anna Nagar, Perambur, and Koyambedu, and is easily accessible from the Chennai International Airport (around 16 kilometers away) and the Chennai Central Railway Station (approximately 17 kilometers away).

For tourists traveling by train, the Ambattur Railway Station is well-connected to the Chennai suburban network, providing convenient access to other parts of the city and beyond.

## Best Time to Visit Ambattur

The best time to visit Ambattur, Chennai, is during the cooler months, from November to February. During this period, the weather is pleasant, making outdoor activities and sightseeing more enjoyable.

### Weather in Ambattur

Ambattur experiences a tropical climate with hot summers, a humid monsoon season, and mild winters. Summer temperatures can rise up to 40°C (104°F), making it uncomfortable for outdoor activities. Monsoon season brings moderate rainfall from June to September, which cools the region but may disrupt travel plans. Winters, on the other hand, are more comfortable with temperatures ranging from 20°C to 30°C, which is perfect for sightseeing and exploring the town.

## Tourist Attractions in Ambattur

Although Ambattur may not be as well-known for tourism as other areas of Chennai, there are still several attractions that will pique your interest.

### 1\. **Ambattur Lake**

One of the most serene places in Ambattur, this lake provides a perfect spot for an afternoon stroll. The surrounding greenery offers a refreshing escape from the urban landscape. The lake is an ideal place to relax and enjoy the natural beauty of the area.

### 2\. **Madhavaram Tank**

Not far from Ambattur, the Madhavaram Tank is another peaceful place to unwind. Surrounded by lush greenery, it is a great location for those interested in nature photography and birdwatching.

### 3\. **Koyambedu Market**

Although technically not in Ambattur, Koyambedu Market is just a short distance away and is one of Chennai's largest wholesale markets. Here, you can experience the vibrant local culture and shop for fresh produce, flowers, and local goods. The market also offers a variety of local eateries.

### 4\. **Perambur Railway Heritage Museum**

Located nearby, the Perambur Railway Heritage Museum offers a glimpse into Chennai's rich railway history. The museum houses a collection of old locomotives, railway artifacts, and exhibits that will interest both history and train enthusiasts.

### 5\. **Shirdi Sai Baba Temple**

Although Ambattur is not known for large temples, it is home to a beautiful Shirdi Sai Baba Temple, which attracts devotees and visitors alike. The peaceful environment makes it a nice spot for a visit.

## Culture of Ambattur

The culture of Ambattur is deeply rooted in traditional Tamil customs and practices. Though it is more of an industrial area, you can still experience the vibrant Tamil culture in the local markets, eateries, and festivals.

The people of Ambattur celebrate various traditional Tamil festivals, such as Pongal, Tamil New Year, and Deepavali. The town also hosts smaller local events, including cultural performances, fairs, and temple festivals. While in Ambattur, you can enjoy traditional music, dance performances, and art exhibitions that reflect the cultural richness of the region.

## Local Food Options

Ambattur offers a wide range of local Tamil delicacies, and there are several food options for travelers. From simple street food stalls to more formal eateries, you can experience authentic Tamil cuisine. Here are some popular food options to try while visiting:

### 1\. **Dosa and Idli**

For breakfast, enjoy a classic South Indian meal of dosa (thin crispy crepes) and idli (steamed rice cakes) served with chutneys and sambar. These dishes are available at most local eateries.

### 2\. **Vada**

Another favorite snack in Tamil Nadu, vada is a deep-fried, savory doughnut-shaped fritter made from lentils, and it's often enjoyed with chutney or sambar.

### 3\. **Chettinad Cuisine**

Ambattur has a number of restaurants offering Chettinad cuisine, famous for its rich flavors and aromatic spices. Dishes such as Chettinad chicken curry and fish curry are highly recommended.

### 4\. **Parotta and Korma**

This flaky flatbread paired with a rich, spicy gravy is a must-try when visiting Ambattur.

### 5\. **Sweet Treats**

Don’t miss out on traditional sweets like _payasam_ (a rice pudding made with milk and sugar) and _jilebi_ (crispy, sugary fried dough).

## Stay Options in Ambattur

While Ambattur might not have many luxurious resorts or five-star hotels, it does offer a variety of affordable accommodation options that are perfect for travelers on a budget. From guesthouses and budget hotels to serviced apartments, Ambattur has something to suit every need.

### 1\. **Budget Hotels**

Several budget hotels in Ambattur offer clean, comfortable rooms at affordable prices. These hotels are ideal for short stays, and most are conveniently located near transportation hubs like the Ambattur Railway Station.

### 2\. **Guesthouses**

For a more homely feel, you can opt for guesthouses that provide basic amenities and a local experience. These options are great for solo travelers and families looking for a quiet stay.

### 3\. **Service Apartments**

For longer stays or a more private experience, service apartments in Ambattur are a good option. They offer the comforts of home with a kitchen, laundry facilities, and other amenities.

## Local Transportation

Ambattur is well-connected to the rest of Chennai, with several transportation options available. The Ambattur Railway Station is the main station in the area, providing local train services to and from Chennai. For a quicker commute, you can take a taxi or auto-rickshaw, which are easily available.

### 1\. **Trains**

The local train network is one of the most popular modes of transportation for those traveling to and from Ambattur. It connects the town with Chennai and other suburban areas.

### 2\. **Buses**

There are also several bus services operating between Ambattur and various parts of Chennai. These are budget-friendly and convenient for daily commutes.

### 3\. **Taxis and Auto-Rickshaws**

For a more private and flexible travel experience, taxis and auto-rickshaws are easily available throughout Ambattur. You can hire them to travel around the town or to nearby attractions.

## Excursions Around Ambattur

While Ambattur itself offers some interesting attractions, there are several excursions nearby that you can explore. The proximity to Chennai makes it easy to visit popular tourist destinations, such as Marina Beach, Fort St. George, and the famous Kapaleeshwarar Temple.

### 1\. **Elliot's Beach**

A short drive from Ambattur, Elliot's Beach in Besant Nagar is a quieter alternative to the busy Marina Beach. It’s a great place to relax, take a walk, or enjoy the coastal views.

### 2\. **Guindy National Park**

Located about 12 kilometers from Ambattur, the Guindy National Park is a great place for nature lovers. It is home to a variety of wildlife, including deer, monkeys, and numerous bird species.

### 3\. **Arignar Anna Zoological Park**

Also known as Vandalur Zoo, this zoological park is one of the largest in India. It’s located a little further south from Ambattur and is perfect for a family outing.
