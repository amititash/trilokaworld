It is considered to be a popular location as it is a residential area with a fantastic market, good schools, colleges, and temples in its vicinity. The convenient connectivity of Mira Road to Central Mumbai further makes it an exciting location for day trips. The area is home to some of the most scenic beaches, where people can enjoy the vibrant nightlife and even beach activities like swimming – you can have the most adventurous day out!

## Best Places to Visit in Mira Bhayandar

Whether you are looking for a relaxing day at the beach near Mira road or adventure activities, here are some of the best places to visit in Bhayandar.

### 1) Gorai Beach

Gorai is a popular beach located in the northern part of Mira Bhayandur. With its picturesque landscape, it provides a perfect setting for a relaxing evening. When the evening falls, the beach near Mira road comes to life with people enjoying good music and food at restaurants and beachside bars.

One can also visit local attractions like the Global Vipassana Pagoda located very near to Gorai **Beach.** It is a beautiful temple with intricate design and stunning views. All in all, if you are someone looking for a peaceful escape, this is the place to be!

**Known for**: Picnic area, popular sand beach near Mira road

**Location:** Gorai Beach Road, Koliwada, Gorai, Mumbai, Maharashtra

**Timings:** All day

### 2) Bhayandar Beach

Bhayandar beach is one of the best beaches near Mira Road for couples, family, or even friends to enjoy a relaxing day by the beach. The coastal spot with crystal clear water makes it ideal for beach activities like swimming or leisurely walking along the shoreline.

Additionally, Mira Bhayandur Beach has numerous popular cafes and restaurants in close proximity that serve a range of delicious food and beverages. The best part is that one can easily enjoy one’s favourite snack and drink while watching the gorgeous sunset.

**Known for**: Beach activities, sunset views

**Location:** Uttan Pali Road, Dongri, Uttan, Mira Bhayandur, Maharashtra

**Timings:** All day

### 3) Juice Adventure Park

If you are looking for an adventurous day out, Juice Adventures Park in Mira Bhayandur is a popular place to check out! The adventure park offers a variety of attractions in a relaxing atmosphere for visitors of all ages. One of the key features of the park is its outdoor activities, which include ATV, Air Ball challenge, ziplining, etc.

Furthermore, there’s a beautiful picnic area where families can enjoy the meal in the open air. [Juice Adventure Park]  also hosts themed events and activities, making it a famous place for couples and children alike.

Visitors can choose from varied packages that are designed to suit a range of requirements. This includes the Classic Corporate package for team-building events, One Day Fun Day for an adventurous day out, Crazy Kids – which offers a flat 15% off for kids on Sundays, and the Activities Only package, perfect for those who want to enjoy the thrilling attractions.

**Known for:** Outdoor activities, scenic picnic spots

**Location:** Pali Chowk Road, Bhayandar, Dongri, Uttan, Mumbai, Mira Bhayandur

**Timings:** 10 am to 6 pm

### 4) Sanjay Gandhi National Park

Sanjay Gandhi National Park, located just 7 km from Mira Road, is one of the most popular and best places to visit in Bhayandar for adventure lovers and nature enthusiasts. This park is home to a variety of flora and fauna, including leopards, monkeys, tigers, and various species of birds.

It also provides ample opportunities for recreational activities, making it a perfect holiday destination for families and couples. There’s a train ride that offers a relaxing tour of the park. Additionally, one can plan picnics in designated areas and enjoy serene boat rides in this famous place. All in all, if you are looking for a place to visit near Mira Road, [Sanjay Gandhi National Park]  is a perfect spot for a day-long excursion.

**Known for:** Flora, fauna, picnic spot

**Location:** Western Express Highway, Borivali East, Mumbai, Maharashtra

**Timings:** 9 am to 6 pm

### 5) Essel World Water Kingdom

Make this summer holiday unforgettable with Asia’s largest water theme park – Water Kingdom. It is a 64-acre theme park that boasts rides suitable for people of all age groups. Some of the popular rides you can find at Water Kingdom include Black Demon, Adventures Amazonia, and more. This is certainly one of the most adventurous and best places to visit near Mira road.

Water Kingdom is also home to India’s biggest aqua play pool, The Lagoon. It boasts several popular and thrilling rides which allows people of varied interests to find something that gives them the adventure rush they are looking for.

Additionally, Water Kingdom offers a range of facilities, including changing areas, popular food stalls, and exclusive souvenir shops. It is no surprise that the popular [Essel World Water Kingdom]  is considered to be one of the best places to visit in Bhayandar.

**Known for:** Family, adult and kid rides

**Location:** Global Pagoda Road, Gorai Island, Borivali West, Mumbai, Maharashtra

**Timings:** 10 am to 6 pm

### 6) Maxus Mall

Maxus Mall is one of the best places to visit in Mira Bhayandar for anyone looking for a one-stop destination for dining, entertainment and shopping. The ground floor of the mall features mehendi artists, while on the first floor, you can find fashion and clothing shops from brands like Nike to John Players.

The second floor features a food court and a bowling alley. On the topmost floor is a six-screen multiplex, making it one of the most exciting and popular places to visit near Mira Road. It can accommodate up to 1300 people. Additionally, the mall has a basement parking lot. All of these offerings make Maxus Mall one of the popular places to visit in Mira road for couples.

**Known for:** Entertainment, shopping, dining

**Location**: Temba Rd, Bhayandar, Padmavati Nagar, Bhayandar West, Mira Bhayandur

**Timings:** 11 am to 10 pm

### 7) Kanheri Caves

The Kanheri Caves is a famous place in the Sanjay Gandhi National Park, in the Northern region of Mira Bhayandur. It includes over 100 individual caves showcasing a wonderful collection of ancient Buddhist art.

The caves are home to a number of sculptures, carvings and inscriptions that depict ancient Buddhist stories. Additionally, the area surrounding the cave offers trails for hiking. The cave’s unique location within the National Park makes it an ideal place to visit on Mira Road for a fun holiday trip.

**Known for**: Ancient Buddhist rock-cut caves

**Location:** Sanjay Gandhi National Park, Western Express Highway, Borivali East, Mumbai, Maharashtra

**Timings:** 7:30 am to 5 pm
