# 6 Places To Visit In Ulhasnagar: Explore Sindhi Paradise In 2025

..

SHARES

Updated Date: 16 October 2024

_Positioned on the outskirts of Mumbai, Ulhasnagar is a vibrant city that often gets missed owing to the popularity of other neighbouring cities like Thane, Mumbai, Nashik, and more. However, this hidden gem has a lot to offer to vacationers. From cultural diversity to bustling markets, to alluring tourist spots, there’s so much about to city to woo its guests. Be it the divinity of the Ganesh Ghat or the liveliness of Ulhasnagar No. 4 Shopping Area, you are bound to enjoy all kinds of vibes of the city. Listed below are a few **places to visit in Ulhasnagar** that need your attention!_

## 6 Places To Visit In Ulhasnagar

If you are wondering what to explore in Ulhasnagar then this list of attractions will surefire help you know the city in the easiest way. Each tourist place in Ulhanagar boasts its own uniqueness that you can witness only while visiting the spot.

### 1\. Ganesh Ghat

![Imbibe the blissful vibes of Ganesh Ghat which is one of the best places to visit in Ulhasnagar] 

[Image Credit: Abhijitsathe for Wikipedia] 

Ganesh Ghat is a popular place to visit in Ulhasnagar where tourists as well as locals get to revel in the sheer sanctity of Ulhas River. You can simply sit by the Ghat and treat your senses with mesmerising views and utter calmness. The ghat becomes alive especially during the festival of Ganesh Chathurthi when devotees from far and wide come to the ghat for the immersion of the idol of Shri Ganesh, popularly known as Ganesh Visarjan.

**Location:** 752F+JHC, Mharal, Ulhasnagar, Maharashtra 421103

**Timings:** Sunrise to sunset

**Must Read:** [12 Resorts In Nashik For A Vacation Amid Lush Vineyards] 

### 2\. Swami Shanti Prakash Aashram

![Spend some moments of spirituality at Swami Shanti Prakash Aashram, one of the top places to visit in Ulhasnagar] 

[Image Courtesy: Swani Shanti Prakash Ashram for Facebook] 

One of the most revered sites for the Sindhi community, Swami Shanti Prakash Aashram, is a heavenly abode for those interested in spirituality and architecture. The stunning design of the ashram along with its intricate carvings, and soulful atmosphere make the spot one of the best places to visit in Ulhasnagar From indulging in religious prayers to listening to profound preachings to having a rendezvous with pure souls, there’s a plethora of experiences to try.

**Location:** No 5, Swami Teuram Chowk, near Dhudh Naka, Kailash Colony, Ulhasnagar, Maharashtra 421005

**Timings:** 4:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.

### 3\. Ulhas River

![Relax your senses with the serene views of Ulhas River which is one of the top places to visit in Ulhasnagar] 

[Image Credit: Ganesh Dhamodkar for Wikimedia Commons] 

Find solace by the banks of the Ulhas River which is also one of the best places to visit in Ulhasnagar for couples. The tranquil aura and serene landscape make a perfect amalgam for a leisurely stroll or a picnic with friends and family. The lush greenery and the gentle flow of the river create a soothing environment that’s perfect for soul refreshment. To make the most of your experience, plan a visit during the early hours of the morning.

**Location:** Karjat, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India.

**Timings:** Sunrise to sunset

**Suggested Read:** [The New Fossil Park To Open In Maharashtra Will Help You Relive Your ‘Jurassic Park’ Memories] 

### 4\. Ulhasnagar No. 4 Shopping Area

![Enjoy retail therapy at Ulhasnagar No. 4 Shopping Area] 

[Image Source: Shutterstock] 

The bustling marketplace of Ulhasnagar No. 4 is a shopping haven for both locals and tourists. This area is particularly known for its street markets where you can find a variety of clothes, accessories, electronic goods, and more at budget-friendly prices. This vibrant market is a great place to soak in the local culture and indulge in some retail therapy. Besides this, there are also several eateries where you can satiate your hunger pangs.

**Location:** Ulhasnagar, Maharashtra

**Timings:** 10:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m.

### 5\. Tansa Wildlife Sanctuary

![Witness intriguing flora and fauna at Tansa Wildlife Sanctuary] 

[Image Source: Shutterstock] 

Named after the Tansa River, Tansa Wildlife Sanctuary is a remarkable tourist place near ulhasnagar for those who resonate with flora and fauna. Lying around 60 km from Ulhasnagar, the sanctuary offers a habitat for both wildlife and a variety of plant species, making it a major area for conservation and ecological balance. The sanctuary covers an area of 320 square km and is home to around 54 species of animals and 200 species of birds. Leopards, porcupines, sloth bears, barking deer, and wild boars are some animals visitors can spot here.

**Location:** Palghar district, Maharashtra, India‎

**Timings:** 7:00 a.m. To 6:00 p.m.

**Suggested Read:** [Top 5 Places To Visit In Pandharpur For A Spiritual Sojourn] 

### 6\. Ammu Water Park

![Spend a day fun-filled day at Ammu Water Park while exploring places to visit in Ulhasnagar] 

[Image Courtesy: Ammu Water Park] 

Enjoying the green environs, Ammu Water Park is the ultimate hub for couples and families alike. Fascinating features like 10 water slides, a gigantic waterfall, a mini wave pool, a deep swimming pool, a fantastic DJ, and more allow visitors to make the most of their enjoyable moments at Ammu Water Park in Ulhasnagar. Apart from this, kids get to equally enjoy the park as it houses a separate pool for them, a kids’ aqua zone, a mini train, and a play park. Moreover, the on-site restaurant serves scrumptious dishes to satiate your hunger pangs.

**Location:** Apti Road, Off Kalyan Murbad Road District Thane, Vaholi, Maharashtra 421301

**Timings:** 9:45 a.m. To 4:45 p.m.

**Entry fee:** ₹599 for adults; ₹399 for children

**Further Read:** [Top 22 Places To Visit In Maharashtra In Summer For A Cool Vacation]
