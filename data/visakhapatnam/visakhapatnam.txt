# Places to Visit in Vizag (Visakhapatnam)

There is nothing like escaping to the seas from our busy, chaotic city lives. The feel of fresh air caressing our hair and warm waves splashing against our feet is one of the ideal forms of vacation. This exact dream like holiday destination can be yours in Visakhapatnam, also known as Vizag. A port city, it is known for its variety of industries, but beyond grey, concrete buildings are beautiful beaches, temples, caves and valleys.

The city is perpetually thronging with tourists, every one escaping to a few days of freedom and relief from hectic life. Imagine being able to visit a place that is nothing short of a treasure land. From history to culture to unlimited activities and unlimited places to visit, you have it all right here in Vizag.

The joys of Visakhapatnam lie in its subtle hints at history, culture with a whiff of modernism in the air. This perfect amalgamation of some of the best aspects of a holiday destination is what makes the city one of the best places to visit with ample places to discover. Some of them include:

### 1\. [Simhachalam Temple] 

![Simhachalam Temple, Vizag Top Places to Visit] 

The Simhachalam Temple in Visakhapatnam is one of the most popular places to visit. Also known as Sri Varaha Lakshmi Narasimha temple, it is dedicated to the Hindu deity, Vishnu. The legend is that Vishnu, manifested in the form of a half lion and half human form to save his devotee Prahlada from his murder by his own father, Hiranyakashipu.

Resembling a fortress from the outside, the temple consists of five gateways and three beautiful courtyards. The temple is a visual representation in the form of architecture of the reign of Kalinga, Chalukyas and Cholas.

### 2\. [Rama Krishna Beach] 

![RK Beach, Vizag Top Places to Visit] 

A famous beach popular among locals and tourists alike is the Ramakrishna Beach, named after Ramakrishna Mission ashram located nearby. A perfect place for enjoying fun filled activities such as swimming, sunbathing, surfing and playing beach volleyball, RK beach doesn’t disappoint you.

You can also enjoy camel ride or a ride in one of the fishing boats operated by the local fishers here. If not anything, you can simply sit and relax while enjoying the sunset.

### 3\. [INS Kurusura Submarine Museum] 

![INS Kurusura Submarine Museum, Vizag Top Places to Visit] 

Vishakhaptanam, is a port city, built on the coast of the Bay of Bengal, hence, a submarine museum in the city is of little surprise. Set up in 2011, the museum houses the INS Kurusura that was constructed by Russians in the year 1969, reaching India in 1970.

It served the Indian Navy greatly until the year 2001, when it was decommissioned. The submarine has experienced and played a vital role in various wars, including the Indo-Pak war of 1971. It is the first submarine turned museum in Asia.

For all the history lovers, waste no moment in wondering whether you should visit this museum. Starting from the evolution of submarines in war, to equipment and artefacts, quench your thirst by peeking into the life inside a submarine.

### 4\. [Rishikonda Beach] 

![Rishikonda Beach, Vizag Top Places to Visit] 

Get lost in the sound of the waves splashing against the warm sand at your feet or swim away into the cool waters in the sea at the very famous Rishikonda Beach. Pocketed under Rishikonda hill, the beach is famous for its perfectly, serene beauty. It could not get any better than this.

The beach gives you a preview into the best of two worlds; hills and the beach. If you are the adventurous kind, do not forget to try out the various water sports that are offered on the beach. They are all under expert supervision, so there is no need to worry!

### 5\. [Araku Valley] 

![Araku Valley, Vizag Top Places to Visit] 

Visakhapatnam is a place where you get to experience a variety of things. While there are ample beaches, waterfalls to explore, there is also no dearth of valleys. One such valley that you should not miss in your Vizag tour is the Araku Valley.

Araku is essentially a hill station, situated 120kms away from Visakhapatnam, which is rich in green fields, coffee plantations, picturesque landscapes and most importantly, wonderful weather. The valley is inhabited by tribes and it also contains a museum showcasing some of the best artwork by the localites. The journey to the valley by train is simply breath-taking. A must visit to all!

### 6\. [Kailasagiri Park] 

![Kailasagiri Park, Vizag Top Places to Visit] 

Ever wondered what it must feel like to have a bird's eye view? At this point, Visakhapatnam must feel like a wishing well. The city fulfils wishes before you even know what to dream of. Kailasagiri is nothing but a park on top of a hill. The best part about this hill is that it literally provides you with a panoramic view of the city of Visakhapatnam.

Overlooking beaches, forests and the city, Kailasagiri is a promising place to visit during your stay in the city. Starting from leisurely sitting on a bench and enjoying the view to participating in a variety of activities, you will undoubtedly have a lot of fun.

### 7\. [Borra Caves] 

![Borra Caves, Vizag Top Places to Visit] 

An adrenaline rush, a sense of the unknown will grip you as you step into Borra Caves of Visakhapatnam. According to William King George of the Geological Survey of India, these caves are about one million years old. They are a sight to behold. Their origins are uncertain but there are several legends entwined with these caves and the local tribe of the area.

A very popular tourist destination, the borra caves are one of the must visit places in Visakhapatnam. According to some of the popular legends, when the caves were first discovered, they found a Shivalingam in its premises and turned it into a holy shrine with a temple outside of it.

### 8\. [Dolphin’s Nose] 

![Dolphin’s Nose, Vizag Top Places to Visit] 

A hill that resembles a dolphin’s nose in its shape is another popular tourist spot in Vizag called the Dolphin’s Nose. There is a lighthouse that guides sailing ships to the port and away from harsh regions of the sea.

It was used by the British before the independence of India as a military camp. It is a crucial location because quite a few important wars had been fought in and around the place, such as the war of the British and the French, famously known as the Battle of Visakhapatnam.

### 9\. [VUDA Park] 

![VUDA Park, Vizag Top Places to Visit] 

Located near RK beach, this is one of the popular places in Vizag that is definitely worth a visit. This is an urban park that was named after the then Chief Minister of Andhra Pradesh, N T Rama Rao at the time of its establishment. The park has recreational activities you can participate in along with an ice skating rink that has made its name all over the city.

### 10\. [Visakha Museum] 

![Visakha Museum, Vizag Top Places to Visit] 

If you want to explore more museums, you could visit the Vishakha Museum. The museum is dated back to about 260 BC, tracing through the history of the city and depicting the various aspects of culture that the city inhabits.

### 11\. [Victory at Sea Memorial] 

![Victory at Sea Memorial, Vizag Top Places to Visit] 

A glimpse into history, remembering the Indo-Pakistan war of 1971, this memorial was constructed in the year 1996. It was dedicated to the Indian Navy and Eastern Naval Command sailors. A moment of pride will imbibe in you in one visit to this memorial. It is located on Beach Road in Vizag.

### 12\. [Yarada Beach] 

![Yarada Beach, Vizag Top Places to Visit] 

Another popular attraction in Vizag, Yarada beach is one of the well maintained beaches of the city. The golden sand and pristine waters at the beach makes it one of best places in Vizag to spend some memorable moments with your loved ones.

The picturesque beauty of the surroundings and breathtaking views of sunrise and sunset will leave you awestruck.

### 13\. [Indira Gandhi Zoological Park] 

![Indira Gandhi Zoological Park, Vizag Top Places to Visit] 

The two best places to visit in a new city are its museums and zoological parks. Keeping this in mind, Visakhapatnam does not hesitate in providing you with one of the largest parks in the state with a variety of eight different species. It is a favourite destination for children and animal lovers of all ages.

An entirety of six hundred and twenty-five acres, the myriad creatures available in the park is sure to surprise you. It also has a special section for moths and butterflies. A mini train runs through the premises to give you an ultimate view of the places, making sure you don’t miss out on anything.

### 14\. [Matsyadarshini Aquarium] 

![Matsyadarshini Aquarium, Vizag Top Places to Visit] 

Marine life is an important aspect of Visakhapatnam. The MatsyaDarshini Aquarium is one such place where the depiction of this importance is displayed. Starting with Butterfly Fishes to Perch Duskys, you have it all. Exotic aquatic animals and marine invertebrates are major attractions.

### 15\. [Tenneti Park] 

![Tenneti Park, Vizag Top Places to Visit] 

Located on the Beach Road in Jodugullapalem, Tenneti Park is one of the oldest parks in Vizag. The ideal location of the park makes it favourite among tourists and locals, as it offers sea view on one side along with lush greenery all around. It is a perfect place for capturing some amazing clicks with your loved ones.

### 16\. [Bojjana Konda] 

![Bojjana Konda, Vizag Top Places to Visit] 

This is an ancient Buddhist heritage site dating back to the 4th century. It is situated on a hilltop and is a rock cave. It contains monolithic stupas made of rocks and relics and artefacts of the Buddhist influence over the village of Sankaram are still evident here.

### 17\. [Katiki Falls] 

![Katiki Falls, Vizag Top Places to Visit] 

Visakhapatnam is a land of endless surprises. The city is a hub for all kinds of travellers, providing something unique for everyone. Originating from the River Gosthani, Katiki Waterfalls is situated very close to Borra Caves. It is the ideal place to visit for all nature loving, adventurous travelers.

It requires a short trek until the waterfall through serpentine, rocky routes, adding thrill to the journey. There are pit stops to nourish you with sumptuous Bamboo Chicken and Coconut Water. Take a moment to elevate out of your worries, as you enjoy the sounds of the variety of birds singing, a bath in the cold river water or simple solitude.

### 18\. [Bavikonda Buddhist Complex] 

![Bavikonda Buddhist Complex, Vizag Top Places to Visit] 

Buddhism is a prominent aspect in the history of religious evolutions of India. It had a major effect in Visakhapatnam, especially in the dynasties that ruled the city over the years. A shred of evidence of it is the Bavikonda Buddhist Complex.

The wells in the Bavikonda Buddhist Complex were constructed to store rainwater. Buddhism is a religion that preaches calm, sobriety in life and tranquillity. There is no better place in Visakhapatnam to experience just that.

### 19\. [Kondakarla Bird Sanctuary] 

![Kondakarla Bird Sanctuary, Vizag Top Places to Visit] 

The lap of nature is nothing but wondrous and that is what you will find at this bird sanctuary. Surrounded by an endangered evergreen forest, unique birds and a clear lake, this is the ideal place to skip out to and truly unwind. The lake is flanked by coconut trees on one side and hills on the other, providing you with scenic beauty.

### 20\. [Kali Temple] 

![Kali Temple, Vizag Top Places to Visit] 

Located in RK Beach, is the famous Kali Temple. This is one of the few temples that receives the highest footfalls and darshans. Dedicated to Goddess Kali, the temple was built in the year 1984. Every year they celebrate Vijaydashami on a grand scale attracting visitors from all around the country and the world. This is one of the most prominent tourist attractions of Vizag.

Is Visakhapatnam worth visiting?

Renowned for its culture and fishing, discovering fun things to do in Visakhapatnam is easy. Make sure to add Rama Krishna Beach to your itinerary when here.

How do I spend a day in Visakhapatnam?

Rama Krishna Beach and Yarada Beach are two awesome places to begin your vacation adventures. Be sure to explore the retail and dining options in Visakhapatnam while you're here too.

What is the top tourist attraction in Visakhapatnam?

The top tourist attraction in Visakhapatnam is Rama Krishna Beach, so be sure to put it on your agenda.

Is three days enough to stay in Visakhapatnam?

Three days will give you enough time to check out some popular city sights, like Rama Krishna Beach, Yarada Beach and Kambalakonda Wildlife Sanctuary. The average stay in Visakhapatnam is three days. If you feel the urge to extend your break, Bheemili isn't far away.

Is Visakhapatnam a walkable city?

With vibrant neighborhoods and leading sights within walking distance of the city center, Visakhapatnam is a great destination to explore on foot. Wander between Kambalakonda Wildlife Sanctuary and Sri Venkateswara Temple. Then take a stroll through Railway New Colony, located 2 miles from downtown, and Dabagardens, which is roughly 2 miles away.

What are some activities to do in Visakhapatnam with kids?

Are there free things to do in Visakhapatnam?

[Visakhapatnam] is the largest City of the Indian state [Andhra Pradesh] and asll so called as **City of Destiny**[\[1\]] in the state of [Andhra Pradesh] compare to other cities [Visakhapatnam] has more tourist places and total tourism income of the state Visakhapatnam tourisam contributes 40% of the income.[\[2\]] Central Visakhapatnam has main tourist places like [RK Beach], [Beach Road], [Kailasagiri] and [APSRTC] runs special tourist buses for city tour.\[ _[citation needed] _\]

## Attractions

\[ [edit] \]

### Parks

\[ [edit] \]

Cycle track in VMRDA city Central Park

- [Kailasagiri] is a famous hill top park and one of the highest tourist visited place in the city.It spreads out to 380 acres with facilities like rope way, mini train and well connected buses are also available.[\[3\]] 

- [Dr.Y.S.Rajasekhara Reddy Central Park] is Central park located in city center [Dwaraka Nagar] area. This park is good connected with all parts of city and have musical fountain, Cycle track, Yoga center and running track [\[4\]] 

- [VUDA Park] this park is besides sea and one of the famous park in the state, this park is famous for skating track and boating
- [Mudasarlova Park] is Visakhapatnam's oldest park and have all most 100 years of history and area of 20 acres (8.1 ha). this park is adjusted with [Mudasarlova Reservoir] located with peaceful area [\[5\]] 

### Zoo park and Wildlife Sanctuary

\[ [edit] \]

Kambalakonda Landscape

- [Indira Gandhi Zoological Park] is the largest zoo park in the state of [Andhra Pradesh] spread over 625 acres (253 ha) with 850 animals and 75 species [\[6\]] 
- [Kambalakonda Wildlife Sanctuary] is Wildlife Sanctuary located near city and its lifeline of city because this Wildlife Sanctuary plays key role in Visakhapatnam city environment and this place famous for trekking.
- [Kondakarla Ava] is lake and birds Sanctuary this lake is destination for migrant birds and seasonal destination for birds, and it a good picnic spot for public [\[7\]] 

### Beaches

\[ [edit] \]

aerial view of Rushikonda Beach

- [RK Beach] Visakhapatnam is famous for beaches and RK Beach is popular and is situated in Central Visakhapatnam[\[8\]] 
- [Rushikonda Beach] is popular tourist destination and cleanest beach in the city, this beach revived [Blue Flag beach] and one of the rarest beach India achieved this feat [\[9\]] 
- [Yarada Beach] is one of the beautiful beach situated South visakhapatnam

### Religious places

\[ [edit] \]

view of Simhachalam Temple

- [Simhachalam temple] is one of the ancient and richest temple in [Andhra Pradesh] and its major religious center for [Hinduism] in Visakhapatnam [\[10\]] 
- [Kanaka Maha Lakshmi Temple] is located in [Burujupeta] in Old town area the people of Visakhapatnam adore the god as locale god especially Women Devotes
- [Sri Sampath Vinayagar Temple] is situated [Asilmetta] area
- [ISKCON Temple] located [Sagar Nagar] in North Visakhapatnam
- [Kali Temple] is one of the landmark in [RK Beach] and [Beach Road] 
- [Someswara Swamy Temple] is located in [Appikonda] its 11th century temple constructed by [Chola Kings] primary deity is lord [Shiva] [\[11\]] 

### Museums

\[ [edit] \]

view of Telugu Samskruthika Niketanam

- [Visakha Museum] is located in [Beach Road] and its treasure of ancient culture
- [Telugu Samskruthika Niketanam] is a Telugu Museum and its on [Kailasagiri] the main aim of this museum is to explore culture of Telugu people, [Telugu literature] and [Telugu language] [\[12\]] 
- [TU 142 Aircraft Museum] is aircraft museum at [RK Beach] 
- [INS Kursura Museum] is one of the first submarine museum in [Asia] located at [Beach road] [\[13\]]

[Home] [travel] Best Places to Visit in Visakhapatnam That Tell Timeless Tales

![Don’t Miss These Best Places to Visit in Visakhapatnam] Source: freepik

Visakhapatnam is an ideal getaway, offering experiences not only for families and friends but for everyone. **From serene natural settings to vibrant spots brimming with joy, the best places to visit in Visakhapatnam have something special for everyone.** This city sets the stage for unforgettable moments with those you cherish. Time slows down here, letting you savor every joyful moment together.

## **Ramakrishna Beach**

Ramakrishna Beach is not just a destination; it is a serenade from the sea, a serenade from the waves. The sands here seem to hum a lullaby, while the lighthouse stands tall, a poetic sentinel watching over the Bay of Bengal. Stroll along the shoreline, let the cool breeze carry away your worries, and feel the sacred calm of the nearby Kali Temple. As one of the best picnic spots in Vizag, this beach promises moments of peace, wrapped in the gentle cadence of the ocean.

## **Kailasagiri**

Sitting atop the city, Kailasagiri feels like a meeting point of earth and sky. The impressive figures of Shiva and Parvati, glowing against the backdrop of endless azure, inspire a feeling of spiritual peace. Ride the ropeway to this ethereal hilltop, where the Titanic Viewpoint offers vistas that steal the breath and the Toy Train takes you on a journey of childlike wonder. It is here, amidst nature’s splendor, that one feels the poetry of life unfold, making it one of the best tourist places in Visakhapatnam.

### **INS Kurusura Submarine Museum**

Like a steadfast guardian of the deep, the INS Kurusura Submarine rests along the sands of Ramakrishna Beach. Its steel frame, once submerged in mystery, now opens its doors to those who seek tales of valor. Walk through its narrow passages, feel the echoes of sailors’ lives, and let the stories of its 31 years of service stir your heart. This submarine, a marvel of history and engineering, is one of the top attractions in Visakhapatnam, honoring the silent courage beneath the waves.

### **Indira Gandhi Zoological Park**

_In the emerald cradle of the Eastern Ghats lies the [Indira Gandhi Zoological Park], a haven where life thrives in its wildest splendor._ Here, Bengal tigers stride with regal grace, colorful macaws chatter like old companions, and butterflies flit through sunlit air like nature’s confetti. Celebrated as one of the Best Places to Visit in Visakhapatnam, the park features sections dedicated to predators, plant-eaters, and feathered marvels, creating a symphony of life. For families and nature enthusiasts, it is a destination where every creature contributes to nature’s timeless masterpiece.

### **Dolphin’s Nose**

Dolphin’s Nose, a hill that curves gently like the marine mammal it resembles, offers views that awaken the soul. Standing atop this natural wonder, you can see the ocean stretching into infinity, its waves whispering secrets only the breeze can carry. Nearby, the Ross Hill Church, serene and sacred, provides a quiet refuge and stunning vistas of the city below. It is a place where nature’s grandeur and human devotion coexist in harmony, a true tourist attraction in Visakhapatnam.

**Also Read**: [Places to Visit in Araku] 

### **Rushikonda Beach**

A slice of untouched paradise, Rushikonda Beach boasts golden shores kissed by the gentle touch of sparkling green waves. Often called a ‘virgin beach,’ it is ideal for both serene relaxation and thrilling activities. Whether you are riding the waves on a jet ski or simply letting them lap at your feet, this beach feels like a song composed by nature. Among the [Best Places to Visit in Visakhapatnam], Rushikonda Beach stands out, where every moment is a verse of peace and wonder.

### **Thotlakonda**

At Thotlakonda, the ruins of a Buddhist monastery rest quietly on a hill overlooking the sea, as if meditating on the passage of time. Here, monks once found solace, their chants merging with the rhythm of the waves below. The remnants of their lives, stone pathways, ancient stupas, and meditation halls tell stories of simplicity and spiritual devotion. This serene site, one of the top sightseeing places in Visakhapatnam, invites you to pause, breathe, and listen to the echoes of ancient wisdom.

### **Matsyadarshini Aquarium**

Step into Matsyadarshini Aquarium, and you step into a world of shimmering marvels. Beneath the shimmering glass, fish in brilliant hues and unique shapes weave through the water, their movements a poetic display of harmony and grace. Among them, the Long Horn Cow Fish, Peacock Wrasse, and Red Squirrel Fish stand out as enchanting spectacles of this aquatic realm. Established in 1994, this aquarium is recognized as one of the Best Places to Visit in Visakhapatnam, serving as a vibrant showcase of the treasures beneath the waves, a must see for those yearning for a marine adventure.

### **VUDA Park**

VUDA Park, spread over 55 acres of greenery, is where families gather, children laugh, and nature thrives. The musical fountain here dances to its own rhythm, while the international-standard skating rink invites both young and old to glide into moments of joy. Shady trees, vibrant flowers, and open spaces make it one of the best places to visit in Vizag for a day of relaxation and delight. It is a place where every corner hums with the song of life.

### **Red Sand Hills**

The Red Sand Hills, known as Erra Matti Dibbalu, are a remarkable display of time’s patient artistry. Formed over 12,000 years, these fiery dunes glow with a hue that seems borrowed from the setting sun. Walking here feels like stepping into a masterpiece crafted by nature herself, where every footstep becomes part of the story. Located along the Visakhapatnam-Bheemunipatnam Beach Road, these hills are a haven for dreamers and photographers alike, making them one of the Best Places to Visit in Visakhapatnam for an unforgettable experience.

##### Destination

# Visakhapatnam

HighlightsGallery

Visakhapatnam, also known locally as Vizag, is the largest city in [Andhra Pradesh]. It is primarily an industrial port city, but also attracts tourists due to its beautiful sandy beaches, well-built and maintained parks, Buddhist remnant sites, and nearby scenic areas, such as the Araku Valley.

The port of Visakhapatnam is famous for being home to the oldest shipyard in all of India. Visakhapatnam's history stretches back to the 6th century BCE when it was considered a part of the Kalinga Kingdom and later ruled by the Vengi, the Pallava, and Eastern Ganga dynasties. Conquered by the Mughals in the 16th century CE, European powers eventually set up trading interests in the city, and by the end of the 18th century, it had come under French rule. It came under British rule in 1804 and it remained a British colony until India's independence in 1947.

Located a short distance away from Visakhapatnam, Araku Valley is a hill station replete with gushing waterfalls, crystal clear streams, and lush green gardens. Often touted as "The Goa of the East", Vizag is replete with many pristine beaches along its coastline, with the most famous one being Yarada Beach. Yarada beach is a stunning site surrounded by majestic hills on three sides, and by the Bay of Bengal on the other side.

For history enthusiasts, the Borra Caves are one of the best tourist attractions that Vizag has to offer for you. These caves are over thousands of years old and are one of the biggest among all the caves found in India. The Katiki Waterfalls, situated a short distance away from the Borra Caves, is a gushing waterfall that measures around 50 feet in height and is surrounded by the exuberant greenery of the valley.

Being a coastal region, the popular cuisine of Visakhapatnam includes seafood, as well as traditional South Indian cuisine.

# Must-Visit Tourist Spots in Visakhapatnam

Discover the top tourist spots in Visakhapatnam, from stunning beaches and historical landmarks to cultural attractions and nature escapes. Plan your visit to explore the best of Vizag.

20 November 2024

4 minutes

- [Top things to do] 

Visitors who are hungry for a taste of Indian history need look no further than Visakhapatnam, often known as ‘Vizag’, one of the largest metropolitan areas in India and one of the country’s oldest port cities turned tourist hotspot.

Located on the eastern coast of India, this bustling city offers an enticing blend of scenic landscapes, ancient history, and rich cultural heritage. As well as housing India’s oldest shipyard, this South Indian destination is famed for its scenic beaches, clear blue waters, and lush green hills.

With a mix of relaxed beachside retreats and fascinating historic sites, Vizag offers a unique experience that attracts tourists from all over the world.

## Beaches in Visakhapatnam

It’s impossible to talk about the best places to visit in Visakhapatnam without diving into the many astounding beaches in Visakhapatnam. Famed for their white sands and crystal blue seas, Vizag beaches are iconically clean and tend to be crowd-free. Any Vizag beach is guaranteed to impress, but a few of the best ones to consider during your stay include:

### Ramakrishna Beach

Ramakrishna Beach, also known as RK Beach, is located on the east coast of the Bay of Bengal, just 18 km from Visakhapatnam International Airport. The beach gains its name from the nearby Ramakrishna Mission ashram and is within close proximity to other religious icons like Kali Temple. Though swimming is prohibited due to sea pollution, this picturesque beach remains a favourite spot for locals and tourists alike, offering beautiful ocean views and a bustling atmosphere. Visitors can enjoy a stroll along the pedestrian promenade or take a trip to nearby MatsyaDarshini Aquarium, home to aquatic wonders like Electric Rays and Sea Anemones.
**Location:** RK Beach, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

### Rushikonda Beach

Visitors keen to dive right into the sea should look no further than Rushikonda Beach. Known for its clear blue waters, this beach is a favourite for water sports like surfing, scuba diving, and banana boats. Surrounded by lush palm forests and rocky outcrops, Rushikonda Beach is the ideal destination for adventurers and thrill-seekers. The nearby [Novotel Visakhapatnam Varun Beach] hotel is a fantastic place to stay if you plan on spending your days here.
**Location:** Rushikonda, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

### Lawson’s Bay Beach

If relaxation is more your style, Lawson’s Bay Beach, also known as Pedawaltair Beach, offers sprawling golden sands and one of the best views of the Bay of Bengal. Only 8-9 km from Vizag city centre, it remains one of the best-hidden gems for swimming, sunbathing, and long beach walks. With its quieter atmosphere and pristine sands, Lawson’s Bay is perfect for unwinding away from the city’s hustle and bustle.
**Location:** Pedda Waltair, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

### Yarada Beach

Yarada Beach, considered one of the most beautiful beaches in Vizag, is flanked by lush greenery and banana and coconut plantations. Its untouched natural beauty and scenic views of the Arabian Sea make it a peaceful retreat, far from the crowds. Close to Dolphin’s Nose, this beach offers stunning views. Enjoy your own slice of paradise with walks along the shoreline or a picnic in solitude while admiring the crystal-clear waters.
**Location:** Yarada, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

## Visakhapatnam Historical Sites

As one of India’s oldest port cities, Visakhapatnam has a rich history. Exploring Vizag’s historic sites offers a glimpse into its storied past, from ancient maritime activities to significant colonial landmarks.

### Kurusura Submarine Museum

The VMRDA INS Kurusura Submarine Museum is the first of its kind in South Asia and is housed in a real Soviet-built I-641 class submarine. Having served in the Indian Navy for 31 years, this submarine is now a museum displaying artefacts, photographs, and more, chronicling the evolution of submarines. A tour of the museum offers a rare opportunity to explore life inside a submarine and gain insights into India’s naval history.
**Location:** RK Beach, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

### Kailasagiri

Kailasagiri Park, spanning 100 acres of Vizag hilltops, is home to 40-foot-tall statues of Shiva and Parvathi. The park offers panoramic views of the city, accessible via road or a cable car system, making it a must-visit attraction in Visakhapatnam. Visitors can explore beautifully landscaped gardens, walk along scenic trails, and take in breathtaking city and ocean views from this elevated vantage point.
**Location:** Kailasagiri, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

## Cultural Attractions in Visakhapatnam

### Sri Varaha Lakshmi Narasimha Temple

The Sri Varaha Lakshmi Narasimha Temple, perched on Kailasa hilltop, dates back to the 11th century and is known for its stunning iconography of Lord Vishnu. The temple is a significant cultural landmark in Vizag, drawing many visitors, especially during the Chandanotsavam Festival. With its intricate sculptures and rich heritage, this temple is both a spiritual and cultural highlight of the region.
**Location:** Simhachalam, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

### Indira Gandhi Zoological Park

Indira Gandhi Zoological Park is one of India’s largest zoos, stretching across 625 acres and housing 87 species, including Bengal tigers and Indian elephants. The park’s naturalistic enclosures provide a great experience for visitors of all ages, and its dedication to conservation adds an educational element to the visit. With its emphasis on natural enclosures and conservation education, this is a great spot for animal lovers.
**Location:** Kambalakonda, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

## Visakhapatnam Nature Spots

### Borra Caves and Araku Valley

Located 114 km from Visakhapatnam, Araku Valley is a stunning hill station known for its coffee plantations and waterfalls. The lush greenery and cool climate make it a popular escape from the city’s warmth. The nearby Borra Caves, among the largest in India, offer fascinating insights into the region's geological and tribal history. These limestone caves, with stalactites and stalagmites, provide a unique underground experience enhanced by stories of ancient tribes that once inhabited the area.
**Location:** Borra, Araku Valley, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

## Shopping in Visakhapatnam

### Jagadamba Junction

As one of Vizag’s busiest spots, Jagadamba Junction is home to Jagadamba Centre, a shopping hub where visitors can buy authentic handicrafts and fashionable sarees. Here, visitors can also indulge in local street food, with popular vendors offering regional delicacies at Dastreet’s multi-cuisine stalls.
**Location:** Jagadamba Junction, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

### CMR Central Mall

CMR Central Mall in Maddilapalem is the largest mall in Vizag, featuring five floors of shopping opportunities, including popular brands like Max and B’Ethnic, and familiar food chains like McDonald’s and Pizza Hut. The mall’s vibrant atmosphere, complete with cinema and games, offers a blend of local culture and modern convenience for visitors of all ages.
**Location:** Maddilapalem, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh
