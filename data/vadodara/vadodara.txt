 A city of lively celebrations and joyous festivities 
 Vadodara is the place to be during festivals. The festival of Uttarayan, which marks the beginning of the sun’s journey northwards, is celebrated with great enthusiasm by flying colourful kites in Gujarat. To add to the festive spirit, the Gujarat government hosts a week-long International Kite Festival in various cities across the state. 
 Navratri, a nine-day long festival dedicated to worshipping Goddess Durga, celebrated with immense fervour and devotion. Magnificent idols of Goddess Durga are placed in spacious compounds, where people of all ages come together to dance the Garba, a traditional folk dance of Gujarat. From dusk till midnight, the air reverberates with the joyous sound of people clapping, tapping Dandiya sticks, and twirling to the rhythmic beats of the Garba. 
 And if you're visiting Vadodara during Navratri, make sure you join the Garba festival, the perfect way to experience the rich culture and tradition. 
 Timeless craftmanship and artistic treasures 
 Vadodara is a city that has a history of patronizing the arts. While all the artisans are not based in the city, there are several outlets that provide a view the products of these amazing craftspeople. A wide range of traditional craft including beadwork, woodcarving, bandhini fabric, Pithora paintings, silver jewellery, Patola sarees and Sankheda lacquer work await you. 
 The magnificent beadwork of Gujarat, made by deftly stringing together multicoloured and multi-styled bead, is one of the most popular souvenirs from Vadodara. You can find a plethora of items made with this exquisite art form, ranging from elegant home décor to fashionable accessories like necklaces, bangles and earrings. 
 Carved wooden delights such as tea pots, bowls, table lamps, even toys make a timeless memory of this ubiquitous art form in Gujarat, found everywhere from intricate palatial carvings to utilitarian items. 
 The beauty of Bandhini is sure to captivate. Choose from an endless variety of dupattas, saris, scarves, dresses or simply fabric in Bandhani. The stunning textile pattern is famous worldwide for its vibrant colours, fine crinkled texture, and infinite tiny specks, created painstakingly by pinching, tying, and resist-dyeing the fabric. 
 Pithora paintings, the stunning artwork created by the talented Rathwa tribes of central Gujarat traditionally depict the main deity, Pithora, along with a procession that displays his numerous accomplishments. These vibrant paintings, often seen as wall hangings, are believed to bring peace, prosperity, and happiness to homes. And of course, Kutch's silver jewellery id a true testament to the rich cultural heritage of the region. Men and women alike adorn themselves with these stunning pieces, including bracelets, necklaces, nose rings, earrings, finger rings, and even anklets. Bhuj's silver work extends beyond traditional jewellery to encompass a range of lifestyle products, including dining sets, tea pots, cups, cutlery, bowls, candle stands and more. 
 Explore the luxurious world of Patola, the exquisite double ikat woven sari crafted in the historic city of Patan, Gujarat, India. These silk saris have a rich legacy, with the word Patola possibly originating from the Sanskrit word “Pattakula”, meaning a silk fabric. This legendary heritage of Indian textiles from North Gujarat was once exclusively reserved for the royal and aristocratic families, making it an incredibly exclusive and highly valued fabric. The intricate weaving process involves dyeing both the warp and weft threads to create a complex and beautiful pattern, making it one of the most complex textile-weaving techniques in the world. Each Patola sari is a masterpiece, a testament to the skill and dedication of the artisans who create them. Experience the luxury of Patola for yourself and discover the timeless elegance of this iconic Indian textile. 
 Just 50 km away from Vadodara, discover the exquisite craft of lacquer work in Sankheda. The artisans of the Kharadi Suthars community specialize in crafting intricate and colourful wooden furniture using traditional woodworking, woodturning, and hand painting techniques. These skilled artisans prefer using handheld tools and straightforward methods to create masterpieces that showcase their brilliant craftsmanship. The roots of this art form date back to the 1800s, making it a long-standing tradition in the region. But that's not all that makes Sankheda special. The town has a rich history, with stone relics from the Stone Age and five ancient temples, believed to have been built during the period of the Mahabharata. These relics provide a glimpse into the past and suggest that the Pandavas might have even resided there for a while! 
 A unique and diverse shopping scene 
 Vadodara is a city that perfectly blends traditional charm with modernity. Explore the charm of its bustling local markets to discover exquisite local handicrafts and textiles. For those who prefer a more modern shopping experience, Vadodara also offers several malls where you can find national and international brands while enjoying modern amenities. From high street shopping to authentic local craft, you would be spoilt for choice in Vadodara. 
 Among its authentic local shopping bazaars is the bustling Teen Darwaza Bazaar. This is the ultimate destination for ethnic wear, where you will find a dazzling array of bandhani sarees, suits, ghagra cholis, and dress materials to choose from. The market is teeming with a variety of shops offering an endless selection of ethnic wear, so you can find the perfect attire that reflects your style and taste. Not only that, but you can also indulge in shopping for beautiful silver ornaments at a bargain price. Immerse yourself in the authentic culture and traditions of Vadodara by exploring this shopping haven that promises an unforgettable shopping experience! The colourful Madar Bazar is home to a treasure of sarees, chaniya choli, and salwar suits, all at unbeatable prices. And that's not all – be sure to check out the stunning collection of traditional paintings on display, each one created by a talented local artist and capturing the rich tradition of the region. The diverse shopping scene at Mandvi Market is a must-visit destination for both locals and tourists. Mandvi Market is renowned for its exquisite silver jewellery, perfect for accessorizing any outfit. Also on offer are unique handicrafts here that add character to any home décor. Get your hands on the finest ethnic footwear and locally made wall hangings, each of which adds a touch of cultural authenticity to your shopping experience. 
 If you prefer high street retail, experience the best of shopping, dining, and entertainment all under one roof at Vadodara’s variety of malls, including Inorbit Mall, EVA the Mall, Seven Seas Mall and many more. Get ready to indulge in a world-class shopping experience with a vast selection of international and domestic brands across fashion, lifestyle, and beauty.  A true foodie paradise 
 Looking for a culinary adventure? Vadodara’s got you covered, boasting a tantalizing mix of mouth-watering traditional dishes, street food, and the latest and greatest international restaurants. Satisfy your cravings with a stroll through the city's bustling food markets, where the aromas of fragrant spices and sizzling delicacies entice your taste buds. And when you're ready for a sit-down meal, choose from a diverse range of dining options that cater to every palate and preference. Come discover the culinary delights of Vadodara and experience a food lover's paradise like no other! 
 No better cuisine to start with than the traditional Gujarati thali, where sweet and salty flavours blend seamlessly to create a culinary masterpiece. A feast for the senses, featuring a delectable array of dishes that typically includes three flavourful vegetable dishes, a sprouted pulse dish like ugadyala or dry dal, fragrant rice, a tangy kadhi, another dal, a mouth-watering sweet dish or mithai, freshly made rotis, crispy pooris, crunchy papad, along with refreshing salad, chutney, and pickle. The Gujarati cuisine is a true celebration of seasonal ingredients, with the spices used in the recipes changing to suit the weather, while salt, jaggery, and lemon create a perfectly balanced taste. 
 Like a snack instead? Get ready to tantalize your taste buds with the world-famous Sev Usal in Vadodara! This sweet and savoury mouth-watering dish, prepared with dried green peas and a blend of spicy and aromatic ingredients that will leave you craving for more. What makes Sev Usal so special is the Tari, prepared using an abundance of garlic and a tadka of red chilli powder, giving it a deliciously fiery kick. Enjoy it with some chopped onions, spring onions, loads of crispy sev! You will also find a wide variety of popular snacks like egg omelettes, farsan and jalebi, pav bhaji and Kachori, and much more in Vadodara.  Natural havens of beauty 
 Natural beauty in Vadodara walks hand in hand with the city’s exquisite manmade structures. From sprawling city parks to scenic lakes, there is no dearth of places for nature lovers in the city. 
 Discover the wonders of Sayaji Baug, the sprawling green haven also known as Kamati Baug. This iconic park, gifted by Maharaja Sayajirao, boasts over 98 species of trees, a large zoo housing Asiatic lions, a health museum, and a planetarium. Kids will love the toy train and flowerbed clock while history buffs can explore the famous Baroda Museum and Picture Gallery. With its massive 113-acre expanse, there's something for everyone at Sayaji Baug, making it the largest public garden in the western region of India. So why wait? Come and immerse yourself in the beauty of this treasured gem. 
 Experience the magic of Sursagar Lake! Located in the heart of the city, it is the perfect spot to unwind and find tranquillity within the bustling metropolis. The lake is surrounded by a concrete wall that offers visitors a perfect vantage point. Rebuilt in the 18th century with stone banks and masonry, Sursagar Lake is a breath-taking sight that shines under the moonlight. You can enjoy a leisurely boat ride or take a casual stroll in its pleasant atmosphere. At the center of the lake stands a magnificent 120-foot-tall statue of Lord Shiva known as Sarveshwar Mahadev that is beautifully decorated with lights during festivals like Shivratri. 
 Discover the hidden gem of Wadhwana, a small village just 50 kms from Vadodara. Immerse yourself in the serene beauty of the vast lake with a 2 km radius, surrounded by watchtowers, offering picturesque views of the surrounding landscapes. Knowledgeable forest department personnel guide visitors and share insights about the local flora and fauna. What’s more, visitors can enjoy stargazing with the high-quality Celestron telescope provided. Wadhwana is a perfect destination to indulge in nature and enjoy a rejuvenating environment. 
 Vadodara’s wildlife treasures are unique to the region and boast an impressive diverse range of flora and fauna. Explore the breath-taking Ratanmahal Sloth Bear Sanctuary, home to sloth bears and a variety of exotic birds. Take a trek through the dense forests of the Jambughoda Wildlife Sanctuary, home to exotic leopards and wild boars. With its unique landscapes and exotic wildlife, Vadodara is a paradise for nature lovers!

When to visit

Located in western–central India, with the deserts of Rajasthan and Gujarat to the north and west, Vadodara has a hot climate for much of the year. Most people will find winter (December–February) the coolest and most pleasant time to visit, although October, November, and March are generally pleasant, too. The summer and monsoon bring high temperatures and even higher humidity, so avoid visiting between April and September.

Getting around

Travelers who only stay here for a day or two usually find hiring a car and driver is the best way to get around Vadodara. You can also hail taxis or auto-rickshaws as needed, but be prepared to negotiate a price. Local buses are OK for traveling beyond the city—such as to Champaner–Pavadagh–if you’re on a budget, but rather slow, uncomfortable, and crowded for city travel.

Traveler tips

Aim to spend at least half a day at the Laxmi Vilas Palace and the connected Maharaja Fatehsingh Museum, a private art collection of the former rulers of Vadodara, the Gaekwad family. Although visitors can only enter part of the palace as it’s still inhabited by the family, there’s a lot to see, and the grounds and exterior views are beautiful.

October through March are the best months to visit Vadodara in western India, as the weather is warm to hot but dry and comfortable. This is peak travel season for most of India. The summer and monsoon months (April–September) can be very uncomfortable due to the heat and humidity.

**Q:** Is Vadodara safe at night?

**A:**

Yes, Vadodara is generally safe at night. However, the city has more than 2 million people, so taking precautions is important. Avoid walking around alone at night—especially if you’re a woman—and have a plan to get back to your accommodations if you go out at night.

If it has been some time that you have not taken the elderly members of your family somewhere, then you can plan Dakor as your next travel destination with the entire family. ![Kabirwad] 

Kabirwad is an island in the River Narmada. The island was named after the 18th century poet Kabir. You will need to take a boat ride to reach the island. The island has lush greenery and a banyan tree which is believed to be more than 100 years old.

**Distance from Vadodara:** 80 km

![Champaner] 

The hills of Champaner and Pavagadh are often frequented by tourists. The destination is mostly popular for ruins of old monuments and forts. **Distance from Vadodara:** 49 km

Toranmal is different from all the places to visit near Vadodara. Situated in the Satpura range, it is a hill station which is blanketed by verdant greenery. The lake looks spectacular during the flowering season, which starts around June and remains till mid August. If you make any plan to visit this place during the peak flowering season, make sure your camera is working fine.

**Distance from Vadodara:** 304 km

Located between Kapur Talao and Munj Talao, the Jahaz Mahal resembles a ship.

**Distance from Vadodara:** 315 km
