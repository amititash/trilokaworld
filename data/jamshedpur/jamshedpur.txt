Dimna Lake is a beautiful artificial lake located on the outskirts of Jamshedpur in the Indian state of Jharkhand. The lake is a popular destination for tourists exploring the region's natural beauty and outdoor activities. The lake was created in 1958 by damming the Subarnarekha River and is spread over approximately 27 square kilometres. Lush green forests surround the lake and offer breathtaking landscape views. The lake's crystal-clear waters reflect the greenery, creating a picturesque setting that will take your breath away. Dimna Lake is a beautiful destination and a popular spot for outdoor activities. Visitors can indulge in boating, kayaking, fishing, and swimming. The lake also offers a range of adventure activities, such as trekking, camping, and rock climbing.

Dimna Lake, located near Jamshedpur in Jharkhand, India, is a serene and picturesque spot that attracts both locals and tourists. Created as a reservoir by Tata Steel, the lake is nestled in the foothills of the Dalma Hills, offering a perfect blend of natural beauty and tranquility.
One of the most striking features of Dimna Lake is its scenic beauty. The calm, clear waters of the lake reflect the surrounding greenery and hills, creating a mesmerizing landscape that is ideal for photography and nature walks. The area is well-maintained, with clean surroundings and pathways, making it a pleasant spot for picnics and leisurely strolls.
For those who enjoy outdoor activities, Dimna Lake offers various options. Boating is a popular activity here, allowing visitors to paddle across the serene waters and take in the breathtaking views. Additionally, the nearby Dalma Wildlife Sanctuary provides opportunities for trekking and wildlife spotting, adding an adventurous element to the visit.
The lake's location makes it an ideal getaway from the hustle and bustle of city life. It's a perfect spot for a day trip or a weekend retreat, offering a peaceful environment to relax and rejuvenate. The mornings and evenings are particularly beautiful, with the sunrise and sunset casting a golden hue over the lake, making it a delightful experience for nature lovers.
One of the highlights of Dimna Lake is its accessibility. It is well-connected by road and is just a short drive from Jamshedpur, making it an easy destination for a quick escape. The infrastructure around the lake, including parking facilities and small eateries, adds to the convenience for visitors.
Overall, Dimna Lake is a gem in Jharkhand’s landscape, offering a perfect mix of natural beauty, recreational activities, and tranquility. It is a must-visit for anyone looking to experience the serene side of Jamshedpur and its surrounding natural beauty.

Hudco Lake, located in Jamshedpur, is a popular tourist spot known for its artificial waterfalls and picturesque park. Rankini Mandir, located about 35 km from Tatanagar Railway Station, is a revered temple dedicated to Goddess Rankini, believed to be an incarnation of Goddess Kali. The temple holds immense historical and cultural significance, especially among the Bhumij tribes, who originally worshipped the deity as a stone idol. Over time, the worship evolved into that of Goddess Durga, likely due to the influence of the Dhalbhum kings.
The current structure of the temple is around 70 years old, built in the 1950s. It is managed by a trust formed in 1954. Alongside the main temple, there are shrines dedicated to Lord Ganesha and Lord Shiva, adding to the spiritual ambiance of the place. A Hari Mandir, dedicated to Radha-Gobind, has also been constructed nearby.
Rankini Mandir continues to be a place of deep faith, attracting both tribal and non-tribal devotees. The temple’s location on the Hata-Jadugora state highway, near Rohinibera village, makes it accessible for visitors.
For those interested in history, spirituality, and local legends, Rankini Mandir is a must-visit destination that offers a glimpse into the region’s rich traditions and deep-rooted beliefs.
