[NJP - a messy gateway to Darjeeling!] 






When dropped at NJP, owing to extensive “upgradation” work to the station, use the left path into the station and up the overhead bridge. I made the mistake of walking towards the right and as such found myself walking through a selection of shops. This is very poorly signposted and for a UNESCO world heritage site, I still had not seen a single mention of the toy train. Sighting the tracks from the right overhead bridge did not even give me confidence I was in the right place as all I saw were standard gauge tracks. No lifts or escalators in sight so it's up and down some flights of stairs - made harder with bags.
I walked up to an IR officer to enquire and I had a rattle of Hindi response - which I had to play back in English (for I speak no Hindi) coupled with hand gestures. I was to proceed down the platform, and to the left and up the overhead bridge to the toy train platform; and so I went on. This time an escalator was present which made my ascent somewhat easier.
Found the toy train platform and got the train to Darjeeling. NJP is a mess at present and I hope that after the upgrades, things will be better!

from Siliguri Junction, there are morning buses (7:40, 8:20, ...) going to Kalimpong, you board one of them and they drop you off in front of safari. 50 rs, ~30 mins
how to get back:
there are white shared minibuses waiting in front of the safari. You take one of them and they will drop you off at intersection of Hill Card Rd & Sevoke Rd. 50 rs, ~30 min

## A hidden gem in the foothills of the Himalayas

Imagine a place where the majestic Himalayas rise from the plains, their snow-capped peaks piercing the azure sky. A place where the gentle murmur of the Mahananda River mingles with the rhythmic thrum of bustling bazaars. A place brimming with natural beauty, and pulsating with a vibrant cultural heart. This is Siliguri. The captivating gateway to Northeast India, waiting to be explored by you.

Siliguri's charm is woven into its poetic name, which means a stack of pebbles, alluding to the abundant boulders that once dotted the banks of the Mahananda River that flows gracefully through the heart of the city. A dense forest for centuries, Siliguri is a small yet lively city today famous for its 3 Ts – tea, timber and tourism! With its prime location in the state of West Bengal, the city serves as the starting point for journeys to enchanting destinations like Darjeeling and Sikkim.

Come, let’s discover Siliguri and embark on an offbeat expedition through this gateway to the extraordinary.

![mahananda-wildlife-sanctuary-siliguri-city-body] 

##### Mahananda Wildlife Sanctuary, Siliguri

#### Unravel the layers of time

Siliguri isn't just a gateway to the Himalayas, it's a treasure trove waiting to be explored. Immerse yourself in the colonial charm of the [Darjeeling Himalayan Railway], fondly known as the Toy Train. This UNESCO World Heritage Site chugs along breath-taking vistas, offering a delightful journey back in time. It’s an experience you just can’t miss!

Take a stroll across the magnificent [Coronation Bridge], an architectural masterpiece that marks King George VI and Queen Elizabeth’s coronation. Imagine standing on the bridge, the cool mountain air brushing your face as you gaze down at the sparkling blue waters winding their way through the valley below. It's a truly panoramic vista that will leave you breathless.

For a deeper dive into Siliguri's heritage, head to the Trishakti Heritage Complex near the city. Uncover the stirring tales of the Trishakti Corps warriors, and honour the fallen heroes who fought in World War II at their memorial, and delve into their remarkable history through captivating artefacts at the museum.

#### Blessed by nature

Siliguri boasts a surprising array of natural wonders. Picture yourself strolling amidst emerald tea gardens, their gentle slopes carpeted with fragrant green leaves. Explore Sepoy Dhura, a charming village enveloped by these lush plantations, and witness the fascinating process of tea plucking first-hand. Breathe in the fresh mountain air, explore the region’s tea culture and soak in the panoramic views – a perfect escape from the city's bustle.

For an exciting wildlife encounter, head to the [Mahananda Wildlife Sanctuary.] Keep your eyes glued for elephants, deer, and a dazzling variety of birds as you explore this haven for nature lovers. You can also visit the Bengal Safari Park to see Bengal tigers, Asiatic black bears, and other exotic animals up close. Explore the park through a thrilling safari ride, choosing from different options to suit your preference.

#### A haven for the soul

If you’re seeking spiritual solace, Siliguri offers a chance to cultivate inner peace with its tranquil havens. On the banks of Teesta River, ascend the picturesque hills overlooking Kanchenjunga to visit the Sevoke Kali Mandir, the oldest temple in the city. Dedicated to Goddess Kali, the temple is revered by newlyweds for blessings!

Breathe in the serenity at the [Salugara Monastery], where a majestic 100-foot stupa dominates the skyline, housing revered Buddhist relics within its sacred walls. Or immerse yourself in the vibrant architecture and tranquil meditation halls of the Ewam India Buddhist Monastery where lush gardens and the teachings of Buddha await!

Visit the Lokenath Baba Temple, a tribute to the esteemed Bengal saint. Marvel at the extraordinary split idol portraying Lord Shiva and Lokenath Baba united in divine harmony. Discover tranquillity at ISKCON Temple, a sanctuary of peace amidst the hustle and bustle, and pay homage at the shrines dedicated to Lord Krishna and Radha Rani.

#### A feast for the senses

Siliguri isn't just about sights; it's a feast for all senses. Inhale the intoxicating aroma of freshly brewed Darjeeling tea, a world-renowned delicacy born in the nearby hills. This vibrant city boasts a unique culinary scene influenced by its diverse heritage. Imagine indulging in a blend of Indian, Nepalese, and Bengali flavours. Don't miss local specialities like crispy sel roti (rice doughnuts), hearty thukpa (Tibetan noodle soup), and savoury phaley (fried lentil dumplings).

Wander through the Hong Kong Market, a famous bazaar and a kaleidoscope of colours and sounds. Here, you can find everything from local handicrafts and Tibetan trinkets to international brands and foreign apparel. Head to Mahabirsthan, renowned for its beautiful fabrics at incredibly reasonable prices or go to Bidhan Market for shawls, fabrics, bags, and carpets. For those seeking unique keepsakes, don't miss Seth Srilal Market, a treasure trove of exquisite silver jewellery.

For curious minds, a visit to the North Bengal Science Centre, just a few kilometres from Siliguri, is a must. From a science park to a digital planetarium, there's plenty to spark your curiosity.

#### Adventure beckons

Siliguri serves as the perfect base camp for your Himalayan adventure. Darjeeling, the ‘Queen of Hills,’ is a scenic journey away, offering breath-taking views of Mount Kanchenjunga, the world's third-highest peak. Embark on a thrilling trek through the verdant Singalila National Park, home to diverse flora and fauna. Or take a white-water rafting trip on the Teesta River, an adrenaline-pumping experience that will leave you breathless.

#### Go beyond the gateway

Siliguri is a fantastic starting point, but don't let your adventure end there! Venture out to discover North Bengal's captivating hidden gems. Just 55 km away, Mirik awaits with its lush tea gardens and emerald green lake, Sumendu (also known as Mirik Jhil). Imagine peaceful strolls along the vibrant waterfront, breathing in the crisp mountain air.

Kurseong, a hill station, boasts breath-taking scenery and a serene atmosphere. This haven for artists, located 51 km from Siliguri, will surely inspire you with its stunning beauty. And for a dose of vibrant ecology, rich history, and diverse culture, head to Sukna. This charming town, just 12 km away, sits at the edge of the Mahananda Wildlife Sanctuary and is easily accessible by auto-rickshaw.

Escape to Kalimpong, the ‘land of kings', with its epic Kanchenjunga views and verdant valleys. Venture further into the heart of Sikkim and discover Gangtok, the capital city. This pristine hill station, situated amidst mountains and clouds, serves as a haven for nature lovers and adventure seekers. So, add these hidden gems to your itinerary and transform your Siliguri trip into an unforgettable exploration!

#### Siliguri awaits

Siliguri isn't a city to be rushed. Take your time, wander its streets, strike up conversations with friendly locals, and discover its hidden corners. So, pack your bags, as the place where the magic of the Himalayas meets the warmth of the plains, awaits you.

The ideal time to visit Siliguri is between November and February when you can experience its delightful winter!

# Siliguri

Highlights

Situated at the foothills of the Himalayas in the plains of [Darjeeling District] by the side of River Mahananda, is Siliguri- The Gateway to North East. It is the 2nd largest city in [West Bengal]. The strategic location of the city makes sure that travelers to the Northeast have to pass through Siliguri. It is also the commercial hub of the region.

Siliguri is popular for three T's- tea, timber, and tourism.

Siliguri lies 35 kilometers away from its twin city, Jalpaiguri. Darjeeling Himalayan Railway (toy train) which runs between New Jalpaiguri and Darjeeling, was built between 1879 and 1881. This rail route is designated as UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Bagdogra Airport is an international airport located towards the west of Siliguri city. This airport is a major transport hub in the region with flights connecting [Kolkata], [New Delhi], [Mumbai], [Chennai], Bangalore, Hyderabad, Ahmedabad, Guwahati, and Dibrugarh, and has international connectivity with Paro and Bangkok.

## Siliguri Sightseeing Places

Siliguri, also known to be "the gateway of North East India" has all the proportions to bewitch its visitors. Abundant with myriad sightseeing places, you can blindly include Siliguri in your next holiday checklist. Here are some of the places to see in Siliguri.

### Sed-Gyued Monastery

One of the popular monasteries is The Sed-Gyued Monastery, Salugara. It is a sacred Buddhist destination near Siliguri. Sed-Gyued Monastery is located in Salugara, belonging to the Gelugpa. It was inaugurated by the Dalai Lama in November 2001. There is also a researcher center for Buddhist studies. It is famous for its 100ft tall stupa and certainly one of the best tourist attractions in Siliguri. Travelers can enjoy the view of the monastery from inside and outside. For those who seek inner peace, this Buddhist religious site is certainly a must-visit.

### North Bengal Wild Animals Park

Also known as Bengal Safari Park, North Bengal Wild Animals Park is one of the reasons for thriving tourism in Siliguri. Blessed with rich flora and fauna, North Bengal Wild Animals Parks offers tourists to get closer to wild animals. Some of the wild animals you will get to see here are Tigers, leopards, crocodiles, Asiatic Black Bear, etc. Moreover, birds like Red Jungle Fowl, Kingfisher, Hornbill, dove, etc can also be seen here. Jeep safari can be availed here at a minimal price of rupees 200 (Ticket price is subject to change).

### Suryasen Park

One of the most happening places in Siliguri is Suryasen Park. It is located within walking distance from International Market. The park offers a boat ride and lets the visitors take a stroll in the well-manicured garden and if you are tired from all the shopping you can take a tour of the whole park on the lookalike toy train. The park is mostly visited by the locals to spend the afternoon in a joyous manner. However, many tourists also visit the park when they spend a couple of days in Siliguri.

### Savin Kingdom

If you want to do something more adventurous then you can go to the Savin Kingdom or the newly opened ice skating rink. From shopaholic to sports lover there is something for everyone in Siliguri. This amusement park offers several fun rides for its visitors to enjoy such as Break Dance, Paratrooper, Monorail, Swing Chair, Fume ride, Go Cart, and Dashing cars. Located near Darjeeling More at Durgapur, the Savin's Plaza has a large entertainment mall. There are several food courts and restaurants. A bar facility, a multiplex, and a resort retreat are also there. It is worth noting that the waterpark of Savin Kingdom opens at 10:30 am and closes at 6:30 pm whereas the Amusement Park opens at 11:30 am and closes at 7:30 pm.

### North Bengal Science Centre

Run by the Indian government and located at Matigara, the North Bengal Science Centre is also one of the best places to visit in Siliguri. It is truly a heaven for science enthusiasts as they can have a wide array of experiences. The major attractions of the science centre are a digital planetarium, Taramandal, a mobile Science exhibition, a 3D theatre, a Park full of flowers and trees, a Fun gallery, a nature interpretation, etc. Visitors can also enjoy daily Science shows, sky observation and exhibitions shows here.

### Mahananda Wildlife Sanctuary

You might be thinking that there is no place for nature lovers in this bustling city but you are wrong. Siliguri is also famous for its tea plantations on the banks of rivers Mahananda and Balsan. Siliguri is not as famous as Darjeeling for its tourist attractions but one cannot skip Siliguri as the few attractions that are there, are simply outstanding. Mahananda Wildlife Sanctuary is the most widely visited attraction of this town.

### Coronation Bridge

Popularly known as Sevoke Bridge, Coronation Bridge is one of the major reasons for the growing tourism in Siliguri. More than 20 km distance away from the heart of Siliguri, the bridge is constructed above the shimmering Teesta River to connect Darjeeling with Jalpaiguri District. Built during the colonial era, it is one of the finest architectures near the city from where the scenic view of the daunting hills covered by the thick forest can be enjoyed.

## How to Reach Siliguri

You can reach Siliguri by road, by air or by train. Being an important tourism and business hub near Darjeeling, the transportation system of Siliguri is quite superior.

**By Road:** Siliguri is linked with many major cities like Kolkata, Mumbai, Guwahati, Delhi, etc. Many private or government buses run every day from Kolkata to Siliguri. The distance from Kolkata to Siliguri is approximately 600 km and it usually takes 11 hrs.

**By Air:** Bagdogra is the nearest airport to Siliguri at a distance of 12 km. Directs flights are available from many major cities like Kolkata, Delhi, Mumbai, etc. After landing at Bagdogra Airport, Buses, and Taxis, and aurous are available to reach Siliguri.

**By Train:** Several trains are available from many big cities like Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, etc. It is one of the most convenient ways to reach Siliguri. Just board a train and after reaching NJP, hire an auto or taxi.

## Weather and Best Time to Visit

The weather of Siliguri stays charming all through the year. The temperature does not reach too high or does not get too low. Hence, the tourism of Siliguri is booming due to its appealing weather.

March to May is the months of summer in Siliguri. The temperature goes up to 37°C sometimes. The sky remains quite fair and sunny.

June to September is the season of Monsoon in Siliguri. This time heavy shower can be seen. The entire city starts to look green. During this season, the temperature ranges from 23°C to 30°C.

November to February is the season of winter in Siliguri. The temperature goes down rapidly from 30°C to 10 or 12°C. This is considered to be a pleasant temperature.

The best time to visit Siliguri is from November to February when the weather is pleasant and you can enjoy the beauty of this city at its most.
