# Places to Visit in Bhilai

Bhilai is Chhattisgarh’s second-largest city, and its multiculturalism and diversity have earned it the nickname “Mini India.” It is famous for its tranquil atmosphere and beautiful, verdant landscape. Other than being home to the world-famous Bhilai Steel Plant, the region is packed with places of worship, historical landmarks, and scenic vistas that draw visitors from all around. Because of its importance as a cultural, economic, and technological hub, this area features excellent rail, highway, and air links to all nearby major cities.

### Dholkal Ganesh Temple Dantewada Chhattisgarh

##### Mystical Temple Amidst Lush Greenery

Dholkal Ganesh Temple, located in Dantewada, Chhattisgarh, is a 1000-year-old temple dedicated to Lord Ganesha, nestled atop a hill amidst dense forests.

![St. Vincent Palloti] 

[**St. Vincent Palloti**] 

##### Peaceful place of worship

Located in the Bhillai district, not far from Raipur, the historic church of St. Vincent Palloti is a stunning tourist attraction to explore. This church, which opened in 1965, is noteworthy to members of many faiths, but primarily Catholics. Anglo-Indians, Telugus, Tamils and indigenous groups are all represented among those who maintain and frequent the church.

![Rail Globe Chowk] 

[**Rail Globe Chowk**] 

##### Popular landmark in Bhilai

The Rail Globe Chowk is a significant landmark in the Bhillai area. This landmark is well-known because, back when it was originally constructed, the steel rails used to measure the length of products produced by Bhilai Steel Plant were arranged in seven concentric circles around the globe that serves as the landmark.

[**Chandi Mandir**] 

##### Revered Hindu shrine

Near the city of Bhilai, you’ll find Chandi Mandir, a temple dedicated to the goddess of might. The Chandi Mandir, 15 kilometres from Bhilai, is situated in a beautiful area with the Shivalik Hills as a backdrop. The theological and cultural value of this ancient temple is immense.

[**Civic Center**] 

##### Perfect spot for relaxation

When you've seen everything there is to see in Bhilai, it's time to unwind and recharge. A good time can be had by everybody at the Bhilai Civic Center. In addition to being one of Bhilai's most popular places to shop, Civic Center is also home to a variety of exciting attractions, including a thrilling amusement park.

##### Tranquil place of worship

One of Bhilai's most well-known and often-visited landmarks is the Bambleshwari Temple. Travelling to and from the temple in a single day is possible via rail, bus, or taxi.

[**Ganga Maiya Temple**] 

##### Revered Hindu shrine

The Ganga Maiya Temple is conveniently situated adjacent to Bhilai city in the Durg district. A fisherman in the neighbouring body of water allegedly discovered an idol while recovering his net from the lake, but he decided not to steal the statue and instead donated it to the temple.

[**Uwasaggharam Parshwa Teerth**] 

##### Sacred site for Hindus

One of several places of worship in Bhilai, the 1995-founded Uwasaggharam Parshwa Teerth is a popular pilgrimage destination. Just outside of Bhilai, near Nagpura, this sacred Jain Temple is located.

![Maitri Bagh] 

[**Maitri Bagh**] 

##### Sprawling park and zoo

Maitri Bagh, adjacent to Bhilai, is a small zoo and park designed for visitors and residents alike. If you count the park's 111 acres, the zoo is the largest in the area by a wide margin. Historical records reveal that Russia and India signed the peace treaty that led to the creation of the Maitri Bagh, also known as the Friendliness Garden, as a symbol of the friendship that existed between the Soviet Union and India. The Steel Authority of India Limited is currently responsible for its upkeep.

3 to 4 hours

Suggested Time

![Bhilai Steel Plant] 

[**Bhilai Steel Plant**] 

##### Mammoth steel plant

The Bhilai Steel Plant, which can be found within municipal limits, is the reason the area around it is so well-known. When it comes to steel production in the country, this mill is second in size. The Bhilai Steel Plant, or SAIL as it is more often known, has been recognised as India's best-integrated steel plant eleven times. Production levels are reliable, and the metal is essential to meeting all of the country's manufacturing demands.

1 to 2 hours

Suggested Time

![Balod Siyadevi] 

[**Balod Siyadevi**] 

##### Known for its scenic beauty

Siyadevi, which is adjacent to Bhilai, is a fascinating tourist spot known for its stunning natural surroundings. There is a lovely temple there, which local legend has it was visited by Rama himself. Every month of the year is busy at the Siyadevi Temple, but the monsoon season (July and August) is when the majority of visitors come to witness the stunning waterfall.

5 to 6 hours

[**Deobaloda**] 

##### Charming hamlet with an ancient Shiva temple

Deobaloda is a small, quiet hamlet located about three kilometres from Bhilai. One of the oldest Shiva temples in Chhattisgarh, the red sandstone structure in this hamlet is the village's claim to fame.

## Tourist Places to Visit in Bhilai

[Bhilai] , a bustling city in [Chhattisgarh] , is a treasure trove of cultural and historical Bhilai tourist places. From ancient temples to modern architectural marvels, Bhilai offers a unique blend of the old and the new.

## Best Places to Visit with Family:

### Dholkal Ganesh Temple:

Dholkal Ganesh Temple is a Nestled amidst the lush greenery of the Dholkal hills, this ancient temple is one of the top places to see in Bhilai. The temple is home to a 1000-year-old idol of Lord Ganesha, making it a significant Bhilai attraction for both tourists and devotees.

### St. Vincent Palloti:

[St. Vincent Palloti] is a beautiful church, named after Saint Vincent Pallotti, is a beacon of peace and serenity. Its stunning architecture and tranquil atmosphere make it one of the best places to visit in Bhilai.

### Rail Globe Chowk:

A symbol of Bhilai's industrial prowess, the [Rail Globe Chowk] is a must-visit. The chowk features a large globe supported by four rails, representing Bhilai's contribution to the Indian Railways.

### Chandi Mandir:

One of the oldest temples in the region, the [Chandi Mandir] is dedicated to Goddess Chandi. Its serene ambiance and beautiful architecture make it one of the top Bhilai tourist places.

## Places to Visit with Friends:

### Bambaleshwari Temple:

Located in Dongargarh, the [Bambaleshwari Temple] is a significant religious site. The temple, perched atop a hill, offers stunning views of the surrounding landscape.

### Civic Center:

The heart of Bhilai, the [Civic Center] is a bustling shopping and entertainment hub. It's one of the best places to visit in Bhilai with friends for a fun-filled day out.

### Maitri Bagh:

A zoo-cum-park, Maitri Bagh is a favourite among families. With its lush greenery, diverse wildlife, and fun rides, it's one of the top Bhilai attractions.

### Balod Siyadevi:

A picturesque waterfall located near Bhilai, [Balod Siyadevi] is a perfect spot for nature lovers. Its serene beauty and tranquil ambiance make it one of the best Bhilai tourist places.

### Deobaloda:

Home to the ancient [Deobaloda] Temple, this small village is steeped in history. The temple, dedicated to Lord Shiva, is a significant Bhilai attraction.

## Best Time to Visit Bhilai:

The best time to visit Bhilai is during the winter months (October to March). The Bhilai weather during this time is pleasant, making it perfect for sightseeing and exploring the city's attractions.

## Good Places to Visit at Night in Bhilai:

The Civic Center, with its vibrant nightlife and array of dining options, is one of the best places to visit in Bhilai at night.

## Top Places to Visit in Bhilai in 1 Day:

Start your day with a visit to the Dholkal Ganesh Temple, followed by a trip to the Chandi Mandir. End your day with a visit to the Civic Center for some shopping and dining.

## Best Places to Visit in Bhilai in 2 Days:

On your second day, explore the Bambaleshwari Temple and the St. Vincent Palloti Church . In the evening, visit the Maitri Bagh for some fun and relaxation.

## Best Places to Visit in Bhilai in 3 Days:

On your third day, visit the picturesque Balod Siyadevi waterfall and the historic village of Deobaloda. These are some of the top places to visit in Bhilai in three days.

## Places to Visit Near Bhilai:

The region is rich in cultural, historical, and natural tourist spots. Raipur, the capital city of Chhattisgarh, is just about 30 km away and is known for its ancient temples and bustling markets. A little further away, at about 130 km, is the city of Bilaspur, famous for its archaeological sites and the stunning Bubble Island. For nature lovers, the Barnawapara Wildlife Sanctuary, about 100 km from Bhilai, is a must-visit. It's home to a variety of wildlife and offers a serene escape from the city. Another notable mention is the Achanakmar Wildlife Sanctuary, located about 200 km from Bhilai, known for its rich biodiversity and picturesque landscapes.

# Bhilai Tourist Places

## Places to Visit in Bhilai

![] 

### About Bhilai :

Bhilai, located in the Durg district of Chhattisgarh, is renowned for its industrial significance, especially the Bhilai Steel Plant, one of India's largest steel manufacturing facilities. Beyond its industrial prowess, Bhilai offers a mix of cultural attractions, parks, educational institutes, and vibrant local markets. Its well-planned infrastructure and rich heritage make it an intriguing destination for visitors.

### Major Tourist Destinations in Bhilai

#### 1\. Maitri Bagh Zoo and Park

**Description:** A prominent zoo and park established by the Bhilai Steel Plant, Maitri Bagh Zoo is home to a variety of animals including tigers, lions, and elephants. The park features a musical fountain, gardens, and a toy train, making it a popular recreational spot for families.

**Activities:** Zoo tours, picnicking, boating in the artificial lake, and enjoying the musical fountain.

**Location:** Sector-1, Bhilai.

#### 2\. Civic Center

**Description:** The Civic Center is a bustling commercial and entertainment hub in Bhilai, offering a variety of shops, eateries, and recreational facilities. It frequently hosts cultural events, exhibitions, and local markets, making it a lively place for shopping and leisure activities.

**Activities:** Shopping, dining, attending cultural events, and exploring local markets.

**Location:** Sector-5, Bhilai.

#### 3\. Bhilai Steel Plant

**Description:** One of India's largest steel plants, Bhilai Steel Plant is a significant industrial landmark in the city. Visitors can arrange guided tours to learn about the steel-making process, the plant's history, and its contribution to India's industrial growth.

**Activities:** Guided tours, educational visits, and photography (with permission).

**Location:** Bhilai, Durg district.

#### 4\. Tandula Dam

**Description:** Located on the Tandula River, Tandula Dam is a popular destination for picnics and nature walks. The dam offers stunning views of the reservoir and surrounding landscapes, making it a peaceful spot for relaxation and recreation.

**Activities:** Picnicking, nature walks, and photography.

**Location:** About 60 km from Bhilai, near Balod.

#### 5\. Deobalod

**Description:** Deobalod is a historical site known for its ancient Shiva temple and archaeological significance. The temple features unique architectural elements and attracts devotees and history enthusiasts interested in exploring the region's ancient heritage.

**Activities:** Visiting the Shiva temple, exploring historical sites, and learning about local history.

**Location:** Approximately 3 km from Bhilai.

#### 6\. Pasharwanath Tirth

**Description:** A prominent Jain pilgrimage site, Pasharwanath Tirth is known for its serene environment and beautiful architecture. The temple complex includes intricately carved structures and provides a peaceful retreat for spiritual reflection and meditation.

**Activities:** Spiritual visits, exploring temple architecture, and meditation.

**Location:** Near Bhilai.

#### 7\. Dhamdha

**Description:** Known for its ancient temples and historical significance, Dhamdha features a fort and temples with intricate carvings. The town offers insights into the region’s cultural heritage and is a great place for exploring historical architecture.

**Activities:** Visiting historical temples, exploring the fort, and photography.

**Location:** About 30 km from Bhilai.

#### 8\. Nandini Mines

**Description:** Nandini Mines are known for their limestone deposits and are part of Bhilai Steel Plant’s raw material resources. The mines are not only industrially significant but also provide educational insights into mining processes and geology.

**Activities:** Educational tours, learning about mining processes, and geology (access may require permission).

**Location:** Near Bhilai.

#### 9\. Siyadevi Temple

**Description:** A picturesque temple dedicated to Goddess Sita, located amidst natural surroundings. Siyadevi Temple is a serene spot for worship and offers beautiful views of the surrounding landscapes, making it a peaceful retreat for spiritual seekers and nature lovers.

**Activities:** Religious visits, enjoying scenic views, and meditation.

**Location:** About 30 km from Bhilai, near Balod.

#### 10\. Hajra Falls

**Description:** A scenic waterfall located in a forested area, Hajra Falls is known for its natural beauty and tranquil surroundings. It is a popular spot for nature enthusiasts, providing opportunities for trekking, picnicking, and enjoying the serene environment.

**Activities:** Trekking, picnicking, and nature photography.

**Location:** Approximately 100 km from Bhilai, near Dongargarh.

#### Travel Tips

**Accommodation:** Bhilai offers a range of accommodations, from budget hotels to luxury stays. Booking in advance is recommended during peak tourist seasons and festivals.

**Local Cuisine:** Try local dishes such as Faraa, Chana Samosa, and various Chhattisgarhi specialties. Restaurants and street food stalls provide diverse culinary options.

**Cultural Etiquette:** Respect local customs, particularly in temples and religious sites. Dress modestly and seek permission before photographing people or sacred objects.

Bhilai’s mix of industrial prominence, cultural attractions, and natural beauty provides a diverse and engaging experience for visitors, making it an essential destination for travelers exploring Chhattisgarh with Traveler.

### How to Reach Bhilai

### By Air

**Nearest Airport:** Swami Vivekananda Airport, Raipur (RPR),

**Distance:** Approximately 45 km from Bhilai.

**Connectivity:** Serves major cities like Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, and Bangalore.

**Transfer:** Taxis, buses, and car rentals are available from Raipur to Bhilai.

### By Rail

Railway Station: Bhilai Railway Station (BIA)

Distance: Centrally located in Bhilai.
Connectivity: Connected to major Indian cities such as Mumbai, Kolkata, and Delhi. Key trains include the Howrah-Mumbai Mail and the Durg-Nizamuddin Express.

Transfer: Autos, taxis, and local buses available from the station.

### By Road

**Major Highways:** NH53 and NH30 connect Bhilai to surrounding regions.

**From Raipur:** About 1-hour drive via NH53.

**Bus Services:** Regular state and private buses link Bhilai with Raipur and other nearby towns.

**Self-Drive:** Car rentals are available for those preferring to drive.

### Best Time to Visit

**Ideal Season:** October to March

**Weather:** Pleasant and suitable for sightseeing and outdoor activities.

**Monsoon (July to September):** Brings moderate rainfall, good for experiencing local greenery.

**Avoid:** Summer (April to June) due to high temperatures.

# Tourism in Bhilai

Bhilai, known as ‘Mini India’ owing to its multiculturalism and diversity is the second largest city located in the state of Chhattisgarh. **[Bhilai] ** has won accolades for being one of the fastest developing cities of the world. It is known for its lush green and clean environment and serene atmosphere. Besides housing the famous **[Bhilai Steel Plant],** it also boasts of a number of tourist attractions including religious places as well as historical relics and picnic spots. This place being an important educational, industrial and commercial hub is well connected to all major cities by means of rail, road and air. Tourists from all over the country, flock here to catch a glimpse of this simple, yet beautiful city.

## Best Time to Visit Bhilai

Bhilai experiences hot summers with temperatures soaring as high as 40oC at times. In addition, extremely hot winds blow in summer, making the climate highly unsuitable for sight-seeing. Also, the pattern of rainfall in Bhilai is unpredictable and highly variable, causing inconvenience to the travelers. Hence, a suitable time to visit Bhilai would be during October to March, in winter season. The climate during the winter is cool and pleasant and it would be an ideal opportunity to enjoy nature at its best.

## Places of Interest In and Around Bhilai

![Sightseeing in Bhilai] 

**Bhilai Steel Plant:** It is one of the main attractions in Bhilai. The plant is known for producing beams, rails, angles, crossings, sleepers, plates, channels, wide rods etc.

**[Maitri Bagh]:** It is a combination of a landscaped garden and a zoo, and was established by Bhilai Steel Plant. Also known as the ‘Friendship Garden’, it stands as a symbol of India-USSR friendship.

**Uwasaggharam Parshwa Teerth:** It is a Jain Temple in Nagpura. Dedicated to Shri Parshwanath it houses several beautiful temples, guesthouses and gardens and is worth visiting.

**Dhamdha:** The Dhamdha Shrine about 35 kilometres from Bhilai and is one of the most prominent tourist spot for Hindus.

**Tandula Dam:** About 60 kilometres from Bhilai, it is a famous tourist spot and attracts hundreds of tourists, especially during the winter. Various restaurants and accommodation options are available here.

**Deobalod:** At about 3 kilometres from Bhilai, it is home to an ancient Shiv Mandir.

**Civic Center:** Known to be the shopping hub of Bhilai, it offers a variety of options for social activities, entertainment and shopping.

**Ganga Maiya Temple:** About 60 kilometres from Bhilai, this temple located at Jhalmala has its own importance among the locals as well as tourists.

**Hajra Falls:** Situated at about 100 kilometres from Bhilai, amongst dense forests, hills and tunnels, this place is a popular picnic spot and a visual delight for the tourists.

**Siyadevi:** Located amidst a forest, this place is known for the famous temple dedicated to Sita Maiya and a natural waterfall between July and February.

**Kali Bari:** This temple dedicated to Goddess Kali is located in sector 6 of Bhilai.

**Kharkhara:** A dam built across the Kharkhara river and its surroundings in Durg is a favourite among the nature lovers.

## How to Reach Bhilai ?

Bhilai is well connected to all major Indian places by rail, air and road, making it easily accessible. The nearest airport is Swami Vivekanand Airport in Raipur, about 45 minutes from Bhilai. It is connected to all major cities through regular flights. The nearest major railway station is the Durg Railway Station, about half an hour from the city as it has almost all major train stoppages. Chhattisgarh State Road Transportation Corporation (CSRTC) and other private travel services run buses from major cities to Bhilai. Autos and shared diesel run three wheelers called tempos serve as means transport within the city.

## Where to Shop in Bhilai ?

![Hangout destinations in Bhilai] 

**[Shopping in Bhilai] ** can be a fun-filled and rejuvenating experience. Civic Center, one of the oldest shopping destinations is a favorite among the locals. The entire area is dotted with numerous shops and is considered a favorite hangout spot by youngsters . There are many markets in Sector 6 that makes it one of the biggest shopping centres in Bhilai. Besides, Akash Ganga, Gangotri, Sector 10 are other famous destinations for shopping, where the locals as well as the tourists can buy anything and everything they need at affordable rates. Malls like Surya Treasure Island, Jain Plaza add to the charm of this city.
