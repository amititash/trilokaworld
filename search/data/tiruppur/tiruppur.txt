# Places to Visit in Tirupur

Tirupur is a well-known city in the southern part of the Deccan Plateau, nestled right on the banks of the Noyyal River. The city is recognised as the “Knitwear Capital of India” and is a major tourism destination for the state of Tamil Nadu. The city is well-equipped to host visitors from all over the country, whether they are in town for a quick weekend or planning an extended adventure vacation. Tirupur, in the Indian state of Tamil Nadu, is a spiritual hub for individuals interested in visiting religious and spiritual sites.

[**Arulmiga Uthukuli Murugan Temple**] 

##### Dedicated to Lord Murugan

The Arulmiga Uthukuli Murugan Temple has been worshipping the Hindu god, Lord Murugan for over two centuries. The temple's chariot celebration, known as "theru," is also well-known.

[**Sivanmalai Temple**] 

##### Sacred Hindu temple

Sivanmalai Temple is set about 5 kilometres from Kangeyam in the Tirupur district. Lord Murugan is the patron god of this temple. Each year during the Thaipoosam festival, the temple is flooded with worshippers due of its fame. Also, three days of celebration are dedicated to cars at the annual "Thearotam" festival.

[**Amaravathi Dam**] 

##### Technology surrounded by nature

Udumalpet, in the Tirupur District, is the site of the Amaravathi Dam. The Indira Gandhi Wildlife Sanctuary, among others, are quite nearby. Its primary functions are flood mitigation and irrigation. K. Kamaraj formally inaugurated the Dam in the late 1950s. Early in 2003, the Amaravathi Dam provided power for more than forty per cent of Tirupur.

[**Panchalinga Waterfall**] 

##### Gurgling waterfall amid nature

Tirupur is home to the well-known Panchalinga Waterfall. The beautiful waterfall is found near Tirupur, about 18 kilometres from Udumalaipettai and 3 kilometres from the Thirumoorthy Temple. Stunning in both appearance and serenity, the Panchalinga Waterfall is a wonderful place to reflect and take in the scenery. A number of dams and religious structures can be found in the area.

[**Arulmiga Subramaniya Swamy**] 

##### Tranquil place of worship

The temple of Arulmiga Subramaniya Swamy is devoted to the deity Lord Subramaniya, who is shown as holding a Velu. Lord Karthikeya is another title for him. Sitting atop Sivanmallai Hill, only around 25 kilometres from Tirupur, this temple is a popular tourist destination.

##### Peaceful site for Hindus

Another significant temple, Sukreeswarar Temple can be seen in Tirupur. The Pandya Dynasty monarchs commissioned its construction in the 1100s A.D.

Tirupur's neighbouring town of Andipalayam is home to the beautiful Andipalayam Lake. It's a great place to go on a picnic or do something else relaxing. That place is perfect for a family outing. The lake is set to have boating facilities, which will increase the potential for tourists in the area.

[**Tirupur Tirupathi Temple**] 

##### Sacred place of worship

It's also worth visiting the Tirupur Tirupathi Temple while in the city. Pilgrims visit it all year round because of its convenient location within the city limits. Every day of the year, at regular intervals, sacred rituals are carried out at the temple.

[**Tirupur Kumaran Memorial Statue**] 

##### Insightful tourist attraction

A memorial statue stands here in honour of the liberation warrior, Tirupur Kumaran. He was a native of Tirupur and was slain in a lathi charge by British police during a protest march there. Tirupur Kumaran Route is the primary road that runs the length and width of the city, and it is named after him.

[**Avinashi Temple**] 

##### Revered Hindu shrine

Sundara Pandya, a member of the Pandya dynasty, built Avinashi Temple in the 15th century. Two young boys, so the tradition goes, once came to this tank to take a dip. The resident crocodile in the lake ate one of them. The temple's vehicle, one of the biggest of its type in all of South India, is a major attraction.

## Tiruppur District

### A buzzing industrial hub, Tiruppur teems with a multitude of options for tourists. Dotted with dams, stone sculptures, historic temples and wildlife sanctuaries, Tiruppur has something for everyone. You just need to chart your trip and explore it to the fullest.

Start your exploration of Tiruppur on a green note. Head to Indira Gandhi Wildlife Sanctuary. The scenic splendor of this place leaves you in awe. Keep your eyes and ears wide open for the surprise sights. This is home to a myriad of wildlife like elephant, gaur, tiger, panther, sloth bear, deer, wild bear, wild dog, porcupine, flying squirrel, jackal, pangolin, civet cat and birds like racket-tailed drongo, re-whiskered bulbul, black headed oriole, tree pie, spotted dove, green pigeon.

Spread over an area of 958 sq.km, this sanctuary is graced with beautiful rivulets, streams, take forests, green covered hills and much more. Amaravathi crocodile farm is a must visit spot. It houses the largest wild breeding population of crocodiles in South India. The project was launched in Amaravathy reservoir for captive breeding of broad snouted Mugger crocodiles which are facing extinction. The sight of crocodiles of varying sizes scattered across the reservoir, sedately opening their mouths and their swift leaps is something you wouldn't want to miss.

A panoramic sight awaits you at Amaravathi dam located in Indira Gandhi wildlife sanctuary. If you are fit, climb the steep steps that will open up to a spectacular view of the plains and the hilly areas. Enjoy boating and relax at Thirumoorthy dam site. You can also explore the remotest regions from here noted for their scenic charm. Soak up the beauty of sunflower farms, coconut groves and paddy fields as you drive your way to Udumalpet. The history buff in you would be disappointed, if you skip the stone sculptures at Tirumurugan Poondi. Get acquainted with the traditional families who have legacy in this art for centuries. Pick your way through the historic temples of Tiruppur known for their brilliant architecture and artistic glory.

The scenic location of the temples adds to their appeal. Arulmigu Subramanyaswamy Temple at Sivanmalai, Arulmigu Uthukuli Murugan Temple, Arulmigu Avinashi Lingeswarar Temple, Avinashi and Thirumuruganpoondi Temple are some of the famous temples and popular tourist destinations. Do plan your trip accordingly if you love to bask in the festive spirit of temples. The chariot festival of Arulmigu Avinashi Lingeswarar Temple, Avinashi draws people in droves. The temple chariot is known for its immaculate wooden carvings.

### [Amaravathi Reservoir] 

The Amaravathi Reservoir, nestled amongst the rolling hills of the Western Ghats, is a resplendent gem of nature's abundance. This man-made lake, crafted with ingenuity and precision, has become a veritable paradise for travellers seeking a respite from their mundane routines. This majestic body of water is surrounded by the verdant forests and towering hills of the Western Ghats, infusing the region with an ethereal aura of serenity.
