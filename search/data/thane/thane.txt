# Thane

#### Introduction to Thane District

Thane, located in the northwestern region of Maharashtra, is an integral part of the Mumbai Metropolitan Region and serves as an immediate neighbor to Mumbai. It is one of the most well-balanced metropolitan cities in India, offering a unique blend of natural beauty and rapid industrial development. Known as the “City of Lakes,” Thane boasts over 30 lakes, adding to its scenic charm.

Geographically, Thane is characterized by undulating terrain, with the Western Ghats extending towards the Arabian Sea. The district is interspersed with swift-flowing rivers, including the Ulhas and Vaitarna, which enhance its natural landscape.

Today, Thane is a thriving urban hub with a strong industrial base and modern infrastructure while retaining its historical and cultural essence. Its strategic location, rich history, and natural beauty make it a significant part of Maharashtra’s development.

#### The History of Thane

Thane’s history dates back to ancient times, with its earliest mention found in the works of Greek philosopher Ptolemy, who referred to a place near Thane Creek as “Chersonesus.” Venetian traveler Marco Polo also described Thane as a thriving kingdom due to its well-developed ports and bustling trade. Historically, Thane was known as Shreesthanak and maintained strong trade relations with Europe, Greece, Babylon, and Persia.

Evidence of Thane’s early rule is found in Ashoka’s edicts (225 BC) and Kanheri Caves, indicating that King Rudraman ruled North Konkan, including Thane and Colaba, in the 2nd century AD. The region was later ruled by the Mushervan dynasty (531–578 AD) and then by the Shilaharas for nearly 400 years.

From 1535 to 1739, Vasai, a part of Thane, was under Portuguese control. Thane’s shipbuilding industry flourished during this period, with timber from Vasai being used to construct fleets for voyages to Europe and Mecca. However, after a long siege led by Chimaji Appa, the Marathas took control in 1739. Vasai remained under Maratha rule until 1802, when the Treaty of Vasai handed it over to the British, incorporating it into the Bombay Presidency.

Although Thane did not play a role in the 1857 Revolt, it prospered under British rule due to trade expansion. Finally, in May 1960, Thane officially became part of the Maharashtra state, marking its modern political identity.

#### Top Tourist Attractions in Thane

Thane is a vibrant destination that offers a mix of historical landmarks, natural beauty, adventure spots, and cultural experiences. Whether you are a nature enthusiast, history buff, or adventure seeker, Thane has something to offer.

##### Religious Sites

- **Titwala Ganesh Temple**

One of the most revered religious sites in Thane, Titwala Ganesh Temple is dedicated to Lord Ganesha. This temple attracts thousands of devotees, especially during Ganesh Chaturthi and Sankashti Chaturthi. The temple is believed to be associated with the Mahabharata era, making it a site of historical and spiritual significance. The temple’s peaceful surroundings and sacred pond add to its charm, making it an ideal place for meditation and devotion.

##### Forts

- **Bassein Fort (Vasai Fort)**

A historic fort that has witnessed the rise and fall of multiple empires, Bassein Fort was originally built by the Yadavas of Devagiri in 1184. It later became an important strategic location under Portuguese rule (1535–1739) and served as a major shipbuilding hub. After a long siege, Maratha forces under Chimaji Appa captured the fort in 1739. Today, the fort’s majestic ruins, ancient stone walls, and Gothic-style churches attract visitors, making it a popular spot for history lovers and Bollywood filmmakers. The lush greenery around the fort adds to its charm, making it an excellent destination for photography.
- **Gorakhgad Fort**

An ideal destination for trekkers and history enthusiasts, Gorakhgad Fort is a hilltop fortress that was historically used as a meditation site by saints and hermits. The fort is accessible via a moderate trek, offering breathtaking views of the Sahyadri mountain ranges. Though in ruins today, the fort still retains its ancient caves, rock-cut steps, and a small temple dedicated to Lord Shiva. The trek to Gorakhgad is best attempted during the monsoon when the surroundings turn lush green, providing a scenic and adventurous experience.
- **Mahuli Fort**

Perched at 2815 feet above sea level, Mahuli Fort was once an important Mughal stronghold before being captured by Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj. The fort, surrounded by dense forests and waterfalls, is a paradise for trekkers and history buffs. With rock-cut caves, ancient water tanks, and temple ruins, Mahuli Fort offers a glimpse into Maharashtra’s rich past. The trek is challenging, making it popular among experienced hikers and adventure seekers.
- **Ghodbunder Fort**

Built by the Portuguese in the 16th century, Ghodbunder Fort was an important military base before being captured by the East India Company. The fort, situated on a hilltop overlooking the Ulhas River, provides stunning panoramic views of Thane. Although parts of the fort are in ruins, visitors can still explore its massive stone walls, bastions, and watchtowers. The fort is a favorite spot for sunset photography, attracting history lovers and trekkers alike.

##### Beaches

- **Kelva Beach**

With a coastline stretching 8 kilometers along the Arabian Sea, Kelva Beach is a must-visit destination for beach lovers. Lined with Suru trees and golden sands, this beach is popular for weekend getaways, water sports, and horseback riding. The sunset views at Kelva Beach are breathtaking, making it a perfect spot for photographers and couples. Several local food stalls offer delicious Maharashtrian delicacies such as Vada Pav, Misal Pav, and Bhajiya, providing a delightful culinary experience.

##### Natural Wonders

- **Kachrali Talav**

A beautiful freshwater lake located in the heart of Thane, Kachrali Talav serves as a popular recreational spot. The lake features a well-maintained jogging track, boating facilities, and a mini-garden, making it an ideal place for families and fitness enthusiasts. The lake is particularly beautiful during the early morning and sunset hours, offering a serene escape from city life.
- **Har Har Gange Waterfalls**

Located in Suraj Waterpark, Har Har Gange Waterfalls is one of the most impressive artificial waterfalls in India, standing at 24 meters high and 26 meters wide. The cascading water, combined with lush green surroundings, creates a tranquil experience for visitors. This spot is perfect for families looking for a relaxing retreat.
- **Naneghat Hills**

One of the most scenic trekking destinations near Thane, Naneghat Hills is a 2600-foot-high mountain pass that once served as a major trade route during the Satavahana dynasty. The trek to Naneghat is filled with ancient caves, rock-cut inscriptions, and scenic valleys. The highlight of the trek is the massive stone-carved pot, once used for collecting tolls from traders. This place is perfect for stargazing, monsoon trekking, and photography.
- **Thane Creek Flamingo Sanctuary**

One of the most popular birdwatching destinations in Maharashtra, the Thane Creek Flamingo Sanctuary is home to a variety of migratory birds, including the vibrant pink flamingos. Every year, between February and May, thousands of flamingos migrate here, creating a stunning spectacle. The sanctuary, surrounded by lush mangrove forests, provides a serene escape from the bustling city and is perfect for birdwatchers and nature lovers. The best way to explore this sanctuary is through a boat safari, which allows visitors to get up close to the birds in their natural habitat.
- **Tansa Wildlife Sanctuary**

Spread across 320 square kilometers, Tansa Wildlife Sanctuary is a lush green paradise, housing more than 200 species of birds and a variety of wild animals such as leopards, barking deer, palm civets, and wild boars. The sanctuary, rich in biodiversity, also serves as a crucial water source for Thane and Mumbai. With its dense forests and serene lakes, this place is a perfect getaway for wildlife photographers, birdwatchers, and adventure seekers looking to explore nature in its raw form.

##### Other Attractions

- **Elvis Butterfly Garden**

A hidden gem for nature lovers, Elvis Butterfly Garden is home to more than 140 species of butterflies. This well-maintained park is an open-air sanctuary where visitors can witness thousands of colorful butterflies fluttering around. The best time to visit is during early mornings when butterflies are most active. The garden also has a mini-forest area, making it an ideal spot for children and nature enthusiasts to learn about butterfly conservation and plant biodiversity.

# Best Places in Thane to Visit for Couples, Family and Friends

Located on the outskirts of Mumbai, Thane, fondly known as the “City of Lakes,” is renowned for its lush parks, charming lakes, and flawless fusion of contemporary architecture with the natural world. Increasing urbanisation in the region has attracted tourists and real estate developers, who have invested in creating a residential haven with lots of green space, good connectivity, and various recreational activities.

Whether you are a family looking for adventure, a couple seeking peaceful gateways to spend some quality time, or a group of friends in search of thrilling activities, Thane has something to offer everyone.

In this blog, we’ll explore some amazing places to visit in Thane and examine how its real estate market supports and enhances this vibrant way of life. But first, let’s cover a few interesting facts about the Thane district.

## About Thane District

Situated in the western part of the Indian state of Maharashtra, Thane spans an area of more than 147 square kilometres. With a rich history that dates back to the 13th century, Thane has seen British, Portuguese, and Maratha rule.

In addition to the stunning lakes, Thane is a great place for trekking because it is surrounded by the Sahyadri Hills. It is also home to several gardens, resorts, and forts. Boat rides on the lakes are another option.

![Thane District] 

Besides being a popular tourist place, the district serves a variety of delectable traditional street delicacies, such as Pav Bhaji and Bhajiyas. Now, let’s explore some of the best places in Thane to visit with friends and family and romantic settings for couples.

## Best Places to Visit in Thane

### Thane Creek Flamingo Sanctuary

![Section Image] 

For those who enjoy observing birds, Thane Creek Sanctuary is the ideal location. This sanctuary, surrounded by mangrove woods, is home to a variety of bird species. It’s hard to look away from these birds since they add so much colour to the entire area, with the pink-coloured flamingos as its main attraction.

The sanctuary is a great spot to unwind and escape from the city’s bustle. It’s especially worth visiting in April when the flamingos migrate here. In addition to enjoying the views of these magnificent birds, you can enjoy refreshments, take a cool stroll through the outdoors, and take pictures of the lovely birds.

**Location:** Mangrove Forest, Coastal Marine Biodiversity Center, Airoli Creek, Diva Nagar Road, Thane, Maharashtra—400708

**Time to Visit:** 09:00 AM–06:00 PM

**Entry Fee:**

For Adults: INR 50 per person

For Children: INR 25 per person

### Elvis Butterfly Garden

![Section Image] 

The Elvis Butterfly Garden, one of Thane’s most popular tourist places, is a habitat for more than 132 different species of vibrant butterflies. This outdoor butterfly garden provides a singularly educational experience for guests of all ages.

The garden’s owner gives each visitor a tour of the garden while describing the different stages of the butterfly’s development. In their natural environments, visitors can also see all the butterflies at various stages, including larvae, pupae, caterpillars, and finally, butterflies.

**Location:** Ward No. 1, Village Road, Gowniwada, Owale, Thane, Maharashtra – 400615

**Time to Visit:** 08:00 AM–12:00 PM (open only on Sundays)

**Entry Fee:**

For Adults: INR 100 per person

For Children: INR 50 per person

### Sanjay Gandhi National Park

![Section Image] 

With over 40 mammal species, 254 bird species, 150 butterfly species, and 78 reptile and amphibian species, Sanjay Gandhi National Park covers 103 sq km and is actually around 20% of Mumbai’s total geographical area.

Apart from animal species, it also habitats more than 1,300 plant species. At the park‘s exit gate, there is a gift shop with artefacts, coffee table books about SGNP Biodiversity, clothing and trekking equipment, literature about Maharashtra’s tribes, organic food, and forest fruit.

**Location:** Borivali East, Mumbai, Maharashtra—400066

**Time to Visit:** 09:00 AM–06:00 PM (closed on Mondays)

**Entry Fee:**

For Adults: INR 85 per person

For Children: INR 45 per person

### Upvan Lake

![Section Image] 

Upvan Lake, an artificial but environmentally friendly lake in Thane, Maharashtra, was constructed in 1880 by the Thane Municipal Corporation. However, the lake’s beauty and serene, picturesque surroundings draw both locals and visitors. It is currently one of Thane’s most visited tourist destinations. Its surroundings make it the ideal location for a family picnic or a mental-reviving stroll in the evening.

Moreover, it features pleasant spots to sit and read a book under the trees, as well as running lanes for fitness aficionados. There is also a boating facility for those who want to see the lake and the surrounding flora.

**Location:** Pokhran Road No. 2, **Thane** West

**Entry Fee:** Free

**Time to Visit:** Open 24 hours

### Talao Pali

![Section Image] 

Known for its natural beauty, Talao Pali, or Masunda Lake, is one of the most popular places to visit in Thane for water activities and relaxation, making it a favourite for locals and visitors alike.

**Location:** Shivaji Road, Thane West

**Entry Fee:** Free

**Time to Visit:** Open 24 hours

### Ovalekarwadi Butterfly Garden

![Section Image] 

Ovalekar Wadi Butterfly Garden, home to more than 500 species of butterflies, is among Thane’s most picturesque tourist places. Colourful butterflies are trudging over the wide sky as the park is covered in cocoons, eggs, and larvae. You can learn a lot about these lovely critters and take great pictures in the verdant garden.

**Location:** Ovale Village, Thane West

**Entry Fee:** Rs. 100 for Adults, Rs. 50 for Children

**Time to Visit:** 8:00 AM–12:00 PM (Sundays only)

### Shree Kopineshwar Temple

![Section Image] 

Dedicated to Lord Shiva, the Shri Kopineshwar Temple is one of Maharashtra’s most recognised tourist destinations. At five feet tall, it houses one of Maharashtra’s largest statues of Lord Shiva.

The monument’s gate, an architectural wonder in itself, welcomes the pilgrims with a massive sculpture of Shiva’s bull, Nandi. Devotees particularly like the temple during Mahashivratri, which it commemorates with great fanfare and grandeur.

**Location:** Jambli Naka, **Thane** West, **Thane**, Maharashtra—400601

**Entry Fee:** Free

**Time to Visit:** 6:00 AM–9:00 PM

### Yeoor Hills

![Section Image] 

The Yeoor Hills, one of Thane’s top destinations for those seeking a brief escape from the bustling metropolis, provide the ideal retreat in the middle of nature. The Yeoor Hills are a perfect place for a picnic because they are teeming with native plants and animals.

Numerous small tribal communities can also be found here, providing a wealth of sightseeing options. A popular location for trekking and cycling vacations on its lengthy and winding routes, it is one of the most amazing places for couples in Thane.

The area is made even more charming by the numerous streams and waterfalls that can be found here. If you’re fortunate, you may also see some wildlife here, including vipers, cobras, and leopards.

**Location:** Yeoor Hills, Thane West

**Entry Fee:** Free

**Time to Visit:** Open 24 hours

### Suraj Water Park

Suraj Water Park is one of Thane’s best entertainment destinations, spanning 11 acres. Thanks to its pools and slides of all sizes and heights, all family members will be active all day. The park has a number of thrilling slides for adventurers, and the wave pool is a great place for leisurely people to spend a day.

Additionally, the Water Park features a rain dance space that is fully furnished with a DJ spinning hip-hop music. Locker facilities and rental systems are also available at the facility.

**Location:** Dongripada, Thane West, Thane, Maharashtra—400615

**Entry Fee:**

For Adults: INR 800 per person

For Children: INR 650 per person

**Time to Visit:** 10:00 AM–6:00 PM

### Vardhaman Fantasy Park

![Section Image] 

One of Thane’s newest attractions is Vardhaman Fantasy Amusement Park. Inspired by Disney World, this amusement park has seven different zones: Wonders of the World, Texas Cowboy, Greek Village, Colo Theatrum, Viva Las Vegas, Lake Venetia, and Spanish cuisine. Along with rides that promise to keep kids and adults active all day long, the amusement park also features an inclusive food court.

**Location:** Shivar Garden, Mira Road East, Mira Bhayandar, Maharashtra—401107

**Entry Fee:** INR 2

**Time to Visit:** 10:00 AM–10:00 PM
