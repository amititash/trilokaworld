Tucked along the banks of the mighty Krishna River, Vijayawada isn’t just a pin on the Andhra Pradesh map—it’s a full-blown experience. This city dances to the beats of ancient legends and modern chaos, with a vibe that’s equal parts spiritual retreat and street-food sprint. We’re talking temples with centuries of stories, caves carved way back in the 4th and 5th centuries (yes, before Google existed), islands you didn’t expect, and markets where you’ll definitely spend more than you planned. So seriously—why aren’t you here yet? Come, let’s explore the whirlwind of places to visit in Vijayawada together. And once your feet are tired and your camera roll is full, we’ll show you where to crash in style. (Psst… It’s Grand Vijayawada. You’re gonna love it.) Heritage &amp; History Buffs Unite Bapu Museum: Where Art Meets Armour Bapu Museum is that one history buff in the friend group—full of stories. Once known as the Victoria Jubilee Museum, this 1887 stunner has hosted everything from ancient weapons to porcelain with royal attitude. Outside, you’ll still find epic stone carvings repping everything from Buddhist lore to Satavahana swagger. Oh, and plot twist: it’s named after legendary filmmaker and artist Sri Bapu, not Gandhi—though he did once receive a flag design here! Historic? Totally. Boring? Never. Distance from Grand Vijayawada: 10 mins/2 km (Approx.) Gandhi Hill: A Hilltop Tribute to the Father of the Nation High above the city, at 500 ft., stands a grand tribute to Mahatma Gandhi—complete with a 52-foot statue that practically waves at you from the hilltop. Opened by KMR Aziker Hussain, this place isn’t just about height—it’s about history. There’s a library, an exhibit house (because why not?), and it shines light on the inspiring journey of Bapu and his fight for freedom. If you’re a history buff or just love oversized statues and scenic views, this hill is totally your vibe! Distance from Grand Vijayawada: 20 mins/6 km (Approx.) Amaravathi Archaeological Museum: Old stones. Big stories. Amaravathi Archaeological Museum is where ancient stories come to life—minus the dust and drama. Packed with the 3rd century B.C. Buddhist sculptures, relics, and Jataka tales carved in stone, it’s basically Andhra’s time machine in museum form. Quiet, calming, and oddly photogenic, it’s the perfect mix of zen and “whoa, this is from when?” moments. Distance from Grand Vijayawada: 80 mins/40 km (Approx.) Ancient Wonders &amp; Rockstars Mogalarajapuram Caves - Hidden, Historic, &amp; Totally Insta-worthy Mogalarajapuram Caves are Vijayawada’s version of a low-key legend. Carved way back in the day (we’re talking 5th–6th century BC level throwback), these caves casually flaunt rare carvings of Lord Nataraja, Ganesh, and Ardhanarisvara. It’s ancient, artsy, and honestly kind of underrated. You might not find a crowd, but you will find vibes, mystery, and the perfect ‘Hey! I’m cultured!’ Instagram post. Bonus: it’s right in town—no hiking boots required. Distance from Grand Vijayawada: 10 mins/2 km (Approx.) Kanaka Durga Temple &amp; Durga Ghat: Kickstart Your Vijayawada Story Perched on the banks of the mighty Krishna River, the Kanaka Durga Temple is Vijayawada’s crown jewel—and not just because it's on a hill with a view! This iconic temple is dedicated to Goddess Kanaka Durga, the fierce warrior who gave the demon Mahishasura a serious reality check and liberated the city. She's depicted here in full power mode—eight arms, each holding a celestial weapon, like a mythological multitasker on a mission. Whether you're a believer, a history buff, or just here for the vibes, this is the perfect spot to kick off your Vijayawada adventure. Distance from Grand Vijayawada: 20 mins/7 km (Approx.) Akkanna Madanna Caves: Ancient Rock Homes that Still Rock The Akkanna Madanna Caves are like ancient VIP lounges—cut into solid rock, blessed by Shiva, and still cooler than half the architecture on Instagram. Built way back in the 7th century (no power tools, mind you), they later borrowed the names of two temple-loving brothers who showed up centuries later. Either way, it's a great place to channel your inner historian—or just take a dramatic photo pretending you discovered it. Distance from Grand Vijayawada: 20 mins/7 km (Approx.) Undavalli Cave Temple: History Carved in Stone Think of Undavalli Caves as ancient India’s answer to a luxury multistorey villa—only carved entirely out of rock. With giant granite sculptures (hello, reclining Vishnu!), secret corridors, and river views that beat any modern balcony, this 4th-century wonder has serious main-character energy. Distance from Grand Vijayawada: 30 mins/10 km (Approx.) Scenic Spots &amp; Weekend Legends Bhavani Island: Boats, Breeze, and Bliss Bhavani Island is like the chill cousin of Vijayawada—right in the middle of the Krishna River. This lush green retreat is perfect for boat rides, picnics, and unsubscribing from reality. Whether you’re tossing bread to a bird, dodging occasional vendor calls, or simply enjoying riverside zen, Bhavani Island delivers relaxation with zero effort. Think of it as your personal reboot station—nature meets ‘ahh…’ in one easy ferry ride. Distance from Grand Vijayawada: 20 mins/7 km (Approx.) Mangalagiri: Where Holy Hills meet Handloom Thrills Mangalagiri is where spirituality meets serious style. One minute you’re climbing up to the Lakshmi Narasimha Temple for some divine blessings, and the next—you’re deep in handloom heaven, surrounded by sarees that deserve their own runway. It's the only place where you can pray, shop, and look fabulous doing both. Distance from Grand Vijayawada: 40 mins/15 km (Approx.) Kondapalli Fort: One Hill, Centuries of Drama Kondapalli Fort isn’t just a bunch of old rocks on a hill—it’s a 14th-century power move. Built for battle, later used by royals, then turned into a British training camp (talk about a career shift), this fort has done it all. You’ll find royal ruins, elephant stables, and even a cemetery. Distance from Grand Vijayawada: 60 mins/27 km (Approx.) From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

[Vijayawada] formerly known as **Bezawada**, is the second most populous city in the [Indian state] of [Andhra Pradesh]. It is the administrative headquarters of the [NTR district] and a part of [State's Capital Region]. Vijayawada lies on the banks of [Krishna River] and [Budameru Rivulet] surrounded by the hills of [Eastern Ghats].

Vijayawada is considered to be a sacred place for famous temples, churches, mosques and jain temples. It also serves as the ritual host of Pushkaram (a river worshipping ritual in India) of River Krishna.

- **[Akkanna Madanna Caves] **: Akkanna Madanna Caves are located at the foothills of the Indrakeeladri Hill in close proximity to the famous Kanaka Durga Temple. As per legends, these beautiful caves date back to the 7-century and are dedicated to Akkanna and Madanna who were ministers in the court of Quli Qutub Shah, the Nawab of Golconda. The rock-cut caves are triple-celled with pillared hall and are devoid of any ornamental moulding or sculpture. This architecture echoes the richness of the bygone era and the tragic end of the two great secretariats of Qutub Shah. The temple encompassed in the caves was constructed in the 17-century and there is a predecessor cave nearby that belongs to 2-century with idols of Trimurti, Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva.

- **[Gunadala Matha Shrine] **: In 1925, Rf. Arlati, the Rector of St. Joseph's Orphanage at Gunadala, installed a statue of [Mary, mother of Jesus] and later a church was built and consecrated in 1971, now popularly known as Gunadala Mary Matha Church. Since then the Feast of Our Lady of Lourdes became an annual event here, attended by hundreds of people. The church is situated on a hillock on the eastern side of the city.

- **[Kanaka Durga Temple] **: One of the most popular temples in and around Vijayawada, located on the Indrakeeladri on the banks of Krishna River. **Kanaka Durga Temple** is a [Hindu] Temple dedicated to Goddess [Kanaka Durga]. The temple is located in the heart of Vijayawada and is a 10 minute drive from the [Vijayawada Railway Station] and [Vijayawada Bus Station], while being about 20 kilometre away from the [Vijayawada Airport]. Buses depart for the temple from both the bus stand and railway station every 20 minutes. One can either motor up the ghat road or take to the steps on foot. During [Dasara], a special pujas are performed for 9 days known as [Navaratri] festival. This Navaratri festival is celebrated in this temple for every year. A large number of pilgrims from the city and its environs visited the temple after a holy dip in the River Krishna.

- **[Moghalrajapuram Caves] **: Mogalrajapuram Caves are five rock-cut cave temple groups located in different parts of Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh, India. Dedicated to Shiva, they were excavated during the Eastern Chalukya reign or the Vishnukundins reign. They are generally dated to about the 7th century, after the Akkanna Madanna Caves.

- **[Prakasam Barrage] **: The original dam across the Krishna was built over a 150 years ago. The dam construction was started in 1852 and completed in 1855. And the present structure dates to the 1950s. It is 1,223.5 m (4,014 ft) long. Several canals through the city of Vijayawada terminate in a lake behind the barrage.[\[1\]] 

- **Krishnaveni mandapam**: It is also known as the river museum and located at the side of Prakasam Barrage. It was constructed by the Krishna Industrial and Agricultural Exhibition society which consists of geographical route map of River Krishna, world currency, Krishnamma Idol etc.

- **[Undavalli caves] **: Located in Other side of the Krishna River, five kilometers from Vijayawada, these caves are said to have been carved in the 7th century A.D. Buddhist monks used this two-storey cave structure as a rest house during the monsoon. A huge monolith of the Buddha in reclining posture is a magnificent sight.[\[1\]] [\[2\]] 

- **Rajiv Gandhi Park**: Created by the Vijayawada Municipal Corporation with great care, this park welcomes the tourists at the entrance of the city with its impressive horticultural network. It includes a mini zoo and a musical water fountain. This park is open from 2pm to 8pm.

- **[Gandhi Hill] **: The first Gandhi memorial with seven stupas in the country was constructed on this hill at a height of 500 ft (150 m). The 52 ft (16 m) stupa was unveiled on 6 October 1968 by Dr. Zakir Hussain, the President of India. Gandhi Memorial Library, a sound and light show on Mahatma Gandhi's life and a planetarium are the other attractions.

- **[Victoria Jubilee Museum] **: A place for archaeology lovers, this museum has a carefully preserved collection of ancient sculptures, paintings, idols, weapons, cutlery, and inscriptions. This museum is closed temporarily due to repair works.

- **[Bhavani Island] **: Perhaps one of the largest islands on a river, Bhavani Island is located on Krishna River close to the city. AP Tourism is converting this 133-acre (54 hectare) island into an attractive tourist spot and a riverfront resort.[\[1\]]

# Places to Visit in Vijayawada

Lying on the banks of the Krishna River and surrounded by the Indrakeeladri Hills, Vijayawada is the second most populous city of Andhra Pradesh. The name “Vijayawada '' roots back to one of the mythological legends, which said that Goddess Durga killed Mahishasura – the demon. As she was victorious the place came to be known as “Vijayawada '', precisely meaning “the place of victory”. The hills were called “Indrakeeladri Hills'' as Lord Indra and his associates frequently visited them. The river Krishna, a permanent water supply together with the Buckingham Canal on the river’s southern bank is Vijayawada’s most significant feature. With deep cultural history and a flavoursome food palate, let us explore the city of Vijayawada.

# 9 Best Places To Visit In Vijayawada

Vijayawada, located on the banks of the Krishna River, is a vibrant city combining modernism with classic appeal.

Do you know? It is also known as **“The Land of Victory”.**

Vijayawada offers a wide range of attractions, Whether you are a history buff, a culture lover, a nature lover, or an architecture lover.

So, no matter what type of vacation you are looking for, you will have something for you. After so much praise, I hope you are thinking of visiting this place. Right?

To save you time, I’ve compiled a list of the best places to visit in Vijayawada. Read this blog and choose the attractions that best suit your interests.

## Top 9 Must-Visit Attractions in Vijayawada

Vijayawada has many tourist attractions, but are all of them worth visiting? Perhaps, perhaps not.

Because every place doesn’t appeal to everyone, right? To make things easier for you, here you will get a list of the famous places in Vijayawada. You can choose any place from the list depending on the type of vacation you are planning.

### **1\. Kanaka Durga Temple**

Kanaka Durga temple is the first attraction on the list of the best places for visitors to visit in Vijayawada. This temple is dedicated to Goddess Durga. Every year, a lot of people flock to this temple to get Goddess blessings. It is located on the hill Indrakeeladri, providing a panoramic view of the Krishna River.

Stories tell that the Goddess defeated the demon Mahishasura and made her home on Keela Mountain.

No matter whether you are a devotee or a nature lover, you must visit this temple.

- **Time to Explore**: 1-2 hours
- **Entry Fee**: Free
- **Distance from Vijayawada**: 5.0 Km
- **Things to Do**: Worship the deity, Observe temple architecture, Take part in Aarti

### **2\. Bhavani Island**

Vijayawada’s Bhavani Island provides the perfect combination of leisure activities and natural beauty nestled in the Krishna River. For adventure seekers, this island is a must-visit destination in Vijayawada.

Speed boating, rope walking, and rope cycling are among the thrilling pursuits available to adventurers. The island also has a robotic Jurassic Park and a mirror maze for kids.

So, whether you’re traveling with kids or alone, make sure to include this island on your list of places to see in Vijayawada.

- **Time to Explore**: 4 -5 hours
- **Entry Fee**: Free
- **Distance from Vijayawada**: 7.1 Km
- **Things to Do**: Boating, Swimming, Fishing, Walking, Water sports, Photography

### **3\. Prakasam Barrage**

Just 3 km from Vijayawada train station, Prakasam Barrage is a major water regulator and traffic bridge spanning 1220 meters across the Krishna River.

Many visitors make their trips in the evening to see these magnificent lights and enjoy the skyline views.

The barrage’s color effect makes it ten times more appealing in the evening than in the daytime. Hence, Prakasam Barrage is a necessary stop for everyone visiting Vijayawada.

- **Time to Explore**: 1-2 hours
- **Entry Fee**: Free
- **Distance from Vijayawada**: 7.8 Km
- **Things to Do**: View the illuminated barrage at night, Enjoy the backwaters, Photography

### **4\. Undavalli Caves**

Undavalli Caves, which exhibit ancient Indian rock-cut architecture, is another great attraction in Vijayawada. These carefully carved sandstone caverns, dating from the fourth to the fifth century BCE, represent old architectural styles.

Whether you enjoy history, spiritual seekers, or architecture, these caves are a must-visit.

- **Time to Explore**: 2 – 3 hours
- **Entry Fee**: Rs 25
- **Distance from Vijayawada**: 10.5 Km
- **Things to Do**: Explore Ancient rock-cut caves, Admire architecture

### **5\. Kondapalli Fort**

Do you love historical places? Yes, I have good news for you: Our next attraction on our list of places to see in Vijayawada is for you.

Kondapalli Fort, also known as Kondapalli Kota, is an amazing historical monument in the small town of Kondapalli, in the Guntur district, near Vijayawada.

This fantastic fort attracts tourists from all over the world. Locals often consider it a good place to spend leisurely days with family and friends.

- **Time to Explore**: 2 -3 hours
- **Entry Fee**: Rs 30
- **Distance from Vijayawada**: 27.1 Km
- **Things to Do**: See the three-story rock tower, Tour of Tanish Mahal, Explore the deep reservoir

### **6\. Amaravati**

Our next destination is not in Vijayawada, but it’s not too far away. I have included this city because it is close to Vijayawada and offers unique experiences.

Amravati, situated on the banks of the Krishna River in Guntur district, showcases the rich culture of Andhra Pradesh. Today, it is the capital of the state.

For pilgrims as well as history buffs, Amaravati is a must-visit.

Whether you’re an old Buddhist scholar, seeking blessings at its famous temples, or simply enjoying the tranquility of this riverfront town, Amaravati offers you a soul-stirring experience. Thus, if you want to visit a nearby place in Vijayawada, include it in your itinerary.

- **Time to Explore**: Half Day
- **Entry Fee**: Free
- **Distance from Vijayawada**: 20 Km
- **Things to Do**: Visit historical sites, Explore Buddhist heritage and temples

### **7\. Subramanya Swamy Temple**

Are you a religious person? Are you interested in exploring some ancient temples?

Then, visit the Subramanya Swamy Temple in Vijayawada,

This temple is dedicated to the Lord of Serpents, Kartikeya, which is situated at the bottom of the Indrakiladri Hills. In this temple, Lord Subramanya appears as three different incarnations: a child, a natural being, and a serpent.

Before approaching the temple gates, devotees customarily take a purification bath in the Kumaradhara River. If you’re visiting Vijayawada with your family, do not forget to visit Subramanya Swamy Temple.

- **Time to Explore**: 2 – 3 hours
- **Entry Fee**: Free
- **Distance from Vijayawada**: 41 Km
- **Things to Do**: Worship the deity, Take a holy dip in Kumaradhara River

### **8\. Bapu Museum**

Bapu Museum, also famous as the Victoria Jubilee Museum, is a living heaven for archaeology lovers. The museum was built in 1887 in honor of the Golden Jubilee coronation of Queen Victoria.

The museum is home to various ancient things recovered from archaeological diggings. The collections include idols, paintings, weapons, sculptures, and more. If you want to know more about the Indian heritage, include this in your list of the must-see places in Vijayawada.

**Time to Explore:** 2 – 3 hours

**Entry Fee:** Adults: Rs. 5 and Children: Rs. 3

**Distance from Vijayawada:** 2.6 Km

**Things to Do:** Check out the Gandhi Gallery, Examine the tribal and folk art collections, See the Photographic Exhibitions, and Stroll through the Museum Gardens.

### **9\. Gandhi Hill**

Gandhi Hill is among the major tourist attractions in Vijayawada. This Park cum memorial was built to pay tribute to the father of the nation.

The place offers many activities to entertain kids and adults. If you want a breathtaking experience to relax outside Citi’s chaos, then this place should be in your itinerary.

The distance from Vijayawada to Mahatma Gandhi Hill is about 6.7 km via MG Road or Vijayawada Road. You can book a cab or drive your vehicle for a hassle-free experience.

**Time to Explore:** 2-3 hours

**Entry Fee:** Free

**Distance from Vijayawada:** 6.7 Km

**Things to Do:** Toy Train Ride, Explore the Hilltop Garden, Witness breathtaking sunset
