[Patna Zoo] 





Sanjay Gandhi Biological Park, commonly known as Patna Zoo or Sanjay Gandhi Jaivik Udyan, is a prominent ecological and recreational destination in Patna, Bihar. Established in 1973 and spanning over 153 acres, it stands as one of India's largest zoological gardens, offering a harmonious blend of wildlife conservation, botanical diversity, and leisure activities. 🐾 Wildlife and AttractionsThe park boasts a rich collection of fauna, including majestic Royal Bengal tigers, lions, elephants, black bears, rhinoceroses, and various species of deer and birds. Visitors can also explore a well-maintained aquarium, a snake house, and an orchidarium, enhancing the educational experience. The expansive greenery and diverse flora make it a serene spot for nature enthusiasts. 🌿 Visitor ExperienceLocated conveniently on Bailey Road, the park is easily accessible and serves as a popular spot for family outings, school excursions, and picnics. Many visitors appreciate the park's cleanliness and the opportunity to observe animals in spacious enclosures. However, some have expressed concerns about the well-being of certain animals, suggesting a need for ongoing improvements in animal care. 🕒 Timings and Entry FeesOpening Hours: Tuesday to Sunday, 7:00 AM – 5:30 PM (Summer), 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM (Winter). Closed on Mondays and public holidays. Entry Fees: Adults – ₹30, Children – ₹20. ✅ Tips for VisitorsWear comfortable footwear and carry water, as exploring the park involves considerable walking. Adhere to park rules: avoid feeding animals and dispose of litter responsibly. Plan visits during morning hours to enjoy a more comfortable climate and fewer crowds. Check for scheduled animal feeding times for an engaging experience. In summary, Sanjay Gandhi Biological Park offers a refreshing escape into nature within the urban landscape of Patna. Its combination of wildlife, botanical gardens, and recreational spaces provides an enriching experience for visitors of all ages. While there are areas for improvement, particularly concerning animal welfare, the park remains a cherished destination for those seeking education, relaxation, and a closer connection to nature.

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

**Tourism in Patna** is refers to [tourism] in capital city of [Bihar] state in [India]. [Patna] is known for its tourist destinations and people across the state and abroad visits Patna. This article deals with major tourist attractions in Patna.

## History of tourism

\[ [edit] \]

The documented history of tourism in the Patna region dates back to the 4th century [BC]. [Greek] [geographer] [Megasthenes] (c. 350–290 BC) visited the region during the reign of [Chandragupta Maurya]. His observations were recorded in _[Indika] _.[\[1\]] [Dionysius] was the son of [Megasthenes], who visited [Pataliputra] in the reign of [Ashoka the Great].[\[2\]] 

[Fa-Hien] (ca. 337 - c. 422) was a Chinese [tourist] who traveled to acquire [Buddhist scriptures] and take back to China between 399 and 412.[\[3\]] [Hieun-Tsang] visited the sacred [Buddhist sites] in [Magadha] and spent much time studying at the great [Nalanda University] between 629 and 645.[\[4\]] 

## Types of tourists and tourism

\[ [edit] \]

In the earliest days tourism in the region was purely for [educational purposes].[\[3\]] As Patna is one of the most sacred cities of religions like [Buddhism], [Jainism], [Sikhism] and [Islam], many people travel to Patna as part of [religious tourism].

- International tourists - Patna attracts tourists from all over the globe, though most are [Buddhists] from [Japan] and [China].
- Domestic tourists visit the area on their respective pilgrimages.

Today, tourism in Patna is mainly either religious-based or moderated [educational tourism]. The Bihar government is also promoting [adventure tourism].[\[5\]]

### Archaeological sites

\[ [edit] \]

The history and tradition of [Patna] starts from the earliest dawn of civilization. The original name of Patna was [Pataliputra]. Its documented history starts around 600 BC.[\[6\]] As Patna has 2600 years of history and has seen a number of [empires], there are numerous [archaeological sites] in the area.

- [Kumhrar] \- The [archaeological] remains of the [Mauryan] period (322–185 BCE) have been discovered here, including the ruins of a [hypostyle] 80-pillared hall.[\[7\]] [\[8\]] The [excavation] finding here dates back to 600 [BC],[\[9\]] and marks the ancient capital of [Ajatashatru], [Chandragupta] and [Ashoka], and collectively the relics range through four continuous periods from 600 BC to 600 AD.[\[9\]] 
- [Didarganj Yakshi] is a fine example of [Mauryan art]. The sculpture is currently housed in the [Patna Museum],[\[10\]] and is India's most famous piece of art.
- [Agam Kuan], which means "unfathomable well", is said to date back to the period of the [Maurya emperor] [Ashoka]. The well is located east of Patna.[\[11\]] [\[12\]] 
- [Chausagarh] or Chausa, of [Buxar], while very well known in the annals of Indian history as the place where in 1539 AD Sher Shah defeated the [Mughal] emperor [Humayun], is also a place of great antiquity.

### Ganges river banks

\[ [edit] \]

The Bihar State Tourism Development Corporation (BSTDC) has recently started operating an air-conditioned [floating restaurant] _[MV Ganga Vihar] _ from [Gandhi Ghat] offering dinner cruises twice a day.[\[13\]] 

The Tourism Department focuses on the Ganges [Ghats] in Patna as a major attraction. Motor boats are available to rent at a moderate price and are fitted with Yamaha 25 HP air-cooled two-stroke engines. Lucky tourists can spot [Ganges dolphins].[\[14\]] There are a number of [forts] (like Raja Ghat, at Rani Ghat, collectarate Ghat) and [pilgrimages] (like [Kali Mandir] at Kali Ghat, [Gurdwara Pahila Bara] at Gai Ghat, and [Gurdwara Gobind Ghat] at Gaobind Ghat) situated on the bank of the [Ganges] in Patna. Tourists can visit [Mahatma Gandhi Setu] which is [one of the longest bridges in the world] and situated at [Gaighat].

### Religious complex

\[ [edit] \]

Patna is a popular destination for people who are on [pilgrimage].

#### Hindu pilgrimages

\[ [edit] \]

- [Mahavir Mandir], located in Patna, is one of the holiest [Hindu temples] dedicated to [Hanuman]. Millions of pilgrims visit the temple every year. It is the second most-visited religious shrine in [North India]. The Mahavir Mandir Trusts have the second highest budget in North India after the famous [Vaishno Devi] shrine.[\[15\]] 
- [Patan Devi] \- The sacred [temple] is held to be one of the 51 Siddha [Shakti Pithas] in India. According to [Puranic] legends, the 'right thigh' of the corpse of [Sati] had fallen here when it was chopped off by [Lord Vishnu] with his ' [Sudarshan Chakra] '. The ancient temple, originally called _Maa Sarvanand Kari Patneshwari_, is believed to be the abode of the goddess [Durga].[\[16\]] 
- [Kali Mandir] 
- [Birla Mandir] 

#### Sikh pilgrimages

\[ [edit] \]

Patna is one of the holiest cities in [Sikhism], as the [tenth Guru of the Sikhs], [Guru Gobind Singh], was born here in 1666 and spent his early years before moving to [Anandpur].[\[17\]] Patna was also honoured by visits from [Guru Nanak] in 1509 as well as [Guru Tegh Bahadur] in 1666.

- [Takht Shri Harmandir Saheb] is one of the [Five Takhts] of Sikhism. The [Gurdwara] at [Patna Sahib] is in remembrance of the birthplace of Guru Gobind Singh, the tenth Guru of the Sikhs.[\[17\]] 
- [Gurdwara Pahila Bara], commonly known as [Gurdwara Ghai Ghat], is dedicated to [Guru Nanak Dev], who during his visit to Patna stayed here in 1509 AD [Guru Tegh Bahadur] along with his family visited this place in 1666 AD[\[18\]] 
- [Gurdwara Gobind Ghat] is where the child [Guru Gobind Singh] used to play with his playmates on the bank of the [Ganges]. It is situated on the bank of river Ganges and hardly 200 yards from [Takht Shri Harmandir Saheb]. It is also known as [Gurdwara Kangan Ghat].[\[19\]] 
- [Gurdwara Guru ka Bagh] \- This [Gurdwara] is situated 2 miles away from the [birthplace] of [Guru Gobind Singh].[\[20\]] 
- [Gurdwara Bal Leela] \- This place is just a few meters away from Takhat Patna Sahib. Guru ji were playing with other children during his childhood. Gurdwara Bal Leela is also known as [Maini Sangat].[\[21\]] 
- [Gurdwara Handi Sahib] \- This Gurdwara was built in the memory of [Guru Teg Bahadur], as he with [Mata Gujri] and [Bala Preetam] stayed here in 1728.[\[22\]] 

#### Islamic pilgrimages

\[ [edit] \]

- [Pathar ki Masjid] \- The Pathar Ki Masjid stands on the bank of the river Ganges near Takht Shri Harmandir Saheb. Parvez Shah, son of Jahangir, established Pathar Ki Masjid in 1621.\[1\] The structure is built of stones, and so it got its name as Pathar Ki Masjid.[\[23\]] 
- [Sher Shah Suri Masjid], a [mosque], also known as Shershahi, is an example of the [Afghan] style of architecture.[\[24\]] [Sher Shah Suri] built this mosque in 1540–1545 to commemorate his reign. It is sited in the southwest corner of [Purab Darwaza] near [Dhawalpura].[\[25\]] 
- [Maner Sharif] is the place where [Makhdum Daulat] in 1608 breathed his last. It was then in 1616 that [Ibrahim Khan], Governor of [Bihar] who was also his disciple, finished the construction of his [mausoleum].
- [Bihar Sharif] \- The city was an active centre of Muslim learning and art. Today it is a small town with a rich cultural past and is a major Muslim pilgrimage centre.[\[26\]] 
- [Phulwari Sharif] \- Some important places in Phulwari Sharif: Khanqah Mujeebia, Sheesh Mahal, Shahi Sangi Masjid, [Imarat-E-Shariya].
- [Begu Hajjam's Mosque] \- built in 1489 by the Bengal ruler Alauddin Hussani Shah.

#### Jain pilgrimages

\[ [edit] \]

Main article: [Kamaldah Jain temple] 

Kamaldah Jain Temple

- **Kamaldah Jain Temple** : Kamaldah Jain Temple Complex is the oldest Jain temple in Patna built in 18th century. This temple belonging to Śvetāmbara sect. of Jainism, is dedicated to [Neminatha], the 22nd tirthankara. It is believed to be built on the site where [Jain acharya] [Sthulabhadra] (297—198 BCE) spent his last days. ## Frequently asked questions

Is Patna worth visiting?

Whether you’re coming to enjoy the cultural highlights and local charm or sample unique foods and explore in nature, there are plenty of things to do in Patna.

How do I spend a day in Patna?

After you’ve checked off leading attractions like ISKCON Temple Patna and Phulwari Sharif, spend the rest of your day discovering Patna’s shopping and dining scene.

What is the top tourist attraction in Patna?

ISKCON Temple Patna is the top tourist attraction in Patna and should feature high on every sightseeing list of the area.

Is three days enough to stay in Patna?

Three days in Patna is more than enough to enjoy the city’s leading attractions. Many guests book for two days on average. ISKCON Temple Patna, Phulwari Sharif and Patna Museum are top-rated sights. Like to discover more? Hang around a while longer to visit nearby hotspots like Danapur.

Is Patna a walkable city?

If discovering on foot sounds like your style of getaway, Patna is a great place to visit. Find leading attractions, like ISKCON Temple Patna, within walking distance of downtown. You can also enjoy a stroll through Rajendra Nagar, a cool neighborhood located about 2 miles from downtown.

What are some activities to do in Patna with kids?

Planning what to do in Patna with kids has never been so easy. Whether you’re traveling with teenagers or toddlers, there’s a range of family-friendly attractions to keep everyone entertained here.

What to do in Patna for adults?

There are plenty of things to do in Patna, with experiences for adults of all ages. Finish your day off at Pathar Masjid or Gandhi Sangrahalaya.

Are there free things to do in Patna?

One of the top ways to learn about free things to do in Patna is to ask a local tour guide. You’ll get insider tips on interesting neighborhoods, parks and other top attractions you can experience without opening your wallet. Phulwari Sharif is one sight that doesn’t have an admission fee.

### Can you suggest an itinerary for Patna?

**Day 1:**

Arrive in Patna. Begin by diving in the cultural ruins of the very famous Nalanda University. Patna is the birthplace of the tenth Sikh guru- Guru Gobind Singh. Spend the latter half of the day exploring the Sikh religion, visit Takht Shri Patna Sahib.

End your day with a stroll in the street markets shopping local souvenirs at Patna Market or Ashok Rajpath.

**Day 4:** (Optional)

Wake up early next day and head to Patna Museum. It is known for its preserved historical artefacts. Next, proceed to Gandhi Sanghralaya. Post lunch, you can spend the evening at the Buddha Smriti Park. End your trip by visiting the P & M mall- the most popular shopping arcade in the city.

## **About Patna**

Holding the great history of India, Patna is one of the oldest cities in North India. The city is existing since the 5th century BC and was recognized as Patliputra then. Patna’s location is beautifully adorned with monasteries, temples, museums, and marvelous ruins all over the city. Despite the recent industrialization and modernization, Patna has retained its special old city charm. If you want to witness the amazing blend of old and new, then a visit to Patna city is necessary. It’s time you shake off your belief that Patna is only famous for making political news and witness the true Patna scenery yourself.

### **How To Reach Patna**

- **By Air:** If you plan to book flights to Patna, then Lok Nayak Jayprakash Airport is the nearest airport. Major international and domestic flights operate to and from this airport as it is connected to major cities.
- **By Road:** The highways and roads that lead to Patna are well constructed. This makes the Patna location quite accessible, even via road. You can drive yourself, or you can rent cabs to reach Patna by road.
- **By Train:** Patna is also reachable via rail and is one of the best ways to reach there. Patna Railway station links the city to other major Indian cities with a wide range of trains at regular intervals.

### **Best Time To Visit Patna?**

Travelers can visit Patna throughout the year. However, the best season to witness the true Patna beauty is the winter season. It is the ideal time to explore all of Patna’s tourist places in comfortable climatic conditions. The months from October to March are perfect to plan a Patna Trip. That’s because the entire city looks washed and beautiful after the rains.

## **Commuting Options Within Patna**

**Patna’s** **location** is quite favorable when it comes to commuting options. The transport options that are available in Patna include rickshaw, auto-rickshaw, bus, and e-rickshaw. Amongst all these options, an e-rickshaw is one of the most pocket-friendly ways to explore all the locations in Patna.

For a city that has been around 2500 years old, it is impossible to not have interesting places to explore. There are a plethora of places to visit in Patna which you should not miss out on.

### 1\. **Gandhi Ghat**

![Gandhi Ghat] 

Gandhi Ghat is amongst the top Patna beautiful places to visit. The popular religious site is located on the banks of the river Ganges. The main attraction of Gandhi ghat is the Ganga Aarti done by a group of priests dressed in unique saffron robes with almost 51 lamps altogether. Another interesting thing about this ghat is that the ashes of Mahatma Gandhi were immersed at this Patna location.

- **Location:** Patna, Bihar
- **Timings:** Throughout The day
- **Entry Fees:** No entry fees

## 2\. **Patna Sahib Gurudwara**

![Patna Sahib Gurudwara] 

**Patna city** is flooded with Sikh tourists because of Patna Sahib Gurudwara. Harmandir Sahib is the birthplace of the 10th Sikh Guru, Guru Gobind Singh Ji. Maharaja Ranjit Sing built Takht Shri Patna Sahib to commemorate the event. Here you can get the best glimpse of Sikh history and artifacts associated with Sikh gurus and fighters.

- **Location:** Mithapur
- **Timings:** NA
- **Entry Fees:** Free

### 3\. **Sri Krishna Science Centre**

![ Sri Krishna Science Centre] 

Sri Krishna Science Centre is one of the most important places to visit in Patna. It is a magnificent science center that was built in 1978. If you are interested in science, then this is the perfect place for you. Sri Krishna Science center has a great collection of exhibits that demonstrates the principles of science.

- **Location:** West Gandhi Maidan, Dujr Diara, Patna, Bihar

### 4\. **Golghar**

![Golghar] 

Golghar is one of the top Patna tourist places to visit and is often crowded with visitors. This historical monument has a beehive-shaped structure and spiral staircase. Britishers built it as a granary during British rule. The most interesting thing about Golghar is its huge dome which is 29 meters in height and stands without any support of pillars.

- **Location:** Ashok Rajpath, Chahajju Bagh
- **Timings:** 9:30 am to 6:00 pm
- **Entry Fees:** Free

## 5\. **Bihar Museum**

![Bihar Museum] 

Bihar Museum is also called Patna Museum. This museum was constructed in 1917 and is a must-visit site for all history bugs. The **century-old Patna Museum** houses unique historical and archaeological finds of Patna city. The museum also houses many galleries like Natural History Gallery, which is again a must-visit place, especially with kids. It also consists of beautiful paintings, **art objects**, sculptures, etc.

- **Location:** Near Kotwali Thana, Budh Marg
- **Timings:** 10:00 am to 4:30 pm
- **Entry Fees:** INR 15

### 6\. **Sanjay Gandhi Botanical Garden**

![Sanjay Gandhi Botanical Garden] 

Sanjay Gandhi Jaivik Udyan or Sanjay Gandhi Biological Park is a famous place to visit in Patna. It is located on the famous Bailey road of Patna and was open to the public as Patna zoo. It is one of the most visited famous attractions of Patna. The park is currently home to over 800 animals of about 110 species. If you are someone who admires wildlife, then a visit to this place is a must.

- **Location:** Patna, Bihar Shariff, India
- **Timings:** 6:00 am to 5:00 pm
- **Entry Fees:** INR 30 for adults, INR 10 for children

## **Best Street Food Dishes To Try In Patna**

The **Patna location** offers all the right ingredients to tickle your taste buds when it comes to food. So if you are visiting the city for the first time, then do not miss out on the popular Bihari cuisine delights.

### 1\. **Litti Chokha**

One of the most famous dishes in Bihar is Litti Chokha. It is also the soul food for all Biharis. Your Patna trip would be incomplete without tasting its special little chokha. It is made up of baked wheat dough and is accompanied by chokha.

**Best place To Try It**: Raj Sweets Shop, Maurya Lok, Dak Bungalow Road, and Boring Road

### 3\. **Batatapuri**

Bihar is famous for its best street food scenes. All of **Patna’s** **famous places** are surrounded by street vendors serving the most delicious Batatpuri. After trying Batatpuri chaat in Patna, you will never forget its taste.

- **Best Place to Try it**: Any street-side vendor

### 4\. **Chana Ghugni**

Chana ghugni is one of the most fulfilling dishes to eat in Patna. It is also one of the cheapest food dishes you can eat in Patna. Chana Ghughni is also the most prominent snack served in every home during the evening in all cities of Bihar.

- **Best Place To Try It**: Maner Sweets, Darchanga house, Rajgir

## 5\. **Khaja**

The amazing multi-layered sweet dish of Patna is a specialty of Bihar. It is made of wheat, flour, and mava dipped in sugar syrup. The taste of this puff pastry is next to heaven. The irrefutable taste of khaja leaves people with a wish to eat it more and more.

- **Best Place to Try It**: Opp Patna Museum

## 6\. **Malpua**

Malpua is very similar to a small pancake. It is served to the guests along with Champaran Ahuna mutton curry. It is a dish that is specially made during the Holi festival in Bihar. The taste of malpua is irresistible, and it is worth trying.

- **Best Place To Try It:** Harilal’s Patna, Litti chokha shop

## **Best Shopping Places In Patna**

Shopping in **Patna city** is one of the best things to do during your vacation. From clothing, accessories to handicrafts and household goods, Patna shopping places offer it all.

### 1\. **Patna Market**

Patna market is one of the oldest markets in the city. It is one of the **best places to visit in Patna** if you are a die-hard shopaholic. From clothes, jewelry to electronic goods, you will find it all here. So if you plan to go on a shopping spree in Patna, then do make a point to stop here.

- **Location:** Ashok Rajpath Road
- **Known for**: Souvenirs, fabrics, handicrafts

## 2\. **Khaitan Market**

Khaitan Market is another popular market that is located near Gandhi Maidan. It is one of the most amazing shopping complexes in the entire city. The market has more than 500 shops where you can purchase items at wholesale rates. You must also try the famous kachauri sabji from the street vendors in the market.

- **Location:** Birla Mandir Road, Patna
- **Known For:** Bridal shopping, clothes, Weddin trousseau

## 3\. **Hathwa Market**

While you are on a shopping spree in Patna city, you cannot miss out on visiting the famous Hathwa Market. It is the lifeline location in Patna for all shopping lovers and a popular tourist destination. The market offers an amazing collection of western as well as traditional clothes. The market is a little biased towards women and offers clothes in different designs and styles.

- **Location:** Bakarganj, Patna
- **Known For:** Women Apparels.

## 4\. **Maurya Lok**

Maurya Lok is one of the biggest and oldest shopping places in Patna. It has been one of the most favorite shopping destinations for locals for at least a decade. The shopping complex has an ample amount of space and offers great brand clothing, accessories, and cosmetics. If you are visiting Patna, then do check out the collection of garments at this shopping complex.

- **Location:** Fraser Road Area, Patna
- **Known For:** Branded clothes and footwear

### 5\. **P&M Mall Patna**

If you are looking for a single place where you can enjoy shopping, entertainment, and can have fun, then P&M mall is the perfect place. It is one of the first malls in Patna and is quite a popular family destination. Here you can go for luxury brand shopping and eating as well.

- Lo **cation:** Patliputra Industrial Area, Bihar 800015
