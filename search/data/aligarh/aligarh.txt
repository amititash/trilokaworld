# Tourist Places To Visit In Aligarh

Planning A Trip To Aligarh? Here's our list of top tourist places to visit in Aligarh

Aligarh, one of the most important cities in [Uttar Pradesh] is quite a popular city amongst tourists coming from all over the country. Also known as Koil or Kol, Aligarh is one of the most important educational centers in the country. The city of Aligarh has had a lot of influence from the Mughal emperors who have ruled the region for a long time and the influence is seen in the monuments and structures inside the city. Aligarh is mainly famous for Aligarh Muslim University, which is one of the main tourist attractions in the city. Other than this, there are several forts, gardens and the university campus has many different attractions. Below is a list of the tourist places to visit in Aligarh, let’s have a look.

## Aligarh Fort

![Aligarh Fort: Tourist Places To Visit In Aligarh] 

Photograph by indiamyworld.com

Originally built in the 14th century, the Aligarh Fort is one of the top attractions in the city of Aligarh. The fort is known by different names like Baunasaur Quila, Ramgarh Quila or Aligarh Quila and is managed by the Aligarh Muslim University. This fort is a very important one in history and represents influences of the Islamic culture from the bygone eras. The architecture of the fort is absolutely exquisite; with intricate and detailed carvings that hold a lot of meaning. This magnificent structure has other features like minarets, terraces, bastions and a huge grand entrance for the public as well. Surrounding the fort is a lovely garden that takes the beauty of the fort to another level.

## Aligarh Muslim University

![Aligarh Muslim University: Tourist Places To Visit In Aligarh] 

Photograph by scroll.in

The Aligarh Muslim University is the best Anglo Muslim educational center in [India] and is very famous in the city; this university is the main base of the city and defines the significance of the city itself. If you are more inclined towards the history of the country and especially this city, the Aligarh Muslim University is definitely the place you want to visit. This structure was established and founded by Sir Syed Ahmed Khan in the year 1875 and was set up with the intention of making modern education available for the Indian population. The Aligarh Muslim University is known for its notable alumni of politicians, sportspersons, lawyers and many more and is has also been portrayed in several movies of Bollywood.

## Ibn Sina Academy of Medieval Medicine and Sciences

![Ibn Sina Academy of Medieval Medicine and Sciences: Tourist Places To Visit In Aligarh] 

Photograph by wikipedia.com

The Ibn Sina Academy of Medieval Medicine and Sciences was named after a well known physician of Unani medicine by the name of Abu Ali Ibn Sina. This school was built by a very intelligent Indian researcher and doctor by the name of Hakim Syed Zillur Rahman in order to commemorate and show respect to this well known scientist. The Ibn Sina Academy of Medieval Medicine and Sciences was established with the aim of promotion of ancient medicine and methodology in [India]. This institution as also meant to preserve the traditional Indian medicine practices and to safeguard the techniques that relates to various ailments in the human body.

## Jama Masjid

![Jama Masjid: Tourist Places To Visit In Aligarh] 

Photograph by pinterest.com

Jama Masjid is one of the most famous tourist attractions in Aligarh and is also a highly revered religious place in [Uttar Pradesh]. This mosque was established and built in the year 1879 by Sir Syed Ahmad Khan, the ruler of Aligarh in the seventeenth century. The Jama Masjid also goes by the name of Sir Syed Masjid and is visited by a large number of pilgrims every year; this is also the place where Sir Syed Ahmad Khan’s tomb lies. This structure is established in the same vicinity as the Aligarh Muslim University. In January 1915, the mosque was fully constructed and that is when the ritual and tradition of reading the Namaaz at 5.00 AM was imbibed by the mosque. The mosque is huge and can accommodate around one thousand people.

## Sir Syed House Museum

![Sir Syed House Museum: Tourist Places To Visit In Aligarh] 

Photograph by wikimedia.org

Located at the heart of the Aligarh Muslim University is Sir Syed House Museum, a well known art gallery and museum in Aligarh. Back in the day, this was the residence of Sir Syed Ahmad Khan, which has now been converted to a Museum, giving immense information about the Aligarh Muslim University. This is a well maintained museum that has collected archeological objects like sculptures, doorjambs, carved stone pieces and so much more. There are different sections inside the museum that tells the history of different eras in the city. This is a great place to visit if you want to know more about Aligarh and its history. After you are done with the museum, enjoy a walk in the beautiful lush greenery outside of the structure.

## Khereshwar Temple

![Khereshwar Temple: Tourist Places To Visit In Aligarh] 

Photograph by inspirock.com

The Khereshwar Temple is one of the most famous temples in Aligarh and is highly respected and revered by locals as well as visitors from other cities. This temple has a shrine of the holy Shiva Linga which is very tiny in size and is locally known as Invisible Shiva Linga. At the temple you can also find other brass idols of various gods and goddesses in Hindu mythology. The Khereshwar Temple is said to be about a thousand years old and is visited by many people throughout the year; many people visit the temple to hear more about the legends and myths of Dvapara Yuga. The best time to visit the temple is during Mahashivratri when the temples is fully lit up and looks absolutely beautiful.

## Teerthdham Mangalayatan

![Teerthdham Mangalayatan: Tourist Places To Visit In Aligarh] 

Photograph by bhaktibharat.com

If you are looking to explore a Jain temple, Teerthdham Mangalayatan is the place to visit. This temple is said to be one of the biggest Jain temples in the entire country and was established by the Shri Adinath Kund-Kund Kahan Digamber Jain Trust. The Teerthdham Mangalayatan are a group of of different temples that pay attention to social concepts like education, social service and research activities. This temple is located at a distance of 13 km from the main city of Aligarh on the Aligarh- [Agra] Highway, spreading over an area of 16 acres of land. There are five different temples in the temple complex and each one has their own beauty, which is worth visiting for.

## Dor Fortress

![Dor Fortress: Tourist Places To Visit In Aligarh] 

Photograph by inspirock.com

The Dor Fortress is located at the center of the city and is easy to locate. This structure gives its visitors a lot of history about the fort as well as the city of Aligarh. You can easily spend a couple of hours at this place, learning the historic background of the city and the fortress itself. The fortress gets its name from the Dor Rajputs, who ruled over the Aligarh region once and had established this fortress to protect themselves from other clans. Once inside the fortress, you can see a lot of things like horse and elephant stables, sculptures and structures, minarets and a beautiful well. This is a great place to go sightseeing; even though the fortress does not have any specific timing, it is advisable to go there in the daytime.

## Shekha Bird Sanctuary

![Shekha Bird Sanctuary: Tourist Places To Visit In Aligarh] 

Photograph by localguidesconnect.com

Located at a distance of 17 km from the main part of Aligarh city is the Shekha Bird Sanctuary. This bird sanctuary is situated close to a huge lake called Shekha Jheel where thousands of migrant and non migrant birds visit to breed. This fresh water lake exists throughout the year and has come about from the Upper Ganges canal, flowing adjacent from the lake. The best time to visit the sanctuary is from the month of November to March where you can approximately see more than two hundred species of birds that migrate to that zone in order to get away from the cold in their native region. This is a great place for bird watchers to learn about different species of birds and observe them.

## Baba Barchi Bahadur Dargah

![Baba Barchi Bahadur Dargah: Tourist Places To Visit In Aligarh] 

Photograph by darululoomjais.com

Baba Barchi Bahadur Dargah is a famous mosque situated near Shastri Park in Aligarh. This Dargah is 600 years old and has a lot of history attached to it. This is a mosque that is considered highly religious and is visited by many devotees throughout the year. The reason why the Dargah is so popular amongst devotees is because it is said that people who pray here with all their heart, their wishes are fulfilled. If you do plan to visit, make sure it’s on a Thursday when you are able to attend the Qawwali session; this is a real treat for people who like to sing prayers addressing the almighty.

#### Aligarh

The city of Aligarh is situated in the Uttar Pradesh state in northern India. Aligarh, which combines tradition and modernity, is well-known for its rich history, cultural heritage, and educational institutions. With roots dating back thousands of years, Aligarh boasts a rich historical legacy. The Rajputs and the Mughals were two of the many dynasties that ruled the city.

A historical landmark, the Aligarh Fort was built in 1524 by Muhammad, the son of Shah Jahan. It offers a window into the past of the city and exhibits the architectural prowess of that era. Aligarh is home to the well-known Aligarh Muslim University, one of the city's most notable assets. Since its founding in 1875 by Sir Syed Ahmad Khan, AMU has been essential to the growth of education in the area. The architectural wonder known as Sir Syed Masjid, named for the university's founder, Sir Syed Ahmad Khan, is located on the AMU campus. The mosque is renowned for both its cultural significance and elaborate design.

With influences from many cultures and eras of history, Aligarh is renowned for its cultural variety. The city hosts a number of festivals and events to highlight its diversity. A serene getaway and leisure area can be found in the lovely lake Shekha Jheel, which is close to Aligarh. Indulge in boating and other peaceful activities while visiting the area. Both locals and visitors can enjoy the vibrant atmosphere of the city at Kwarsi Market, a lively bazaar. You can buy for a variety of goods and learn about the local way of life there. The historical structures, monuments, celebrations, and many customs of Aligarh all showcase its rich cultural legacy. The district takes pride in both its historical past and ethnic variety.

Visit Aligarh, which is renowned for its historical significance and cultural heritage, if you have an interest in ancient history and would like to see historical locations. The historical sites and picturesque environs of Aligarh will draw the curiosity of tourists who wish to learn more about the rich history of Uttar Pradesh. Along with a multitude of historical, cultural, and natural features, the area has a unique charm of its own.

#### Major Attractions:

In order to understand more about Aligarh's distinct personality and contributions to the local legacy, you should definitely check out the city's main attractions while you're there. Some of the major attractions are **Aligarh Fort, Naqvi Park, Jama Masjid, Shekha Jheel, Khereshwar Temple, Aligarh Museum, Dor Fortress, Baba Barchi Bahadur Dargah, Kwarsi Market,** and so on.

#### Climatic Condition:

The temperature during the summer is around 40 degrees and the minimum in the winter is 15 degrees.

#### Best time to visit:

The best time to visit Aligarh is in the winter from October to February when the temperature is pleasant and temperate, making city exploration feasible.

#### How to reach:

**By Air:** The Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) in Delhi is the closest airport to Agra located roughly 150 km away. You can take different forms of transportation or hire a taxi from the airport to get to Aligarh.

**By Train:** Aligarh Junction is one of the main train stations in the city and there are several train connections available in Aligarh.

**By Road:** Aligarh is easily accessible by vehicle due to its well-developed road network. National Routes 34 and 91 pass Aligarh, linking it to main cities within the vicinity.

### FAQ:

##### Does Aligarh have any cultural festivals?

Many cultural events take place in Aligarh, especially those hosted by Aligarh Muslim University. These events draw both visitors and intellectuals, such as the annual AMU Literary Festival, Sir Syed Day and other academic and cultural gatherings.

##### Which regional cuisines are most well-liked in Aligarh?

While visiting Aligarh, visitors should sample the Mughlai and Awadhi cuisine, which includes kebabs, biryani, nihari, and a variety of sweet treats including gulab jamun, jalebi, and the region's famous sev.

##### Is Aligarh safe for tourists?

Aligarh is generally considered safe for tourists.

##### Are there accommodations options in Aligarh close to the city's major attractions?

Indeed, Aligarh offers accommodations options for a variety of budgets in its hotels and guesthouses. Booking lodging in advance is advised particularly during the busiest travel seasons.

#### Aligarh Fort

![Image] 

Aligarh Fort, which also goes by the names Aligarh Quila, Baunasaur Quila and Ramgarh Quila is situated in Qila, Aligarh, Uttar Pradesh. Managed by the Aligarh Muslim University, this structure represents historic Islamic influences from an earlier era through its exquisite architecture and intricate symbolic carvings. The magnificent edifice features distinguishing minarets, bastions, terraces, and a grand arched entrance that announces its presence to the general public. There is a garden, supervised by the Botanical Department of Aligarh Muslim University, adds to the grandeur of the fort, with its diverse variety of vibrant flowers, trees and animal species such as delicate koel birds, beautiful peacocks and ferrets among others.

#### SkyHigh

![Image] 

Sky High is the only internationally recognised sky-diving drop zone in India. It is a four-year-old sport-cum-business that attracts those seeking thrill and an escape from their mundane routine. Its propagator, Rudra Bhanu Solanki is an Arizona certified trainee who practices tandem jumps here. The participants are provided with a certificate and merchandise after successful completion of their dive. There are specific guidelines that Sky High abides by (Visit Thing to Keep in Mind for more). They provide the participants with a record of their fall shot on a video and/or camera. Sky High prioritises the safety of its participants over their zeal, and therefore, it is a mandatory measure to provide a medical certificate of the participants stating their fitness. This is followed by signature on legal documentation, training and finally the dive. This activity’s dependence on weather can be frustrating, and at times the necessary measure to either prepone or postpone the activity are taken.

#### Khereshwar Temple

![Image] 

Located in Aligarh, Uttar Pradesh, the Khereshwar Temple is enshrined with a holy Shiva linga, which is so small that it is popularly known as Invisible Shiva linga, along with several brass idols of other Hindu deities. Believed to be over thousands of years old, the temple witnesses numerous devotees visiting in belief about the age-old myths and legends about the Dvapara Yuga. On the occasion of Maha Shivratri each year, devotees from all over India swarm to the Khereshwar Temple.

Aligarh is situated at a distance of about 140 km from Delhi, the capital of India. It is the seat of the district administration and a vibrant city in the North-Indian state of Uttar Pradesh. The town falls in the area of DOAB, which means a place between two rivers. This region was once called ‘kol’ for the tribe of the same name. It came to exist in its present form after the British annexation of the district in 1803. Aligarh is popularly called ‘the city of locks’ because of the large number of lock manufacturing units here. The city had been manufacturing and selling locks since 1890. Aligarh is also known for its brass, bronze, iron and aluminium products. Tourist Places in Aligarh The place has a number of tourist attractions. Some of the major places of interest are listed below. Dor Fortress The famous Dor fortress is situated in the upper area of the city centre of Aligarh. It has lost its former glory with the passage of time but the magnificent ruins are enough to tell its story. It is believed that the fortress was built by Raja Budhasen Dor who was also the founder of the city of ‘kol’, which is called Aligarh today. The site of the ruins was once the court of Raja Budhasen. These ruins extend up to the police ‘kotwali’ today. A large well, stables for horses and elephants are visible in its plan. The ruins of a tall minaret have a story of their own. It is said that the minaret was built by a Rajput ruler Mangalsen at the behest of his widowed daughter. She wanted to pass her days of widowhood watching the River Ganga. The top of the minaret was constructed to enable her to do the same. The well-planned layout of the ruins declares it to be one of the sturdiest and beautiful forts of yesteryears. Aligarh Fort The Aligarh Fort is located on the G.T. Road at a distance of just 6 km from the centre of Aligarh Township. It is a wonderful example of the mix of the Indian and French style of architecture. The fort was initially erected by the son of a governor of Ibrahim Lodhi in the 16th century. The original plan of the court was changed in later years as and when the successive rulers felt the need for reinforcements. This constant tinkering was being done to make it stronger than before. As the result of this addition and changes the Aligarh fort has come to the present time as an example of one of the strongest forts of India. It was successively used by the rulers of the area for various purposes. Late Madhavrao Sindhia used its large grounds to train his soldiers in the European tactics and methods of war. It was one of the centres of the revolt of 1857 by the Indian soldiers against the British. The grounds of the fort are under the care of the Aligarh Muslim University. The department of Botany manages the inner botanical garden. It is easy to see the fort after taking permission from the head of the department of Botany of the University. Dargah Baba Barchi Bahadur The Dargah is a 600 years old symbol of communal harmony. People of all faiths pay homage to the Baba especially at the time of the annual ‘urs’ or the death anniversary of this Sufi saint. The devotees come here and tie a thread and pray to the saint to fulfil their wish. Once the wish comes true the devotee comes again to untie the thread. It is believed that Baba Barchi Bahadur fulfils the wishes of everyone. The Dargah is situated near the Ghantghar circle of Aligarh close to the Ambedkar Park and the Aligarh Railway Station. Moulana Azad Library It is hailed as the largest library in terms of books and periodicals in the country. It also enjoys being the second largest in Asia. It has been stocked with rare books and manuscripts for a long time. This helps the post graduate and doctoral candidates in their study and research. The library is a part of the Aligarh Muslim University but scholars from anywhere can use the facility for the spread of the cause of research and education. The library has a central complex along with 80 different departmental libraries on various floors of the complex. The library is not only rich in stocking the books of educational nature but also of our culture and history. It is also one of the most beautiful places to visit when in Aligarh. Khereshwar Temple Dedicated to Lord Shiva, this temple has an interesting tale to tell. It is believed that the complex of the temple was completed in a day after the enshrining of the Linga. This place is considered as one of the holiest shrines of the area. The temple is replete with beautiful carvings, engraved murals on its ceiling and a beautiful architecture in The Hindu architectural tradition. The temple is situated in the Tajpur village at a distance of about 5 km from the centre of the town. It is connected to the NH 91 through the Khair by-pass road. The temple has several beautiful idols of Hindu deities. Nagliya Deer Reserve Aligarh boasts of a conservation reserve of its own kind situated at Nagliya. It is a small village on the G.T. Road at a distance of about 15 km from Aligarh. The widely spread park is dedicated to the conservation of the Black Deer and other native wildlife species. The People of Nagliya have a good level of awareness for conservation that coincides with the high rate of literacy in the village. Hakim Karam Hussain Gallery This is a unique museum in Aligarh. It exhibits illustrations of a historical nature depicting health issues, history of health and diseases with a cultural perspective of all such issues. The museum exhibits the practice of medicine through history. Those who visit the museum feel spell bound to watch the invaluable materials, sculptures and pictures of the Greek, Mesopotamian Egyptian and Arabian civilizations as they practised medicines in those eras. Shekha Lake Situated at a distance of 17 km from the centre of Aligarh, the lake is spread along an irrigation canal. The lake supplies water to the town. It is famous for its bio-diversity and a place for the migratory birds in winter. A variety of geese, ducks and other migratory birds can be seen in winter at the lake. People visit the place for its natural beauty as well as for bird watching in the season. Teerthdaham Mangalayatan This unique Jain establishment is situated at a distance of 5 km from the town on the Aligarh-Agra Highway. It is spread over an area of 16 acres and is dedicated to the Jain culture, religious beliefs and research. The temple complex is unique in its devotion to not only the Jain religion but the socio-religious issues of the period and how to research and tackle the same. There are four temples dedicated to traditional worship in the large complex. Chacha Nehru Gyan Pushp It is a small but exquisite gallery and museum situated at the Three Dots School Complex. The museum was founded in 1982. It was dedicated to the late PM, Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru, who was popularly known as ‘Chacha’ or uncle by the children of India. The numismatists, philatelists, botanists and geologists enjoy the museum because it has a good collection of the items of their interests like coins, stamps and specimen related to biology, history and geography.
